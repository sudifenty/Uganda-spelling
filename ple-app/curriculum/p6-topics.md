# P.6 Curriculum — verified topic structure

Extracted directly from `curriculum/P6-SET-ONE-2010.pdf` — **Primary Six Curriculum,
Set One**, published by the National Curriculum Development Centre, **© NCDC 2010**,
ISBN 978-9970-117-93-2, 346 pages. Page numbers below are **PDF page numbers** in that
file, so any claim can be checked.

**Where the file came from:** the NCDC website's own resource page no longer lists the P.6
document, so the copy used here was downloaded from the Georg Eckert Institute's education
media depot (`edumedia-depot.gei.de`). Its title page, publisher, ISBN and copyright were
checked against the NCDC original before use.

The P.6 Social Studies theme is **East Africa**. The curriculum states that, with the
rebirth of the East African Community, **Rwanda and Burundi are included as part of East
Africa** in this revised syllabus.

## Written up

All four subjects of P.6 Set One that a learner is examined or assessed on —
**Social Studies (5), Mathematics (12), Integrated Science (12) and English (6)** —
now have complete notes in `notes/`: **35 topics**.
