# P.5 Curriculum — verified topic structure

Extracted directly from `curriculum/P5-SET-ONE-2010.pdf` — **Primary 5 Curriculum, Set
One**, Ministry of Education and Sports, **National Curriculum Development Centre, August
2010**, **© NCDC 2010**, **ISBN 978-9970-117-91-8**, 226 pages. Page numbers below are
**PDF page numbers** in that file.

**Where the file came from:** NCDC's own resource page no longer lists the P.5 document, so
the copy used here was downloaded from the Ugandan teachers' resource site
`fresh-teacher.github.io`. Its **title page, publisher, date and ISBN were checked** against
the catalogue record of the printed book (Uganda Martyrs University library, ISBN
9789970117918) before it was used.

The P.5 Social Studies theme is **Living Together in Uganda**.

## Mathematics — 12 topics *(all written up in `notes/`)*

The syllabus introduction (PDF page 99) states that Mathematics "has been divided into six
themes and twelve topics" and then lists the twelve. The notes follow that official list and
order.

| # | Topic | Theme | PDF pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set Concepts | Sets | 100–101 |
| 2 | Whole Numbers | Numeracy | 101–102 |
| 3 | Operations on Whole Numbers | Numeracy | 102–104 |
| 4 | Patterns and Sequences | Numeracy | 105–106 |
| 5 | Fractions | Numeracy | 106–110 |
| 6 | Data Handling | Interpretation of Graphs and Data | 112–113 |
| 7 | Money | Measurements | 115 |
| 8 | Time | Interpretation of Graphs and Data | 113–114 |
| 9 | Length, Mass and Capacity | Measurements | 116–117 |
| 10 | Lines, Angles and Geometrical Figures | Geometry | 110–112 |
| 11 | Integers | Numeracy | 117–118 |
| 12 | Algebra | Algebra | 119 |

**Correction to an earlier note in this file.** An earlier draft of this table listed
"Fractions (Term I)" and "Fractions (Term II)" as two separate topics and left **Money**
out altogether. That was a mis-read of the scope-and-sequence pages. **Fractions is one
topic** taught in two terms — common fractions in Term I (pages 106–107) and decimals in
Term II (pages 108–110) — and **Money is a topic in its own right** in Term III (page 115).
Topic 5 of the notes covers both halves of Fractions, in two clearly-labelled parts.
