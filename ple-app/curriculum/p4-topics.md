**All four examined subjects for P.4 are now held.** Each document was downloaded in full and
its title page, publisher and copyright checked before use.

**A correction to an earlier record.** The copy of the Social Studies syllabus downloaded
earlier in this project was **truncated** — 163,831 bytes of a file that should be 810,429
bytes, about 20% — and could not be opened. It has been replaced with the complete file. The
earlier note that "no usable P.4 curriculum document" existed was wrong: three more
documents were found on the same NCDC upload path and at the UNESCO IBE mirror.

**A note on the Science document.** It is dated **April 2009**, a year earlier than the other
three, and it comes from the UNESCO IBE mirror rather than from NCDC's own site. Its title
page reads *"THE REPUBLIC OF UGANDA, Ministry of Education and Sports, Primary School
Curriculum, Primary 4 – Integrated Science Syllabus, National Curriculum Development Centre,
April 2009."* No later P.4 Science syllabus was found.

## Social Studies — 6 topics *(all written up in `notes/`)*

Theme for the whole year: **Living Together in Our District**.

| # | Topic | Periods | Term | PDF pages |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Location of Our District in Uganda | 16 | I | 17–20 |
| 2 | Physical Features in Our District | 20 | I | 20–23 |
| 3 | Vegetation in Our District | 25 | II | 23–26 |
| 4 | People in Our District | 15 | II | 26–31 |
| 5 | Our Leaders in the District | 25 | III | 31–35 |
| 6 | How to Meet People's Needs in Our District | 25 | III | 35–40 |

**Important:** every topic is about **the learner's own district**. The syllabus names no
district, so the notes teach the ideas and leave district facts to the learner. No facts
about any particular district have been invented.
