**P7 Set One** covers English, Integrated Science, Local Language Framework,
Mathematics, Religious Education (CRE and IRE) and Social Studies.

Written up so far from the P.7 document: **P.7 Social Studies (10 topics), P.7 Mathematics
(11 topics) and P.7 Integrated Science (8 topics)** — all 29 topics of the three examined
subjects (`notes/`, built into the app — see `docs/notes.md`).

From the **P.6** document: **Social Studies (5), Mathematics (12), Integrated Science (12)
and English (6)** — **P.6 is complete**, 35 topics.

From the **P.5** document: **Social Studies (12), Integrated Science (12), Mathematics (12)
and English (8)** — **P.5 is complete**, 44 topics.

The P.5 file is also recorded above as "read (SST, Science)"; it has now been **read for all
four examined subjects** — Social Studies, Integrated Science, Mathematics (pages 98–119) and
English (pages 11–34).

From the **P.4** documents: **Social Studies (6 topics)**. P.4 Mathematics (12), English (8)
and Integrated Science (12) are verified in `p4-topics.md` but **not yet written up**.

**A correction.** The P.4 Social Studies file downloaded earlier in this project was
**truncated** (163,831 bytes of 810,429 — about 20%) and could not be opened at all. It has
been replaced with the complete file, and three further P.4 documents were then found. The
earlier statement that no usable P.4 curriculum existed was **wrong**.
