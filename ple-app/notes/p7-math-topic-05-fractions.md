## 1. WORKING WITH FRACTIONS

### Definitions

**Proper fraction:** The top number is smaller than the bottom: 3/5.

**Improper fraction:** The top number is bigger: 7/4.

**Mixed number:** A whole number with a fraction: 1¾.

### The four operations

| Operation | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Adding / subtracting | Use the LCM of the denominators | 2/3 + 1/4 = 8/12 + 3/12 = **11/12** |
| Multiplying | Multiply tops, multiply bottoms, simplify | 2/3 × 9/10 = 18/30 = **3/5** |
| Dividing | Turn the second fraction upside down and multiply | 3/4 ÷ 2/3 = 3/4 × 3/2 = 9/8 = **1⅛** |
| Of | "of" means multiply | 3/5 of 40 = **24** |

### Worked example

*A tank was 3/4 full. 1/3 of the water was used. What fraction of the whole tank was used?*

1/3 of 3/4 = 1/3 × 3/4 = 3/12 = **1/4 of the tank**

## 2. DECIMALS

### Definitions

**Terminating (non-repeating) decimal:** A decimal that comes to an end, e.g. 0.375.

**Repeating (recurring) decimal:** A decimal in which one or more digits repeat for ever, e.g. 0.666… written as 0.6̇ or 0.6 with a bar.

### Changing fractions to decimals

Divide the top by the bottom.

| Fraction | Working | Decimal | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3/8 | 3 ÷ 8 | **0.375** | Terminating |
| 1/4 | 1 ÷ 4 | **0.25** | Terminating |
| 2/3 | 2 ÷ 3 | **0.666…** | Repeating |
| 1/6 | 1 ÷ 6 | **0.1666…** | Repeating |
| 1/7 | 1 ÷ 7 | **0.142857142857…** | Repeating |

**Useful rule:** if the denominator has only 2s and 5s as prime factors, the decimal terminates. Otherwise it repeats.

### Changing decimals to fractions

Write the digits over 10, 100, 1000 … then simplify.

- 0.35 = 35/100 = **7/20**
- 0.125 = 125/1000 = **1/8**
- 2.6 = 2 6/10 = **2⅗**

## 3. ROUNDING OFF DECIMALS

### The places after the point

| Place | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Tenths | Hundredths | Thousandths | Ten thousandths | **Hundred thousandths** |

### The rule

Look at the digit **just after** the place you are rounding to. If it is **5 or more**, add
1 to the place; if it is **4 or less**, leave the place as it is. Drop the rest.

### Worked examples

| Number | Round to | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 4.678542 | Hundred thousandths (5 places) | **4.67854** |
| 4.678546 | Hundred thousandths | **4.67855** |
| 7.846 | Nearest tenth | **7.8** |
| 0.06749 | Nearest thousandth | **0.067** |
| 12.3956 | Nearest hundredth | **12.40** |

## 4. PROPORTION

### Definitions

**Direct proportion:** As one quantity increases, the other increases in the same way.

**Indirect (inverse) proportion:** As one quantity increases, the other decreases.

### Worked example — direct proportion

*4 exercise books cost 12,000 shillings. How much will 7 books cost?*

1 book = 12,000 ÷ 4 = 3,000
7 books = 3,000 × 7 = **21,000 shillings**

### Worked example — inverse proportion

*6 workers dig a garden in 8 days. How long will 12 workers take, working at the same rate?*

Total work = 6 × 8 = 48 worker-days
12 workers → 48 ÷ 12 = **4 days**

### Sharing in a ratio

*Share 24,000 shillings between Ann and Ben in the ratio 3 : 5.*
Total shares = 3 + 5 = 8. One share = 24,000 ÷ 8 = 3,000.
Ann = 3 × 3,000 = **9,000**; Ben = 5 × 3,000 = **15,000**. (9,000 + 15,000 = 24,000 ✓)

## 5. PERCENTAGES

### Definition

**Percentage:** A fraction whose denominator is 100. The symbol is %.

| Fraction | Decimal | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| 1/2 | 0.5 | 50% |
| 1/4 | 0.25 | 25% |
| 3/4 | 0.75 | 75% |
| 1/5 | 0.2 | 20% |
| 1/8 | 0.125 | 12.5% |
| 2/3 | 0.666… | 66⅔% |

### Worked examples

1. *Find 25% of 4,800 shillings.*
   25/100 × 4,800 = **1,200 shillings**

2. *A pupil scored 36 out of 45. What percentage is that?*
   36/45 × 100 = **80%**

3. *A shirt costing 20,000 shillings is sold at a 15% discount. What is the new price?*
   Discount = 15/100 × 20,000 = 3,000. New price = 20,000 − 3,000 = **17,000 shillings**

4. *A trader bought a radio at 40,000 and sold it at 50,000. Find the percentage profit.*
   Profit = 10,000. Percentage profit = 10,000/40,000 × 100 = **25%**

5. *After a 20% increase, a worker earns 240,000 shillings. What was the old pay?*
   120% = 240,000, so 1% = 2,000, and 100% = **200,000 shillings**

# KEY DEFINITIONS

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Proper fraction | Numerator smaller than the denominator |
| Improper fraction | Numerator bigger than the denominator |
| Mixed number | A whole number together with a fraction |
| Terminating decimal | A decimal that comes to an end |
| Repeating decimal | A decimal where digits repeat for ever |
| Rounding off | Writing a number to a given place value |
| Direct proportion | Both quantities increase or decrease together |
| Inverse proportion | One increases while the other decreases |
| Percentage | A fraction out of 100 |
| Discount | Money taken off the marked price |

---

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- To divide by a fraction, **multiply by its reciprocal** (turn it upside down).
- "Of" means **multiply**.
- Denominators made only of 2s and 5s give **terminating** decimals.
- The 5th decimal place is the **hundred thousandths** place.
- Percentage = part ÷ whole × 100.
- Profit % is always worked out **on the buying price**.
- 1/3 = 0.333…, 2/3 = 0.666…, 1/6 = 0.1666… — all repeating.

---

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- 3/8 = 0.375; 0.35 = 7/20; 0.125 = 1/8.
- 4.678542 to the nearest hundred thousandth = 4.67854.
- 36 out of 45 = 80%.
- More workers → **fewer** days (inverse proportion).
- To share in a ratio, first find the **total number of shares**.

---

# COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Adding denominators: 1/2 + 1/3 is **not** 2/5.
- Rounding using the wrong digit — always look at the digit **immediately after** the place.
- Working percentage profit on the selling price instead of the buying price.
- Treating an inverse-proportion question as direct (more workers cannot take more time).
- Forgetting to simplify the final fraction.
