## 1. THE 12-HOUR AND 24-HOUR CLOCKS

### Definitions

**12-hour clock:** Counts from 1 to 12 twice a day, using **a.m.** (before midday) and **p.m.** (after midday).

**24-hour clock:** Counts from 00:00 (midnight) to 23:59, using **four figures** and no a.m. or p.m.

### The rules for changing

| From | To | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| a.m. time | 24-hour | Write it with four figures: 7:30 a.m. → **07:30** |
| p.m. time | 24-hour | **Add 12** to the hours: 4:15 p.m. → **16:15** |
| 24-hour before 12:00 | 12-hour | Write it with a.m.: 09:45 → **9:45 a.m.** |
| 24-hour from 13:00 | 12-hour | **Subtract 12** and write p.m.: 20:05 → **8:05 p.m.** |

### The two tricky points

- **Midnight** = **00:00** (or 24:00 at the end of a day) = 12:00 a.m.
- **Midday (noon)** = **12:00** = 12:00 p.m.

### Examples

| 12-hour | 24-hour |
|---|---|
| 6:00 a.m. | 06:00 |
| 11:45 a.m. | 11:45 |
| 12:30 p.m. | 12:30 |
| 1:20 p.m. | 13:20 |
| 7:55 p.m. | 19:55 |
| 12:15 a.m. | 00:15 |

## 2. UNITS OF TIME

| Unit | Equal to |
|---|---|
| 1 minute | 60 seconds |
| 1 hour | 60 minutes |
| 1 day | 24 hours |
| 1 week | 7 days |
| 1 fortnight | 14 days |
| 1 month | 4 weeks (about 30 days) |
| 1 year | 12 months = 365 days (a leap year has 366) |
| 1 decade | 10 years |
| 1 century | 100 years |

### Changing units

- **Hours to minutes:** multiply by 60. 2¼ hours = 2 × 60 + 15 = **135 minutes**
- **Minutes to hours:** divide by 60. 200 minutes = 200 ÷ 60 = **3 hours 20 minutes**

---

## 3. FINDING DURATION

**Duration = arrival time − departure time**

Remember: when you borrow in a time subtraction, **1 hour = 60 minutes**, not 100.

### Worked example 1

*A bus left at 09:40 and arrived at 13:25. How long did the journey take?*

```
    13 h 25 min      →   12 h 85 min
  -  9 h 40 min           9 h 40 min
    -----------          -----------
                          3 h 45 min
```
The journey took **3 hours 45 minutes**.

### Worked example 2

*A train left at 22:30 and arrived at 04:15 the next morning. Find the duration.*

- From 22:30 to midnight = 1 hour 30 minutes
- From midnight to 04:15 = 4 hours 15 minutes
- Total = **5 hours 45 minutes**

### Worked example 3

*A meeting started at 9:20 a.m. and lasted 2 hours 50 minutes. When did it end?*

9:20 + 2:50 = 11:70 = **12:10 p.m.** (70 minutes = 1 hour 10 minutes)

## 4. TIMETABLES

### Definition

**Timetable:** A table showing when activities, lessons or journeys start and end.

### Travel timetable

| Station | Arrival | Departure |
|---|---|---|
| Kampala | — | 07:30 |
| Mukono | 08:10 | 08:20 |
| Jinja | 09:35 | 09:50 |
| Iganga | 10:40 | — |

Questions this table can answer:

- *How long does the bus stop at Jinja?* 09:50 − 09:35 = **15 minutes**
- *How long is the whole journey?* 10:40 − 07:30 = **3 hours 10 minutes**
- *How long does it travel between Mukono and Jinja?* 09:35 − 08:20 = **1 hour 15 minutes**
- *Total resting time?* 10 minutes at Mukono + 15 at Jinja = **25 minutes**

### Class timetable

| Lesson | Time | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | 08:00 – 08:40 | 40 min |
| English | 08:40 – 09:20 | 40 min |
| Break | 09:20 – 09:40 | 20 min |
| Science | 09:40 – 10:20 | 40 min |

*How much teaching time is there from 08:00 to 10:20?* 2 hours 20 minutes in total, minus the 20-minute break = **2 hours** of lessons.

# KEY DEFINITIONS

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| a.m. | The hours from midnight to midday (ante meridiem) |
| p.m. | The hours from midday to midnight (post meridiem) |
| 24-hour clock | Time written in four figures from 00:00 to 23:59 |
| Duration | The length of time something takes |
| Departure | The time of leaving |
| Arrival | The time of reaching a place |
| Timetable | A table showing times of activities or journeys |
| Leap year | A year of 366 days, coming every four years |

---

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- p.m. → 24-hour: **add 12**. 24-hour after 12:59 → 12-hour: **subtract 12**.
- Midnight = **00:00**; midday = **12:00**.
- 1 hour = 60 minutes — when borrowing in time subtraction you borrow **60**, not 100.
- Duration = arrival − departure.
- For journeys that cross midnight, work up to midnight first, then add the rest.
- 24-hour clock times are always written with **four figures** and no a.m./p.m.

---

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- 4:15 p.m. = 16:15. 20:05 = 8:05 p.m. 12:15 a.m. = 00:15.
- From 09:40 to 13:25 is 3 hours 45 minutes.
- 2¼ hours = 135 minutes; 200 minutes = 3 hours 20 minutes.
- Stopping time at a station = departure − arrival.
- Total journey time includes the stops.

---

# COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Writing 4:15 p.m. as 4:15 in the 24-hour clock.
- Adding 12 to a.m. times.
- Borrowing 100 instead of 60 in time subtraction.
- Writing 12:30 p.m. as 24:30 — it is **12:30**.
- Forgetting the stops when asked for the **total** journey time.

# REVISION QUESTIONS

1. Change 5:45 p.m. to the 24-hour clock.
2. Change 11:20 a.m. to the 24-hour clock.
3. Change 18:35 to the 12-hour clock.
4. Change 00:40 to the 12-hour clock.
5. How many minutes are there in 3½ hours?
6. Change 250 minutes to hours and minutes.
7. A bus left at 08:15 and arrived at 12:05. Find the duration of the journey.
8. A lesson started at 10:40 and lasted 55 minutes. When did it end?
9. A journey lasted 6 hours 30 minutes and ended at 15:10. When did it start?
10. A night bus left at 21:45 and reached its destination at 05:30. How long was the journey?
11. Using the travel timetable above, how long does the bus stop at Mukono?
12. Using the same table, how long does the bus take between Kampala and Jinja, including the stop at Mukono?
13. How many hours are there in a week?
14. A film started at 7:50 p.m. and ended at 10:05 p.m. How long was it?
15. Write midnight and midday in the 24-hour clock.

---

# ANSWERS TO REVISION QUESTIONS

1. **17:45**
2. **11:20**
3. 18 − 12 = 6, so **6:35 p.m.**
4. **12:40 a.m.**
5. 3 × 60 + 30 = **210 minutes**
6. 250 ÷ 60 = 4 remainder 10 → **4 hours 10 minutes**
7. 12:05 − 08:15 = **3 hours 50 minutes**
8. 10:40 + 55 min = 11:35 → **11:35**
9. 15:10 − 6 h 30 min = **08:40**
10. 21:45 to midnight = 2 h 15 min; midnight to 05:30 = 5 h 30 min. Total = **7 hours 45 minutes**
11. 08:20 − 08:10 = **10 minutes**
12. 09:35 − 07:30 = **2 hours 5 minutes**
13. 7 × 24 = **168 hours**
14. 10:05 p.m. − 7:50 p.m. = **2 hours 15 minutes**
15. Midnight = **00:00**; midday = **12:00**

# P.7 QUICK REVISION

- p.m. → **+12** · 24-hour after noon → **−12** for p.m.
- Midnight **00:00** · Midday **12:00**
- Borrow **60**, never 100
- Duration = arrival − departure · crossing midnight: split at 00:00
- Journey time includes the stops
