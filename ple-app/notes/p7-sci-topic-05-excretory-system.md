# TOPIC 5: THE EXCRETORY SYSTEM

*Curriculum reference: NCDC Primary Seven Curriculum, Set One, Integrated Science Topic 5,

**What you already know:** You know that the body takes in food, water and air. In P.7 you
learn how the body **gets rid of the waste** it does not need, through the **skin, kidneys
and lungs**.

**By the end of this topic you should be able to:**

- name the excretory organs and the waste each one removes
- describe the structure, functions and care of the human skin
- describe the urinary system and the structure and work of the kidney
- explain how the lungs act as excretory organs
- name diseases and disorders of the skin, kidneys and lungs
- state good habits for the proper functioning of the system

## 1. EXCRETION AND THE EXCRETORY ORGANS

### Definitions

**Excretion:** The removal of waste products made inside the body.

**Egestion:** The removal of undigested food (faeces) from the body — this is **not** excretion.

### The excretory organs

| Organ | Waste removed |
|---|---|
| **Skin** | Sweat — water, salts and a little urea |
| **Kidneys** | Urine — water, urea and mineral salts |
| **Lungs** | Carbon dioxide and water vapour |
| **Liver** | Bile pigments; it also changes harmful substances into urea |

## 2. THE HUMAN SKIN

### Structure

The skin has two main layers:

| Layer | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **Epidermis** (outer) | Dead cells on the surface, growing cells below, and the colouring matter (melanin) |
| **Dermis** (inner) | Sweat glands, sebaceous (oil) glands, hair follicles, blood vessels, nerve endings and fat |

| Part | Work |
|---|---|
| **Sweat gland** | Makes sweat and passes it out through the sweat pore |
| **Sebaceous gland** | Makes oil (sebum) that keeps the skin and hair soft |
| **Hair follicle** | Holds the root of the hair |
| **Nerve endings** | Feel heat, cold, pain, pressure and touch |
| **Blood vessels** | Bring water and waste to the sweat glands and help control temperature |
| **Fatty layer** | Stores fat and keeps the body warm |

### Functions of the skin

- **Excretion** — removes sweat containing water, salts and urea.
- **Regulation of body temperature** — sweating cools the body; blood vessels narrow when it is cold.
- **Protection** — keeps out germs, dirt and harmful rays; protects the organs inside.
- **Sensation** — feels touch, pain, heat, cold and pressure.
- **Formation of vitamin D** when sunlight falls on it.
- **Storage** of fat and water.

### Diseases and disorders of the skin

| Disease | Cause | Signs |
|---|---|---|
| **Scabies** | Mites that burrow into the skin | Itching, small rashes, especially between fingers |
| **Ringworm** | A fungus | Round, itchy patches with a clear centre |
| **Athlete's foot** | A fungus | Cracking and itching between the toes |
| **Eczema** | Allergy or irritation | Dry, itchy, red patches |
| **Boils** | Bacteria infecting a hair follicle | Painful swelling with pus |
| **Jiggers** | A sand flea burrowing into the skin | Itching and swelling on feet and hands |
| **Sunburn** | Too much strong sunlight | Painful, peeling skin |

### Care of the skin

Bathe daily with clean water and soap · wash hands with soap · wear clean, dry clothes ·
cut nails short · treat wounds and cuts at once · eat a balanced diet and drink plenty of
water · do not share clothes, towels, combs or beddings · wear shoes · avoid harmful
skin-lightening creams · dry between the toes.

## 3. THE URINARY SYSTEM

### Parts and their work

| Part | Work |
|---|---|
| **Kidneys** (two) | Filter the blood and form urine |
| **Ureters** (two) | Carry urine from the kidneys to the bladder |
| **Urinary bladder** | Stores urine for a short time |
| **Urethra** | Passes urine out of the body |

### Structure of the kidney

| Part | Work |
|---|---|
| **Cortex** | The outer layer where filtering begins |
| **Medulla** | The middle layer with tubes that collect urine |
| **Pelvis** | The space that collects urine and passes it to the ureter |
| **Nephrons** | Tiny filtering units — each kidney has about a million |
| **Renal artery** | Brings blood with waste to the kidney |
| **Renal vein** | Takes away the clean blood |

### Functions of the kidney

- **Filters the blood** and removes urea, excess water and excess salts.
- **Forms urine.**
- **Controls the amount of water** in the body (osmoregulation).
- Keeps the blood at the right level of salts.

### Diseases and disorders of the kidney

| Condition | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Kidney (renal) stones** | Hard lumps of salts that block the tubes; very painful |
| **Nephritis** | Inflammation of the kidney, often after an infection |
| **Kidney failure** | The kidney stops filtering; treated by **dialysis** or a transplant |
| **Bilharzia (schistosomiasis)** | Worms from snails in dirty water; blood appears in urine |
| **Cystitis** | Infection of the bladder causing painful urination |
| **Diabetes insipidus** | Passing very large amounts of urine |

### Care of the kidneys

Drink plenty of clean water · avoid too much salt · avoid alcohol and unnecessary drugs ·
do not hold urine for too long · treat infections early · keep the private parts clean ·
avoid bathing in or drinking untreated water (bilharzia).

---

## 4. THE LUNGS AS EXCRETORY ORGANS

The lungs remove **carbon dioxide** and **water vapour** produced when the body breaks down
food to release energy. You can see the water vapour when you breathe onto a cold mirror.

### Diseases and disorders of the lungs

| Disease | Cause |
|---|---|
| **Tuberculosis (TB)** | Bacteria spread through coughing and sneezing |
| **Pneumonia** | Bacteria or viruses; the air sacs fill with fluid |
| **Bronchitis** | Infection or irritation of the air tubes |
| **Asthma** | Narrowing of the air tubes; often triggered by dust or cold |
| **Lung cancer** | Mainly caused by smoking |
| **Whooping cough** | Bacteria; a violent cough with a whoop |

### Care of the lungs

Do not smoke and avoid second-hand smoke · keep rooms well ventilated · avoid dust and
smoke from firewood; use energy-saving or outdoor stoves · immunise children against TB
(BCG) and whooping cough · cover the mouth when coughing · take exercise · treat coughs
early.

---

## 5. GOOD HABITS FOR THE WHOLE SYSTEM

- Drink at least **six to eight glasses of clean water** every day.
- Eat a **balanced diet** with plenty of fruits and vegetables, and not too much salt.
- Bathe daily and keep clothes and beddings clean.
- Take **regular exercise**.
- Avoid **alcohol, smoking and drug abuse**.
- Do not take medicine without advice from a health worker.
- Go for **medical check-ups** and treat infections early.
- Use a clean latrine and wash hands with soap afterwards.

# KEY DEFINITIONS

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Excretion | The removal of waste products made in the body |
| Egestion | The removal of undigested food from the body |
| Urea | A waste product formed in the liver from extra protein |
| Nephron | The tiny filtering unit of the kidney |
| Ureter | The tube from the kidney to the bladder |
| Urethra | The tube that carries urine out of the body |
| Dialysis | Cleaning the blood by machine when the kidneys fail |
| Epidermis | The outer layer of the skin |
| Dermis | The inner layer of the skin |
| Sebum | The oil made by the sebaceous glands |

---

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- The four excretory organs are the **skin, kidneys, lungs and liver**.
- The skin excretes **sweat**; the kidneys excrete **urine**; the lungs excrete **carbon dioxide and water vapour**.
- The skin has two layers: **epidermis** (outer) and **dermis** (inner).
- Sweating **cools** the body.
- Each kidney contains about a million **nephrons**.
- Urine flows: kidney → **ureter** → bladder → **urethra**.
- Kidney failure is treated by **dialysis** or a transplant.
- Bilharzia is caught from **dirty water containing snails**.
- Egestion is **not** excretion.

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- Largest excretory organ — the skin.
- Waste removed by the lungs — carbon dioxide and water vapour.
- Tube from the kidney to the bladder — ureter.
- Filtering unit of the kidney — nephron.
- Skin disease caused by a fungus — ringworm (or athlete's foot).
- Skin disease caused by mites — scabies.
- Machine used when the kidneys fail — dialysis machine.
- Vitamin formed by the skin in sunlight — vitamin D.

# ANSWERS TO REVISION QUESTIONS

1. Excretion is the removal of waste products made inside the body. Egestion is the removal of **undigested food** (faeces), which was never part of the body's chemical processes.
2. Skin — sweat; kidneys — urine; lungs — carbon dioxide and water vapour; liver — bile pigments (and it makes urea).
3. The **epidermis** and the **dermis**.
4. Any four of: sweat glands, sebaceous glands, hair follicles, blood vessels, nerve endings, fatty layer.
5. Any four of: excretion of sweat, control of body temperature, protection against germs and injury, sensation, formation of vitamin D, storage of fat and water.
6. Any three, for example: scabies — mites; ringworm — a fungus; boils — bacteria; athlete's foot — a fungus; eczema — allergy or irritation.
7. Any four of: bathe daily with soap and clean water; wear clean clothes; cut nails short; treat wounds early; eat a balanced diet and drink water; avoid sharing towels and combs; wear shoes.
8. Kidneys → ureters → urinary bladder → urethra.
9. Any three of: cortex, medulla, pelvis, nephrons, renal artery, renal vein.
10. Any three of: filtering the blood, removing urea and excess salts, forming urine, controlling the amount of water in the body.
11. Any three of: kidney stones, nephritis, kidney failure, bilharzia, cystitis.
12. By **dialysis** — cleaning the blood using a machine — or by a **kidney transplant**.
13. **Carbon dioxide** and **water vapour**.
14. Any three of: tuberculosis, pneumonia, bronchitis, asthma, lung cancer, whooping cough. Prevention: do not smoke, keep rooms well ventilated, avoid smoke and dust, and immunise children.
15. Any four of: drink plenty of clean water; eat a balanced diet with little salt; bathe daily; exercise regularly; avoid alcohol, smoking and drug abuse; do not hold urine too long; go for medical check-ups.
