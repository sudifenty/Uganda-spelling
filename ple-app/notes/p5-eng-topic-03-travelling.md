# PRIMARY 5 ENGLISH

# TOPIC 3: TRAVELLING

*Curriculum reference: NCDC Primary Five Curriculum, Set One, English Topic 3, 15 periods
(`curriculum/P5-SET-ONE-2010.pdf`, pages 20–21).*

**What you already know:** In P.4 you learnt to **give directions**. In P.5 you build on
that with the language of **travelling** — fares, tickets, departure and destination — and
you use **adverbs**, the **past continuous tense** and the **past simple tense**.

**By the end of this topic you should be able to:**

- name the different means of travel and describe them
- use the vocabulary of travelling correctly
- use adverbs of manner, place, time and frequency
- use the past continuous with *while*
- use the past simple tense
- read a travel timetable and write a composition about a journey

## 2. MEANS OF TRAVEL

| By land | By water | By air |
|---|---|---|
| bus · coach · taxi · car · lorry · bicycle · motorcycle · train | boat · canoe · ferry · ship | aeroplane · helicopter |

**We say:** travel **by** bus, **by** train, **by** boat, **by** air — but **on foot**.

| Correct | Wrong |
|---|---|
| I travelled **by bus**. | I travelled with bus. |
| We went **on foot**. | We went by foot. |

## 3. GRAMMAR: ADVERBS

An **adverb** tells us more about a verb — **how**, **where**, **when** or **how often**
something happens.

| Kind of adverb | Question it answers | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Manner** | How? | quickly, slowly, carefully, fast, well |
| **Place** | Where? | here, there, outside, further |
| **Time** | When? | yesterday, today, early, late, soon |
| **Frequency** | How often? | always, often, sometimes, never, rarely |

**Making adverbs of manner:** add **-ly** to the adjective.

```
quick → quickly      careful → carefully      slow → slowly
happy → happily      easy → easily
```

Some do not change: **fast → fast**, **hard → hard**, **early → early**.
**Good** becomes **well**.

**Where the adverb of frequency goes:** before the main verb.

```
I often travel by bus to town.
She always arrives early.
They never travel at night.
```

---

## 4. GRAMMAR: THE PAST CONTINUOUS WITH "WHILE"

The **past continuous** shows an action that was **going on** in the past. It is formed with:

```
was / were  +  verb + ing
```

| Sentence |
|---|
| The taxi **was moving**. |
| The passengers **were waiting** at the stage. |

**While** joins a longer action to a shorter one. The longer action takes the **past
continuous**; the shorter one takes the **past simple**.

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| … **while** … | The conductor collected the fare **while** the taxi was moving. |
| **While** … , … | **While** we were travelling, one of the children started crying. |
| … **while** … | It began to rain **while** we were waiting for the bus. |

**Was or were?** *was* with I, he, she, it · *were* with you, we, they.

---

## 5. GRAMMAR: THE PAST SIMPLE

The past simple shows an action **finished** in the past.

| Present | Past |
|---|---|
| travel | **travelled** |
| arrive | **arrived** |
| reach | **reached** |
| leave | **left** |
| go | **went** |
| pay | **paid** |
| take | **took** |
| begin | **began** |
| ride | **rode** |
| buy | **bought** |

```
We left home at six o'clock and reached Mbarara at noon.
She paid the fare and took her seat.
```

## 6. READING A TRAVEL TIMETABLE

| Bus | Leaves Kampala | Arrives Gulu | Fare |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning coach | 7:00 a.m. | 1:00 p.m. | 40,000 shillings |
| Midday coach | 12:00 noon | 6:00 p.m. | 40,000 shillings |
| Night coach | 9:00 p.m. | 3:00 a.m. | 35,000 shillings |

**Questions:** Which coach is cheapest? How long does the journey take? If you must be in
Gulu by 2:00 p.m., which coach do you take?

**Answers:** the night coach · six hours · the morning coach.

## 7. SAFETY AND GOOD MANNERS WHEN TRAVELLING

- Keep your **ticket** and your **change** safely.
- Do not hang out of the window of a moving vehicle.
- Ask the driver to **reduce speed** if he is driving dangerously.
- Give your seat to an elderly person or to a mother with a baby.
- Keep **time** — a coach will not wait.
- Do not accept food or drink from a stranger on a journey.

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- We travel **by** bus, **by** train, **by** air — but **on foot**.
- Most adverbs of manner are made by adding **-ly**.
- *Good* becomes **well**; *fast* and *hard* do not change.
- Adverbs of frequency go **before the main verb**: *I often travel by bus.*
- Past continuous = **was / were + verb-ing**.
- With **while**, the longer action takes the past continuous.
- *Leave* becomes **left** and *pay* becomes **paid** in the past.

---

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- The conductor collected the fare **while** the taxi **was moving**.
- **While** we **were travelling**, one of the children **started** crying.
- I **often** travel by bus to town.
- The money paid for a journey is the **fare**.
- The place you are going to is your **destination**.
- The past simple of *leave* is **left**.

---

# COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Writing "by foot" instead of **on foot**.
- Writing "He drives good" instead of **He drives well**.
- Using the past simple in both halves of a *while* sentence.
- Mixing up *was* and *were*.
- Confusing **fare** (money) with **fair** (just, or a market).
- Confusing **departure** (leaving) with **destination** (arriving).
