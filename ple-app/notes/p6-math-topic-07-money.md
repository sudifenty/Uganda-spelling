## 1. CURRENCIES OF DIFFERENT COUNTRIES

### Definition

**Currency:** The money used in a particular country.

| Country | Currency |
|---|---|
| Uganda | Uganda shilling (UGX) |
| Kenya | Kenya shilling (KES) |
| Tanzania | Tanzanian shilling (TZS) |
| Rwanda | Rwandan franc (RWF) |
| Burundi | Burundian franc (BIF) |
| South Sudan | South Sudanese pound (SSP) |
| United States of America | US dollar (USD, $) |
| United Kingdom | Pound sterling (GBP, £) |
| Countries of the Euro area | Euro (EUR, €) |
| South Africa | Rand (ZAR) |
| Japan | Yen (JPY) |
| India | Rupee (INR) |
| Nigeria | Naira (NGN) |
| Ghana | Cedi (GHS) |
| Egypt | Egyptian pound (EGP) |
| Saudi Arabia | Riyal (SAR) |

**Note:** Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania all use a "shilling", but they are **different
currencies with different values** — never treat them as the same.

## 2. EXCHANGE RATES

### Definitions

**Exchange rate:** The value of one country's money in terms of another country's money.

**Forex bureau / bank:** A place where money is exchanged.

### Why people change money

Travelling abroad · importing and exporting goods · paying school fees abroad · sending or
receiving money from relatives working outside the country · tourism · paying for services
in another currency.

### The two rules

- **Foreign currency → shillings: MULTIPLY** by the rate.
- **Shillings → foreign currency: DIVIDE** by the rate.

### Worked examples

*Take: 1 US dollar = 3,700 shillings, 1 Kenya shilling = 28 Uganda shillings, and 1 euro = 4,000 shillings.*

1. *Change 50 US dollars to Uganda shillings.*
   50 × 3,700 = **185,000 shillings**

2. *Change 740,000 Uganda shillings to US dollars.*
   740,000 ÷ 3,700 = **200 dollars**

3. *Change 5,000 Kenya shillings to Uganda shillings.*
   5,000 × 28 = **140,000 Uganda shillings**

4. *A tourist changed 300 euros. How many Uganda shillings did she get?*
   300 × 4,000 = **1,200,000 shillings**

5. *Changing from one foreign currency to another:*
   *How many US dollars can one get for 200 euros?*
   200 euros = 200 × 4,000 = 800,000 shillings → 800,000 ÷ 3,700 = **about 216 dollars**

**Buying and selling rates:** a bureau **buys** foreign money at a lower rate and **sells**
it at a higher rate; the difference is its profit.

## 3. EVERYDAY MONEY PROBLEMS

### Buying and selling

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cost (buying) price** | What the trader paid |
| **Selling price** | What the buyer paid |
| **Profit** | Selling price − cost price |
| **Loss** | Cost price − selling price |
| **Discount** | Money taken off the marked price |
| **Change** | Money given back when you pay more than the price |

**Percentage profit or loss is always worked out on the cost price:**
**% profit = profit ÷ cost price × 100**

### Worked examples

1. *A shopkeeper bought a bag at 45,000 and sold it at 54,000. Find the profit and the percentage profit.*
   Profit = 9,000. % profit = 9,000/45,000 × 100 = **20%**

2. *A shirt marked 30,000 is sold at a 15% discount. Find the amount paid.*
   Discount = 15/100 × 30,000 = 4,500 → **25,500 shillings**

3. *Mother bought 3 kg of sugar at 4,800 a kilogramme and 2 loaves at 5,500 each. She paid with a 50,000-shilling note. How much change did she get?*
   Sugar = 14,400; bread = 11,000; total = 25,400.
   Change = 50,000 − 25,400 = **24,600 shillings**

4. *Simple interest:* **I = P × R × T ÷ 100**
   *Find the interest on 500,000 shillings for 2 years at 12% per year.*
   I = 500,000 × 12 × 2 ÷ 100 = **120,000 shillings**; amount = **620,000 shillings**

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- **Foreign money → shillings: multiply.** **Shillings → foreign money: divide.**
- Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania shillings are **different currencies**.
- Kenya uses the shilling; **Rwanda and Burundi use the franc**.
- The USA uses the **dollar**; the UK uses the **pound sterling**; South Africa uses the **rand**.
- Profit and loss percentages are worked out on the **cost price**.
- **I = P × R × T ÷ 100**; Amount = Principal + Interest.
- Exchange rates **change from day to day**, so always use the rate given in the question.

---

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- Currency of Rwanda → the **franc**. Of South Africa → the **rand**. Of the UK → the **pound sterling**.
- 50 dollars at 3,700 → 185,000 shillings.
- 740,000 shillings at 3,700 → 200 dollars.
- Bought at 45,000, sold at 54,000 → 20% profit.
- 15% discount on 30,000 → pay 25,500.
- Interest on 500,000 at 12% for 2 years → 120,000.

---

# COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Dividing when you should multiply (foreign → shillings is **multiply**).
- Assuming the Kenya shilling equals the Uganda shilling.
- Working profit percentage on the selling price.
- Forgetting to subtract the discount before finding the change.
- Leaving out the currency name in the answer.

# REVISION QUESTIONS

*Use these rates unless told otherwise: 1 US dollar = 3,700 shillings; 1 euro = 4,000 shillings; 1 Kenya shilling = 28 Uganda shillings; 1 pound sterling = 4,600 shillings.*

1. What is a currency? Give the currency used in Rwanda, Ghana and Japan.
2. Name the currency of each: Kenya, Burundi, South Africa, the United Kingdom.
3. What is an exchange rate?
4. Give any three reasons why people change money from one currency to another.
5. Change 80 US dollars to Uganda shillings.
6. Change 1,110,000 Uganda shillings to US dollars.
7. Change 250 euros to Uganda shillings.
8. Change 920,000 Uganda shillings to pounds sterling.
9. Change 7,500 Kenya shillings to Uganda shillings.
10. A tourist changed 400 dollars and spent 1,200,000 shillings. How much did she remain with?
11. A trader bought a radio at 60,000 and sold it at 78,000. Find the profit and the percentage profit.
12. A goat bought at 150,000 was sold at 120,000. Find the loss and the percentage loss.
13. A jacket marked 48,000 is sold at a 25% discount. How much does a buyer pay?
14. Ann bought 4 kg of rice at 4,500 a kilogramme and 3 tins of milk at 3,200 each. She paid with a 50,000-shilling note. Find her change.
15. Find the simple interest on 800,000 shillings for 3 years at 9% per year, and state the amount.

# ANSWERS TO REVISION QUESTIONS

1. A currency is the money used in a particular country. Rwanda — the **franc**; Ghana — the **cedi**; Japan — the **yen**.
2. Kenya — Kenya **shilling**; Burundi — Burundian **franc**; South Africa — **rand**; the United Kingdom — **pound sterling**.
3. An exchange rate is the value of one country's money in terms of another country's money.
4. Any three of: travelling abroad; importing or exporting goods; paying fees abroad; sending or receiving money from relatives working outside; tourism; paying for services in another currency.
5. 80 × 3,700 = **296,000 shillings**
6. 1,110,000 ÷ 3,700 = **300 dollars**
7. 250 × 4,000 = **1,000,000 shillings**
8. 920,000 ÷ 4,600 = **200 pounds**
9. 7,500 × 28 = **210,000 Uganda shillings**
10. 400 × 3,700 = 1,480,000. 1,480,000 − 1,200,000 = **280,000 shillings**
11. Profit = 18,000; % profit = 18,000/60,000 × 100 = **30%**
12. Loss = 30,000; % loss = 30,000/150,000 × 100 = **20%**
13. Discount = 25/100 × 48,000 = 12,000 → pay **36,000 shillings**
14. Rice = 18,000; milk = 9,600; total = 27,600. Change = 50,000 − 27,600 = **22,400 shillings**
15. I = 800,000 × 9 × 3 ÷ 100 = **216,000 shillings**; amount = **1,016,000 shillings**
