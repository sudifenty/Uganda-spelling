# PRIMARY 6 MATHEMATICS

**Theme: Measurements**

# TOPIC 8: DISTANCE, TIME AND SPEED

*Curriculum reference: NCDC Primary Six Curriculum, Set One, Mathematics Theme 4 Topic 2,
6 periods (`curriculum/P6-SET-ONE-2010.pdf`, pages 185–187).*

**What you already know:** You can tell time and measure length. In P.6 you join the three
together — **distance, time and speed** — and you read and draw **distance–time graphs**.

**By the end of this topic you should be able to:**

- state the relationship between distance, time and speed
- apply the formulae to find any one of the three
- change units of speed and time
- read distance, speed and time from a line graph
- plot simple distance–time graphs

---

## 1. THE THREE FORMULAE

### Definitions

**Distance:** How far one place is from another. **Time:** How long a journey takes. **Speed:** How fast something is moving — the distance covered in one unit of time.

| To find | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Speed** | Distance ÷ Time |
| **Distance** | Speed × Time |
| **Time** | Distance ÷ Speed |

**The triangle trick:** write **D** on top, with **S** and **T** below it. Cover the one
you want: cover D and you see S × T; cover S and you see D over T; cover T and you see D
over S.

### Units

Speed is written as **km/hr** (kilometres per hour) or **m/s** (metres per second).
Always make sure the units in one question match: if time is in **minutes**, change it to
hours, or work in metres and seconds.

---

## 2. WORKED EXAMPLES

1. *A car covered 240 km in 4 hours. Find its speed.*
   Speed = 240 ÷ 4 = **60 km/hr**

2. *A cyclist rides at 15 km/hr for 3 hours. How far does he go?*
   Distance = 15 × 3 = **45 km**

3. *How long does a bus take to cover 180 km at 60 km/hr?*
   Time = 180 ÷ 60 = **3 hours**

4. *A taxi covered 90 km in 1½ hours. Find its speed.*
   Speed = 90 ÷ 1.5 = **60 km/hr**

5. *A motorist travelled for 45 minutes at 80 km/hr. Find the distance.*
   45 minutes = 45/60 = 0.75 hours. Distance = 80 × 0.75 = **60 km**

6. *A car left Kampala at 8:00 a.m. and reached Masaka, 130 km away, at 10:00 a.m. Find its average speed.*
   Time = 2 hours → speed = 130 ÷ 2 = **65 km/hr**

7. *Changing units:* 72 km/hr in metres per second
   72 km = 72,000 m; 1 hour = 3,600 s → 72,000 ÷ 3,600 = **20 m/s**
   *(Short cut: multiply km/hr by 5 and divide by 18.)*

8. *A journey of 300 km was covered in 4 hours, including a rest of 30 minutes. Find the speed while moving.*
   Moving time = 3½ hours → 300 ÷ 3.5 = **about 85.7 km/hr**

---

## 3. AVERAGE SPEED

**Average speed = total distance ÷ total time** (including any stops, unless the question
says otherwise).

*A driver covered 120 km in 2 hours, rested for 1 hour, then covered 180 km in 3 hours.
Find the average speed for the whole journey.*

- Total distance = 120 + 180 = 300 km
- Total time = 2 + 1 + 3 = 6 hours
- Average speed = 300 ÷ 6 = **50 km/hr**

## 4. DISTANCE–TIME GRAPHS

### Reading the graph

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| A sloping line going up | The traveller is moving away from the starting point |
| A **steeper** line | Moving **faster** |
| A **flat (horizontal)** line | **Resting** — the distance does not change |
| A gentler slope | Moving more slowly |

**Speed at any stage = distance covered in that stage ÷ time taken for that stage.**

### Worked example

*A traveller's journey:*

| Stage | Time | Distance covered |
|---|---|---|
| First stage | 8:00 – 10:00 | 0 km to 100 km |
| Rest | 10:00 – 10:30 | stays at 100 km |
| Second stage | 10:30 – 12:30 | 100 km to 220 km |

- Speed in the first stage = 100 ÷ 2 = **50 km/hr**
- Length of the rest = **30 minutes**
- Speed in the second stage = 120 ÷ 2 = **60 km/hr**
- Total distance = **220 km**; total time = 4½ hours
- Average speed for the whole journey = 220 ÷ 4.5 = **about 48.9 km/hr**

### Plotting a graph

Put **time on the horizontal axis** and **distance on the vertical axis** · choose a clear
scale and write it down · plot each point · join the points with straight lines · give the
graph a title.

*(The syllabus says to keep to simple journeys — avoid return journeys and bodies moving in
opposite directions.)*

# KEY DEFINITIONS

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Distance | How far one place is from another |
| Time | How long something takes |
| Speed | The distance covered in one unit of time |
| Average speed | Total distance divided by total time |
| km/hr | Kilometres per hour |
| m/s | Metres per second |
| Distance–time graph | A graph showing distance travelled against time |
| Rest | A stage of a journey with no movement |

---

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- **Speed = D ÷ T · Distance = S × T · Time = D ÷ S.**
- Match the units: minutes must be changed to **hours** (÷ 60).
- To change **km/hr to m/s**, multiply by **5/18**; to change m/s to km/hr, multiply by **18/5**.
- **Average speed = total distance ÷ total time**, including rests.
- On a distance–time graph, a **flat line means resting** and a **steeper line means faster**.
- 1 km = 1,000 m; 1 hour = 60 minutes = 3,600 seconds.

---

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- 240 km in 4 hours → 60 km/hr.
- 15 km/hr for 3 hours → 45 km.
- 180 km at 60 km/hr → 3 hours.
- 45 minutes = ¾ hour.
- 72 km/hr = 20 m/s.
- A flat line on a travel graph means the traveller is **resting**.

---

# COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Leaving time in minutes when the speed is in km/hr.
- Dividing distance by speed when you were asked for distance.
- Forgetting the rest time when finding the **average** speed for the whole journey.
- Writing the answer without the unit (km, hours, km/hr).
- Reading a resting line as movement.

# REVISION QUESTIONS

1. State the three formulae connecting distance, time and speed.
2. A bus covered 300 km in 5 hours. Find its speed.
3. A cyclist travels at 18 km/hr for 4 hours. Find the distance covered.
4. How long does a car take to cover 210 km at 70 km/hr?
5. A taxi covered 105 km in 1½ hours. Find its speed.
6. A motorist travelled for 30 minutes at 90 km/hr. Find the distance.
7. Change 54 km/hr to metres per second.
8. Change 25 m/s to kilometres per hour.
9. A train left at 7:00 a.m. and arrived at 11:00 a.m., having covered 320 km. Find its average speed.
10. A driver covered 150 km in 3 hours, rested for 1 hour and then covered 200 km in 4 hours. Find the average speed for the whole journey.
11. A journey of 240 km took 4 hours including a 30-minute stop. Find the speed while the vehicle was moving.
12. On a distance–time graph, what does a flat line mean?
13. On a distance–time graph, what does a steeper line mean?
14. A traveller covered 80 km between 9:00 a.m. and 10:00 a.m., rested for half an hour, then covered 60 km in the next hour. Find the total distance, the total time and the average speed.
15. A lorry travels 45 km in 30 minutes. What is its speed in km/hr?

# ANSWERS TO REVISION QUESTIONS

1. Speed = Distance ÷ Time; Distance = Speed × Time; Time = Distance ÷ Speed.
2. 300 ÷ 5 = **60 km/hr**
3. 18 × 4 = **72 km**
4. 210 ÷ 70 = **3 hours**
5. 105 ÷ 1.5 = **70 km/hr**
6. 30 minutes = ½ hour → 90 × 0.5 = **45 km**
7. 54 × 5 ÷ 18 = **15 m/s**
8. 25 × 18 ÷ 5 = **90 km/hr**
9. Time = 4 hours → 320 ÷ 4 = **80 km/hr**
10. Total distance = 350 km; total time = 8 hours → 350 ÷ 8 = **43.75 km/hr**
11. Moving time = 3½ hours → 240 ÷ 3.5 = **about 68.6 km/hr**
12. It means the traveller is **resting** — the distance is not changing.
13. It means the traveller is moving **faster**.
14. Total distance = 80 + 60 = **140 km**; total time = 1 + ½ + 1 = **2½ hours**; average speed = 140 ÷ 2.5 = **56 km/hr**
15. 30 minutes = ½ hour → 45 ÷ 0.5 = **90 km/hr**

---

# P.6 QUICK REVISION

- **S = D ÷ T · D = S × T · T = D ÷ S**
- Minutes → hours: **÷ 60**
- km/hr → m/s: **× 5 ÷ 18** · m/s → km/hr: **× 18 ÷ 5**
- **Average speed = total distance ÷ total time** (rests included)
- Graph: flat = **rest** · steeper = **faster** · time on the horizontal axis
