## SUB-TOPIC 6A: IMPORTANCE OF ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION

### Vocabulary from the syllabus

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| pollution | Making air, water or land dirty and harmful |
| garbage | Rubbish thrown away, especially from a home |
| refuse *(noun: REF-use)* | Waste material; rubbish |
| erosion | The wearing away and carrying off of soil |
| drought | A long period without rain |
| floods | Large amounts of water covering land that is usually dry |
| storms | Very bad weather with strong wind and rain |
| source | The place where something begins |
| raw materials | Natural materials used to make goods |
| habitat | The natural home of a plant or an animal |
| flora | All the plants of an area |
| fauna | All the animals of an area |
| manure | Rotted plant or animal matter used to make soil fertile |
| drainage | The way water is carried away from land |
| conserve | To protect and use carefully |
| conservation | The protection of nature and its resources |
| overgrazing | Letting animals eat the grass until the ground is bare |

**Note the pronunciation trap:** *refuse* as a **noun** means rubbish (REF-use); *refuse*
as a **verb** means to say no (re-FUSE).

### Activities that damage the environment

Cutting down trees (**deforestation**) · bush burning · overgrazing · poor farming on steep
slopes · swamp draining · dumping garbage anywhere · smoke from factories and vehicles ·
mining and quarrying · poaching · pouring waste into rivers and lakes.

### Why the environment must be protected

- To keep **reliable rainfall** and a good climate.
- To protect the **soil** from erosion and keep it fertile.
- To keep **clean air and clean water**.
- To protect the **habitat** of animals and plants — the flora and fauna.
- To keep supplying **raw materials, food, firewood and medicine**.
- To prevent **floods, drought, landslides and disease**.
- To keep the country **beautiful** and attract **tourists**.
- To leave enough for the people of **tomorrow**.

### Language structures

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| **We must … to …** | *We **must** protect our environment **to** have rain.* |
| **… so …** | *Our well was polluted, **so** we did not have clean water.* |
| **Since …** (at the start) | ***Since** the forests have been cut, we shall have no rain.* |
| **… since …** (inside) | *We shall have no rain **since** the forests have been cut.* |

*Since* here gives a **reason** (like *because*). It can also show **time**: *I have lived
here **since** 2020.*

### Grammar focus: abstract nouns

**Abstract noun:** The name of something you cannot see or touch — an idea, a quality or a state.

| From an adjective | Abstract noun | From a verb | Abstract noun |
|---|---|---|---|
| beautiful | **beauty** | protect | **protection** |
| strong | strength | pollute | **pollution** |
| poor | poverty | conserve | **conservation** |
| wise | wisdom | destroy | destruction |
| kind | kindness | grow | growth |
| free | freedom | erode | erosion |
| dark | darkness | decide | decision |

## SUB-TOPIC 6B: WAYS OF PROTECTING THE ENVIRONMENT

### Vocabulary from the syllabus

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| terraces | Steps cut across a slope to slow running water |
| crop rotation | Changing the crop grown on a plot each season |
| mulching | Covering the soil with dry grass or leaves |
| dumping | Throwing away waste, often carelessly |
| pollute | To make air, water or land dirty |
| erode | To wear away soil |
| reserve | An area kept and protected, such as a forest reserve |
| degrade | To spoil or lower the quality of land |
| dustbin | A container for rubbish |
| dispose | To get rid of something properly |
| incinerator | A machine or pit for burning waste safely |
| waste basket | A small basket for waste paper |

### Ways of protecting the environment

| Way | What is done |
|---|---|
| **Afforestation and reafforestation** | Planting trees where there were none, and replanting cut areas |
| **Terracing and contour ploughing** | Cutting steps and ploughing across the slope to stop erosion |
| **Mulching and cover crops** | Covering the soil so rain does not wash it away |
| **Crop rotation and manure** | Keeping the soil fertile without exhausting it |
| **Controlled grazing** | Keeping the right number of animals on the land |
| **Proper waste disposal** | Dustbins, rubbish pits, incinerators, recycling, composting |
| **Protecting wetlands and river banks** | Not farming or building in them |
| **Using alternative energy** | Solar, biogas and energy-saving stoves instead of firewood |
| **Making and obeying laws** | Forest and wildlife reserves, fines for polluters |
| **Educating people** | Radio messages, posters, debates, environment clubs |

### Language structures

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| **If we don't …** (If 1) | ***If we don't** build terraces, the soil **will** erode.* |
| **If …** (If 3) | ***If** we **had not cut** down all the trees, we **would have had** more rain.* |
| **… as well as …** | *Bush burning **as well as** overgrazing cause environmental degradation.* |
| **… had to …** | *We **had to** build terraces in order to protect our soil.* |

**Careful with *as well as*:** the verb agrees with the **first** subject —
*The teacher, as well as the pupils, **was** planting trees.*

### Grammar focus: If 1 and If 3 again

| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **If 1** — real and likely | If + present simple, **will** + verb | *If we plant trees, we **will** get more rain.* |
| **If 3** — the past that did not happen | If + **had** + past participle, **would have** + past participle | *If we **had planted** trees, we **would have had** more rain.* |

### Writing an environmental article or message

Give it a clear **title** · begin with the **problem** · give **reasons and examples** ·
suggest **what should be done** · end with a **call to action**. Keep sentences short and
use strong verbs.

> **PLANT A TREE, SAVE OUR VILLAGE**
> *Our village used to be green. Today the hills are bare, the rain is late and the soil
> is washed into the river. If we do not act now, our gardens will not feed us. Let every
> family plant five trees this season.*

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- **Flora** = plants; **fauna** = animals.
- *Refuse* (noun) = rubbish; *refuse* (verb) = to say no.
- *Since* can mean **because** or show **time**.
- With **as well as**, the verb agrees with the **first** subject.
- **If 1** = *If + present simple, will + verb.* **If 3** = *If + had + p.p., would have + p.p.*
- Abstract nouns: beauty, strength, poverty, freedom, pollution, conservation, protection.
- Ways of protecting: afforestation · terracing · mulching · crop rotation · proper waste disposal · laws · education.

---

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- Give one word for: all the plants of an area → **flora**.
- Give one word for: a machine for burning waste safely → **incinerator**.
- Form the abstract noun from *beautiful* → **beauty**; from *pollute* → **pollution**.
- *If we don't build terraces, the soil ____ (erode).* → **will erode**
- *If we had not cut the trees, we ____ (have) more rain.* → **would have had**
- *The teacher as well as the pupils ____ (be) planting trees.* → **was**
- *____ the forests have been cut, we shall have no rain.* → **Since**

---

# COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Writing *"If we don't plant trees, the soil erodes"* — use **will erode** in If 1.
- Mixing If 1 and If 3 in one sentence.
- Making the verb agree with the second subject after **as well as**.
- Confusing **flora** and **fauna**.
- Spelling: **environment** (not "enviroment"), **conservation**, **erosion**, **incinerator**.

# REVISION QUESTIONS

3. Give the two meanings of the word *refuse* and use each in a sentence.
4. Name any six activities that damage the environment.
5. Give any five reasons why the environment should be protected.
6. Name any six ways of protecting the environment.
7. Complete: *We must protect our environment ____ have rain.*
8. Complete: *Our well was polluted, ____ we did not have clean water.*
9. Rewrite beginning with *Since*: *We shall have no rain because the forests have been cut.*
10. Complete: *If we don't build terraces, the soil ____ (erode).*
11. Complete: *If we ____ (not cut) the trees, we would have had more rain.*
12. Complete: *Bush burning ____ ____ ____ overgrazing cause degradation.* (three words)

14. Form abstract nouns from: beautiful, poor, strong, protect, conserve, free.

17. Write a short message of not more than fifteen words for a poster about tree planting.
18. Write a short paragraph (about six sentences) about the importance of protecting the environment.

# ANSWERS TO REVISION QUESTIONS

1. **Pollution** — making air, water or land dirty and harmful; **erosion** — the wearing away and carrying off of soil; **habitat** — the natural home of a plant or animal; **conservation** — the protection of nature and its resources.
2. **Flora** means all the plants of an area, while **fauna** means all the animals of an area.
3. As a **noun** it means rubbish: *The refuse was collected on Monday.* As a **verb** it means to say no: *They refuse to pay the fine.*
4. Any six of: cutting down trees, bush burning, overgrazing, poor farming on slopes, draining swamps, dumping garbage, smoke from factories and vehicles, mining and quarrying, poaching, pouring waste into rivers.
5. Any five of: to keep reliable rainfall; to protect the soil; to keep air and water clean; to protect the habitats of plants and animals; to keep supplying raw materials, food and medicine; to prevent floods, drought and disease; to attract tourists; to leave enough for future generations.
6. Any six of: afforestation and reafforestation; terracing and contour ploughing; mulching and cover crops; crop rotation and manure; controlled grazing; proper waste disposal; protecting wetlands and river banks; using solar and biogas; making and obeying laws; educating people.
7. **to**
8. **so**
9. ***Since** the forests have been cut, we shall have no rain.*
10. **will erode**
11. **had not cut**
12. **as well as**
13. **was**
14. beautiful → **beauty**; poor → **poverty**; strong → **strength**; protect → **protection**; conserve → **conservation**; free → **freedom**.
15. *We **had to** build terraces in order to protect our soil.*
16. Any four of: using dustbins and waste baskets; digging a rubbish pit; composting food waste; burning dangerous waste in an incinerator; recycling plastic, paper and metal; taking waste to a proper dumping site.
17. *Learner's message,* for example: **"Plant a tree today — save the rain for tomorrow."**
18. *Learner's paragraph.* It should have a topic sentence, three or four supporting reasons in the present tense, correct punctuation and a closing sentence.
19. **If 1** is about something real and likely: *If we plant trees, we will get more rain.* **If 3** is about something in the past that did not happen: *If we had planted trees, we would have had more rain.*
20. *Learner's sentences,* for example: *Mulching keeps the soil moist during the dry season.* / *The hospital burns its waste in an incinerator.* / *Bush burning can degrade the land.*
