## 1. LENGTH AND ITS UNITS

**Length** is how long something is. It is measured with a **ruler, tape measure or metre
rule**.

| Change | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 centimetre (cm) | = **10 millimetres (mm)** |
| 1 metre (m) | = **100 centimetres** |
| 1 metre | = 1,000 millimetres |
| 1 kilometre (km) | = **1,000 metres** |

```
mm → cm : ÷ 10        cm → mm : × 10
cm → m  : ÷ 100       m  → cm : × 100
m  → km : ÷ 1,000     km → m  : × 1,000
```

**Examples**

```
75 mm  = 75 ÷ 10  = 7.5 cm
4.2 cm = 4.2 × 10 = 42 mm
350 cm = 350 ÷ 100 = 3.5 m
2.4 m  = 2.4 × 100 = 240 cm
```

---

## 2. PERIMETER

**Perimeter** is the **total distance round** a shape. It is a length, so the answer is in
cm, m or mm.

| Shape | Perimeter |
|---|---|
| Rectangle | **2 × (length + width)** |
| Square | **4 × side** |
| Triangle | side + side + side |
| Any shape | add up all the sides |

**Examples**

```
Rectangle 12 cm by 8 cm:
P = 2 × (12 + 8) = 2 × 20 = 40 cm

Square of side 9 cm:
P = 4 × 9 = 36 cm

Triangle with sides 5 cm, 7 cm and 9 cm:
P = 5 + 7 + 9 = 21 cm
```

**Working backwards:** the perimeter of a square is 48 cm. Find one side.

```
Side = 48 ÷ 4 = 12 cm
```

---

## 3. AREA

**Area** is the amount of **surface** a shape covers. It is measured in **square units** —
cm², m².

| Shape | Area |
|---|---|
| Rectangle | **length × width** |
| Square | **side × side** |
| Triangle | **1/2 × base × height** |

**Examples**

```
Rectangle 12 cm by 8 cm:
A = 12 × 8 = 96 cm²

Square of side 9 cm:
A = 9 × 9 = 81 cm²

Triangle, base 10 cm, height 6 cm:
A = 1/2 × 10 × 6 = 30 cm²
```

**Working backwards:** a rectangle has an area of 72 cm² and a length of 12 cm. Find the
width.

```
Width = 72 ÷ 12 = 6 cm
Check: 12 × 6 = 72 cm²  ✓
```

> **Perimeter is a distance (cm). Area is a surface (cm²).** Never mix the two.

---

## 4. MASS

**Mass** is how heavy something is. It is measured with a **weighing scale or balance**.

| Change | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 kilogram (kg) | = **1,000 grams (g)** |
| 1 tonne | = 1,000 kilograms |

```
kg → g : × 1,000       g → kg : ÷ 1,000
```

**Examples**

```
3 kg    = 3 × 1,000 = 3,000 g
2.5 kg  = 2,500 g
4,750 g = 4,750 ÷ 1,000 = 4.75 kg
```

**Word problem.** A sack holds 50 kg of maize flour. It is packed into 2 kg packets. How
many packets are made?

```
50 ÷ 2 = 25 packets
```

**Word problem.** Three bags weigh 12 kg 500 g, 8 kg 250 g and 6 kg 400 g. Find the total.

```
12 kg 500 g + 8 kg 250 g + 6 kg 400 g
grams: 500 + 250 + 400 = 1,150 g = 1 kg 150 g
kilograms: 12 + 8 + 6 = 26 kg
Total = 26 kg + 1 kg 150 g = 27 kg 150 g
```

---

## 5. CAPACITY

**Capacity** is how much a container can hold. It is measured with a **measuring cylinder,
jug or graduated container**.

| Change | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 litre (l) | = **1,000 millilitres (ml)** |

```
litres → ml : × 1,000        ml → litres : ÷ 1,000
```

**Examples**

```
5 litres    = 5,000 ml
0.75 litres = 750 ml
2,500 ml    = 2.5 litres
```

**Word problem.** A jerrycan holds 20 litres of water. How many 500 ml bottles can be filled
from it?

```
20 litres = 20,000 ml
20,000 ÷ 500 = 40 bottles
```

**Word problem.** A tank holds 450 litres. If 165 litres are used, how much remains?

```
450 − 165 = 285 litres
```

# KEY DEFINITIONS

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Length | How long something is |
| Perimeter | The total distance round a shape |
| Area | The amount of surface a shape covers |
| Mass | How heavy something is |
| Capacity | How much a container can hold |
| Millimetre | One tenth of a centimetre |
| Gram | One thousandth of a kilogram |
| Millilitre | One thousandth of a litre |
| Square centimetre (cm²) | The area of a square of side 1 cm |

---

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- 10 mm = 1 cm · 100 cm = 1 m · 1,000 m = 1 km.
- 1,000 g = 1 kg · 1,000 ml = 1 litre.
- **Perimeter of a rectangle = 2 × (L + W).** **Area = L × W.**
- **Area of a triangle = 1/2 × base × height.**
- Perimeter is written in **cm**; area is written in **cm²**.
- To go from a **big unit to a small unit, multiply**; from small to big, **divide**.
- Both measurements must be in the **same unit** before you calculate.

---

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- A rectangle 12 cm by 8 cm has perimeter **40 cm** and area **96 cm²**.
- A square of side 9 cm has perimeter **36 cm** and area **81 cm²**.
- A triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm has area **30 cm²**.
- 75 mm = **7.5 cm**; 350 cm = **3.5 m**.
- 4,750 g = **4.75 kg**; 2,500 ml = **2.5 litres**.
- 20 litres fills **40** bottles of 500 ml.

---

# COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Giving the area when the perimeter is asked for.
- Writing cm instead of cm² for an area.
- Forgetting the **1/2** in the area of a triangle.
- Multiplying when you should divide in a conversion.
- Adding a length in cm to a length in mm without converting first.
