## 1. PEOPLE IN OUR DISTRICT

**Who are the people in our district?**

The people in our district are the different groups of people who live and work in the district.

**What makes up the social environment?**

People and the things they make make up the social environment.

**What are some things made by people in our district?**

- Houses
- Roads
- Schools
- Hospitals
- Markets
- Shops
- Churches
- Mosques
- Factories

**Why do people live in a district?**

People live in a district to carry out different activities such as farming, fishing, trading, teaching and other kinds of work.

**Why is it important to study the people in our district?**

- To know the people who live in our district.
- To know their origins.
- To know their activities.
- To understand their cultures.
- To understand how they live and work.
- To promote peaceful living.

## 2. ORIGIN OF THE PEOPLE IN OUR DISTRICT

**What is origin?**

Origin is the place where a person or group of people came from.

**What does the origin of people in a district mean?**

It means knowing where the people in the district came from.

**What is a tribe?**

A tribe is a group of people who have a common culture, language and history.

**What is a clan?**

A clan is a group of people who believe that they have a common ancestor.

**What is immigration?**

Immigration is the movement of people into a place to live there.

**What is emigration?**

Emigration is the movement of people out of a place to live somewhere else.

**What are some tribes found in our district?**

The tribes found in a district depend on the particular district. Learners should know the major tribes found in their own district.

**What languages are spoken by people in our district?**

People in a district may speak different languages depending on their tribes and communities.

**How are clans related to tribes?**

Several clans may form a tribe.

**Mention some traditional legends about the origin of people in Uganda.**

- The story of Kintu and Nambi in Buganda.
- The story of Labong and Gipir in Luo-speaking areas.
- The story of Mundu and Sera in Bugisu.

**Why do people move from one place to another?**

People move from one place to another for different reasons.

**Give reasons why people move into our district.**

- Looking for water.
- Looking for food.
- Looking for pasture.
- Looking for fertile soils.
- Looking for jobs.
- Looking for better living conditions.
- Looking for security.
- Running away from conflicts.
- Escaping drought.
- Escaping epidemics.

**Why do some people leave our district?**

- Looking for jobs.
- Looking for fertile land.
- Looking for food and water.
- Looking for pasture.
- Running away from conflicts.
- Escaping drought.
- Moving to places with better opportunities.

**Why should we learn about the origin of people in our district?**

- It helps us understand our history.
- It helps us understand different cultures.
- It helps us respect other people.
- It promotes unity.
- It helps us understand why different groups live in the district.

## 3. SETTLEMENT OF PEOPLE IN OUR DISTRICT

**What is settlement?**

Settlement is the place where people live.

**What is settlement pattern?**

Settlement pattern is the way people are spread or arranged in an area.

**Why are some places in the district more populated than others?**

Some places have more people because they have better conditions for living and working.

**Mention factors that influence settlement patterns in our district.**

- Fertile soils.
- Enough and reliable rainfall.
- Trade.
- Jobs.
- Availability of food.
- Good security.
- Availability of water.
- Good roads.
- Health services.
- Education.
- Other social services.

**How does fertile soil affect settlement?**

People are attracted to areas with fertile soils because they can grow crops well.

**How does rainfall affect settlement?**

Areas with enough and reliable rainfall attract people because they support farming and provide water.

**How do jobs affect settlement?**

People move to places where there are jobs so that they can earn money.

**How does trade affect settlement?**

Trading centres attract people because people can buy and sell goods there.

**How does security affect settlement?**

People prefer to live in places where they are safe and peaceful.

**How do social services affect settlement?**

People are attracted to places with services such as schools, health centres, water and good roads.

**Why do many people settle near roads?**

People settle near roads because roads make transport and communication easier.

**Why do people settle near markets?**

People settle near markets because they can easily buy and sell goods.

**What may happen when many people settle in one place?**

- More houses are built.
- More shops may be opened.
- More schools may be built.
- More health centres may be needed.
- More roads and other services may be developed.

## 4. TYPES OF WORK DONE BY PEOPLE IN OUR DISTRICT

**What is work?**

Work is an activity done by a person to earn a living or meet needs.

**What is an occupation?**

An occupation is the work a person does to earn a living.

**Mention types of work done by people in our district.**

- Farming
- Fishing
- Pottery
- Brick laying
- Craft work
- Trading
- Teaching
- Office work

**What is farming?**

Farming is the growing of crops and keeping of animals.

**What is fishing?**

Fishing is the catching of fish for food or sale.

**What is pottery?**

Pottery is the making of things from clay.

**Mention things that can be made from clay.**

- Pots
- Plates
- Cups
- Cooking stoves
- Other clay objects

**What is brick laying?**

Brick laying is the building of structures using bricks.

**What is craft work?**

Craft work is the making of useful or decorative things by hand.

**Mention some crafts made by people in our district.**

- Mats
- Baskets
- Pots
- Carpets
- Other woven items

**What is trading?**

Trading is the buying and selling of goods and services.

**What is teaching?**

Teaching is helping people to gain knowledge and skills.

**What is office work?**

Office work is work carried out in an office, such as keeping records and serving people.

**Why do people work?**

- To earn money.
- To get food.
- To provide shelter.
- To pay school fees.
- To pay medical expenses.
- To provide clothing.
- To meet other needs.

---

## 5. PROBLEMS FACED BY PEOPLE AT WORK

People face different problems while carrying out their work.

**Mention problems faced by people while doing their work.**

- Insecurity and wars.
- Diseases of animals and birds.
- Poor health.
- Poor transport.
- Poor roads.
- Lack of knowledge and skills.
- Weather changes.
- Poverty.
- Unstable prices of goods.
- Thieves.
- Laziness.

**How does insecurity affect people's work?**

Insecurity can make people lose property and prevent them from working freely.

**How do animal diseases affect farmers?**

Animal diseases can cause animals to become sick or die, causing farmers to lose income.

**How does poor health affect people's work?**

People who are sick may not be able to work well or work regularly.

**How do poor roads affect people's work?**

Poor roads make it difficult and expensive to transport people and goods.

**How does lack of knowledge and skills affect work?**

People may find it difficult to do some jobs properly when they lack the necessary knowledge and skills.

**How does bad weather affect people's work?**

Bad weather can destroy crops, affect transport and make some work difficult.

**How does poverty affect people's work?**

Poverty may prevent people from getting the tools and money needed to do their work.

**How do thieves affect people's work?**

Thieves may steal people's crops, animals, money or other property.

**How can some problems faced at work be reduced?**

- Improving roads.
- Providing security.
- Getting medical care.
- Teaching people new skills.
- Using better farming methods.
- Providing better transport.
- Protecting people's property.
- Working hard.

## 6. SOCIAL ACTIVITIES IN OUR DISTRICT

**What are social activities?**

Social activities are activities in which people come together and interact with one another.

**Mention social activities carried out in our district.**

- Introduction ceremonies.
- Marriages.
- Weddings.
- Naming ceremonies.
- Circumcision.
- Cultural celebrations.
- Community activities.

**What is an introduction ceremony?**

An introduction ceremony is a ceremony in which a person introduces his or her future spouse to the family.

**What is a marriage?**

Marriage is the union of a man and a woman as husband and wife according to the customs, religion or laws of their society.

**What is a wedding?**

A wedding is a ceremony held to celebrate a marriage.

**What is a naming ceremony?**

A naming ceremony is a ceremony held when a child is given a name.

**What is circumcision?**

Circumcision is the removal of the foreskin from the penis.

**Why do people take part in social activities?**

- To bring people together.
- To celebrate important events.
- To strengthen family relationships.
- To preserve culture.
- To promote cooperation.
- To teach acceptable behaviour.

---

## 7. IMPORTANCE OF SOCIAL ACTIVITIES

**Why are social activities important?**

- They keep families together.
- They promote culture.
- They unite people.
- They promote acceptable behaviour.
- They promote working together.
- They strengthen family and clan ties.
- They help people share ideas.
- They help people support one another.

**How do social activities promote culture?**

They help people pass on their language, food, dressing, customs and traditions to younger people.

**How do social activities unite people?**

They bring people together to celebrate, work and share experiences.

**How do social activities strengthen families?**

They bring family members together and help them support one another.

**How do social activities promote acceptable behaviour?**

They teach people how to behave well and respect others in society.

## 8. PEOPLE'S WAY OF LIFE

**What is a way of life?**

A way of life is how people live and carry out their daily activities.

**Mention factors that contribute to people's way of life in our district.**

- Work.
- Availability of food.
- Security.
- Education.
- Diseases.
- Unemployment.
- Poverty.
- Trade.
- Transport.
- Communication.

**How does work affect people's way of life?**

Work gives people money to provide food, shelter, education, clothing and other needs.

**How does food affect people's way of life?**

Availability of food helps people live healthy and active lives.

**How does security affect people's way of life?**

Good security allows people to live, work and travel peacefully.

**How does education affect people's way of life?**

Education gives people knowledge and skills that can help them get jobs and make better decisions.

**How do diseases affect people's way of life?**

Diseases make people sick, reduce their ability to work and may cause them to spend money on treatment.

**How does unemployment affect people's way of life?**

Unemployment makes it difficult for people to earn money to meet their needs.

**How does poverty affect people's way of life?**

Poverty makes it difficult for people to get basic needs such as food, shelter, education and medical care.

**How does trade affect people's way of life?**

Trade provides people with goods, services and opportunities to earn money.

**How does transport affect people's way of life?**

Good transport makes it easier for people and goods to move from one place to another.

**How does communication affect people's way of life?**

Communication helps people share information and keep in touch with one another.

## 9. QUALITY OF LIFE OF PEOPLE IN OUR DISTRICT

**What is quality of life?**

Quality of life is the general well-being of people.

**What things can improve the quality of life of people in our district?**

- Good education.
- Good health services.
- Enough food.
- Good housing.
- Employment.
- Good roads.
- Clean water.
- Security.
- Good communication.
- Peace.

**How can education improve people's quality of life?**

Education gives people knowledge and skills for getting jobs and solving problems.

**How can health services improve people's quality of life?**

Health services help prevent and treat diseases so that people can remain healthy.

**How can good roads improve people's quality of life?**

Good roads make transport easier and help people take goods to markets and reach services.

**How can peace improve people's quality of life?**

Peace allows people to live, work, study and develop their communities safely.

## 10. LIVING TOGETHER IN OUR DISTRICT

**Why should people in our district live together peacefully?**

- To promote unity.
- To promote development.
- To help one another.
- To avoid conflicts.
- To create a safe community.

**How can people live peacefully with one another?**

- Respecting one another.
- Helping one another.
- Cooperating.
- Sharing.
- Obeying laws.
- Solving disagreements peacefully.
- Respecting other people's cultures.

**Why should we respect people from different tribes?**

We should respect them because all people are important and have a right to live peacefully with others.

**Why should we respect other people's cultures?**

We should respect other people's cultures to promote peace, unity and good relationships.

**What is cooperation?**

Cooperation is working together to achieve a common goal.

**Why is cooperation important in our district?**

- It makes work easier.
- It promotes unity.
- It helps people solve problems.
- It promotes development.
- It strengthens relationships.
