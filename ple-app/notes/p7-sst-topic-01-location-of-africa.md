## 1. POSITION OF AFRICA USING LINES OF LATITUDE AND LONGITUDE

### Definitions

**Lines of latitude:** Imaginary lines drawn on a map or globe running from **east to west**, showing how far a place is north or south of the Equator.

**Lines of longitude:** Imaginary lines drawn on a map or globe running from **north to south**, showing how far a place is east or west of the Prime Meridian.

**Equator:** The line of latitude at **0°** which divides the earth into the Northern and Southern Hemispheres.

**Prime Meridian (Greenwich Meridian):** The line of longitude at **0°** which divides the earth into the Eastern and Western Hemispheres.

### Explanation

To find any place on the world map, we use two numbers: one from a line of latitude and one from a line of longitude. Where the two lines cross is the position of the place.

Africa lies **between about 37°N and 35°S**, and **between about 17°W and 51°E**.

Four important lines cross Africa:

| Line | Position | Some countries it crosses |
|---|---|---|
| Tropic of Cancer | 23½°N | Mauritania, Mali, Algeria, Niger, Libya, Chad, Egypt |
| Equator | 0° | Gabon, Congo, D.R. Congo, **Uganda**, Kenya, Somalia |
| Tropic of Capricorn | 23½°S | Namibia, Botswana, South Africa, Mozambique, Madagascar |
| Prime Meridian | 0° | Algeria, Mali, Burkina Faso, Togo, Ghana |

### Important Points

- Africa is the **only continent** crossed by the Equator, both Tropics **and** the Prime Meridian.
- Because the Equator passes through the middle of Africa, part of Africa is in the Northern Hemisphere and part is in the Southern Hemisphere.
- Uganda lies on the Equator, so Uganda is in **both** hemispheres.
- The northernmost point of Africa is in Tunisia; the southernmost point is Cape Agulhas in South Africa.

## 2. CONTINENTS OF THE WORLD AND THEIR SIZES

### Definition

**Continent:** A very large mass of land on the earth's surface.

### The seven continents in order of size (largest first)

1. **Asia**
2. **Africa**
3. **North America**
4. **South America**
5. **Antarctica**
6. **Europe**
7. **Australia**

### Important Points

- Africa is the **second largest** continent in the world, after Asia.
- Africa covers about **30 million square kilometres**.
- Africa has **54 countries** that are members of the United Nations. The African Union has 55 members.
- Australia is the smallest continent; Antarctica is the coldest and has no countries.

**A simple way to remember the order:**
**A**sia, **A**frica, **N**orth America, **S**outh America, **A**ntarctica, **E**urope, **A**ustralia.

## 3. OCEANS AND SEAS AROUND AFRICA

### Definitions

**Ocean:** A very large body of salty water covering part of the earth's surface.

**Sea:** A body of salty water smaller than an ocean, often partly enclosed by land.

### The four oceans of the world

- **Atlantic Ocean**
- **Pacific Ocean**
- **Indian Ocean**
- **Arctic Ocean**

### Which ones touch Africa

| Water body | Where it is found in relation to Africa |
|---|---|
| Atlantic Ocean | **West** of Africa |
| Indian Ocean | **East** and **south-east** of Africa |
| Mediterranean Sea | **North** of Africa |
| Red Sea | **North-east** of Africa |

**Note carefully:** the Pacific Ocean and the Arctic Ocean are oceans of the world, but they **do not** touch Africa. This is a common examination trap.

### Important Points

- The Mediterranean Sea separates Africa from Europe.
- The Red Sea separates Africa from Asia.
- Africa is joined to Asia by a narrow piece of land called the **Isthmus of Suez**, through which the **Suez Canal** was dug.

## 4. THE REGIONS OF AFRICA

Africa is divided into **six regions**:

1. **Northern Africa**
2. **West Africa**
3. **East Africa**
4. **Central Africa**
5. **Southern Africa**
6. **The Horn of Africa**

### Explanation

Countries are grouped into regions because they are near one another and often share climate, vegetation, history and trade. Uganda is in **East Africa**.

The Horn of Africa is the piece of land in the north-east that sticks out into the Indian Ocean, shaped like the horn of an animal.

## 6. ISLANDS WHICH ARE PART OF AFRICA

### Definition

**Island:** A piece of land completely surrounded by water.

### Island countries of Africa

| Island country | Ocean | Capital city |
|---|---|---|
| Madagascar | Indian Ocean | Antananarivo |
| Seychelles | Indian Ocean | Victoria |
| Mauritius | Indian Ocean | Port Louis |
| Comoros | Indian Ocean | Moroni |
| Cape Verde | Atlantic Ocean | Praia |
| São Tomé and Príncipe | Atlantic Ocean | São Tomé |

### Important Points

- **Madagascar** is the largest island in Africa and the fourth largest in the world.
- **Zanzibar** is an island, but it is not a separate country — it is part of **Tanzania**.
- Most African islands lie in the Indian Ocean, on the eastern side.

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- Africa is the **second largest** continent after Asia.
- Africa has **54 countries** in the United Nations; the African Union has 55 members.
- Africa has **six regions**: Northern, West, East, Central, Southern and the Horn of Africa.
- Africa is crossed by the **Equator, Tropic of Cancer, Tropic of Capricorn and Prime Meridian**.
- The **Atlantic Ocean** is west of Africa; the **Indian Ocean** is east.
- The **Mediterranean Sea** separates Africa from Europe.
- The **Red Sea** separates Africa from Asia.
- **Madagascar** is the largest African island.
- Uganda is in **East Africa** and lies on the **Equator**.
