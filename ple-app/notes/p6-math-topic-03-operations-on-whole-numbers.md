# TOPIC 3: OPERATIONS ON WHOLE NUMBERS

*Curriculum reference: NCDC Primary Six Curriculum, Set One, Mathematics Theme 2 Topic 2,
5 periods (`curriculum/P6-SET-ONE-2010.pdf`, pages 168–171).*

**What you already know:** You can add, subtract, multiply and divide. In P.6 you use the
four operations on numbers **up to 9,999,999**, with and without **regrouping**, and you
solve word problems.

**By the end of this topic you should be able to:**

- add and subtract whole numbers with or without regrouping
- multiply and divide whole numbers
- know the other words used for each operation
- work out mixed operations using BODMAS
- solve word problems from everyday life

---

## 1. ADDITION

**Regrouping (carrying):** When a column adds up to 10 or more, write the ones digit and carry the tens to the next column.

```
    3 4 6 8 5 2
  + 2 7 9 4 6 9
  -------------
    6 2 6 3 2 1
```
*Check:* 346,852 + 279,469 = **626,321**

### Other words that mean "add"

sum · total · plus · altogether · increase by · more than · and · in all.

---

## 2. SUBTRACTION

When you cannot subtract, **borrow** from the next column.

```
    7 0 0 4 0 0
  - 2 5 6 7 8 9
  -------------
    4 4 3 6 1 1
```
*Check:* 700,400 − 256,789 = **443,611** (and 443,611 + 256,789 = 700,400 ✓)

### Other words that mean "subtract"

difference · minus · take away · less than · decrease by · reduce by · remainder · how many more.

**Careful with word order:** *"Subtract 15 from 40"* means 40 − 15 = 25, not 15 − 40.

---

## 3. MULTIPLICATION

```
      2 4 6 8
    ×     3 5
    ----------
     1 2 3 4 0     (2468 × 5)
   7 4 0 4 0       (2468 × 30)
   -----------
   8 6 3 8 0
```
*Check:* 2,468 × 35 = **86,380**

### Other words that mean "multiply"

product · times · twice (× 2) · thrice (× 3) · double · of.

---

## 4. DIVISION

*Work out 8,736 ÷ 24.*

24 × 300 = 7,200 → remainder 1,536
24 × 60 = 1,440 → remainder 96
24 × 4 = 96 → remainder 0
Answer = 300 + 60 + 4 = **364**
*Check:* 364 × 24 = 8,736 ✓

### Other words that mean "divide"

quotient · share equally · split · goes into · each · per.

**Note:** in division, the number being divided is the **dividend**, the one dividing is
the **divisor**, the answer is the **quotient**, and what is left is the **remainder**.

---

## 5. MIXED OPERATIONS — BODMAS

**B**rackets → **O**f → **D**ivision → **M**ultiplication → **A**ddition → **S**ubtraction

### Worked examples

1. 24 + 6 × 5 = 24 + 30 = **54** (not 150)
2. (24 + 6) × 5 = 30 × 5 = **150**
3. 100 − 36 ÷ 4 + 8 = 100 − 9 + 8 = **99**
4. 5 × (18 − 6) ÷ 4 = 5 × 12 ÷ 4 = 60 ÷ 4 = **15**

---

## 6. WORD PROBLEMS

**How to solve one:** read it twice · find what is **given** and what is **asked** ·
decide the operation from the key words · work it out · **check** that the answer makes
sense · write the unit (shillings, kilogrammes, pupils).

### Worked examples

1. *A school collected 1,245,600 shillings in Term I and 987,450 in Term II. How much altogether?*
   1,245,600 + 987,450 = **2,233,050 shillings**

2. *A trader had 3,000,000 shillings and bought goods worth 1,845,750. How much was left?*
   3,000,000 − 1,845,750 = **1,154,250 shillings**

3. *A factory makes 1,250 bricks a day. How many bricks in 28 days?*
   1,250 × 28 = **35,000 bricks**

4. *45,600 shillings is shared equally among 24 pupils. How much does each get?*
   45,600 ÷ 24 = **1,900 shillings**

5. *A tank holds 2,400 litres. If 350 litres are used each day, how many full days will it last, and how much is left over?*
   2,400 ÷ 350 = 6 remainder 300 → **6 days, with 300 litres left**

# KEY DEFINITIONS

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sum | The answer in addition |
| Difference | The answer in subtraction |
| Product | The answer in multiplication |
| Quotient | The answer in division |
| Dividend | The number being divided |
| Divisor | The number you divide by |
| Remainder | What is left after dividing |
| Regrouping | Carrying in addition or borrowing in subtraction |
| BODMAS | The order in which operations are worked out |

---

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- Always work in the order **BODMAS**.
- *Sum* → add · *difference* → subtract · *product* → multiply · *quotient* → divide.
- "Subtract 15 **from** 40" means **40 − 15**.
- Check subtraction by **adding back**; check division by **multiplying back**.
- Multiplication and addition are commutative; **subtraction and division are not**.
- Always write the **unit** in a word problem answer.

---

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- 24 + 6 × 5 = 54 (multiply first).
- The product of 2,468 and 35 = 86,380.
- 8,736 ÷ 24 = 364.
- "Decrease 5,000 by 1,275" → 5,000 − 1,275 = 3,725.
- "Twice" means × 2; "thrice" means × 3.
- Always check your answer by working backwards.

---

# COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Adding before multiplying when there are no brackets.
- Forgetting to carry or to borrow.
- Reversing a subtraction word problem.
- Losing the remainder in a division word problem.
- Leaving out the unit in the answer.

# P.6 QUICK REVISION

- **BODMAS** always
- sum + · difference − · product × · quotient ÷
- "Subtract A **from** B" = **B − A**
- Check: subtraction by adding back, division by multiplying back
- Word problems: read twice, find the key word, work out, check, write the unit
