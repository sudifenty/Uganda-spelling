## 1. TYPES OF FRACTIONS

| Type | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Proper (vulgar) fraction** | The top is smaller than the bottom | 3/5 |
| **Improper fraction** | The top is bigger than the bottom | 9/4 |
| **Mixed number** | A whole number with a fraction | 2¼ |
| **Equivalent fractions** | Different fractions of the same value | 1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6 |

**Changing forms:** 2¼ = (2 × 4 + 1)/4 = **9/4**  ·  9/4 = 9 ÷ 4 = **2¼**

---

## 2. THE FOUR OPERATIONS ON FRACTIONS

| Operation | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Add / subtract | Use the **LCM** of the denominators | 3/4 + 1/6 = 9/12 + 2/12 = **11/12** |
| Multiply | Multiply tops and bottoms, then simplify | 3/5 × 10/9 = 30/45 = **2/3** |
| Divide | **Turn the second fraction upside down and multiply** | 4/5 ÷ 2/3 = 4/5 × 3/2 = 12/10 = **1⅕** |
| Of | "of" means multiply | 2/3 of 27 = **18** |

**Mixed numbers must be changed to improper fractions first:**
1½ × 2⅔ = 3/2 × 8/3 = 24/6 = **4**

### BODMAS with fractions

*Work out 1/2 + 1/4 × 2/3*
= 1/2 + 2/12 (multiply first)
= 1/2 + 1/6 = 3/6 + 1/6 = **4/6 = 2/3**

## 3. DECIMALS

### Place value after the point

| Place | 1st | 2nd | 3rd |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Tenths | Hundredths | Thousandths |

In 4.276, the 2 is in the **tenths** place, the 7 in the **hundredths** and the 6 in the **thousandths**.

### Rounding off

Look at the digit **just after** the place you are rounding to: if it is **5 or more**, add
one; if **4 or less**, leave it.

| Number | Round to | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 7.846 | Nearest tenth | **7.8** |
| 3.567 | Nearest hundredth | **3.57** |
| 12.4951 | Nearest thousandth | **12.495** |
| 8.96 | Nearest whole number | **9** |

### Operations on decimals

- **Add / subtract:** line up the **points**. 4.5 + 12.75 = **17.25**
- **Multiply:** ignore the points, multiply, then count the decimal places. 0.4 × 0.3 = **0.12**
- **Divide by a decimal:** make the divisor a whole number. 4.8 ÷ 0.6 = 48 ÷ 6 = **8**

### Changing between fractions and decimals

3/8 = 3 ÷ 8 = **0.375**  ·  0.45 = 45/100 = **9/20**  ·  2/3 = **0.666…** (repeating)

## 4. RATIO AND PROPORTION

### Ratio

*Share 45,000 shillings between Ann and Ben in the ratio 4 : 5.*
Total shares = 9; one share = 45,000 ÷ 9 = 5,000.
Ann = 4 × 5,000 = **20,000**; Ben = 5 × 5,000 = **25,000** ✓

### Direct proportion

*6 books cost 18,000 shillings. Find the cost of 10 books.*
1 book = 3,000 → 10 books = **30,000 shillings**

### Inverse proportion

*8 workers finish a job in 12 days. How long will 6 workers take?*
Total work = 8 × 12 = 96 worker-days → 96 ÷ 6 = **16 days**

## 5. PERCENTAGES, PROFIT, LOSS AND INTEREST

### The three conversions

| Fraction | Decimal | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| 1/4 | 0.25 | 25% |
| 1/2 | 0.5 | 50% |
| 3/5 | 0.6 | 60% |
| 7/20 | 0.35 | 35% |

**Fraction → percentage:** multiply by 100. **Percentage → fraction:** divide by 100.

### Worked examples

1. *Find 15% of 60,000 shillings.* → 15/100 × 60,000 = **9,000 shillings**
2. *Express 24 out of 40 as a percentage.* → 24/40 × 100 = **60%**
3. **Profit:** *A radio bought at 50,000 was sold at 65,000.*
   Profit = 15,000. Percentage profit = 15,000/50,000 × 100 = **30%**
4. **Loss:** *A goat bought at 120,000 was sold at 96,000.*
   Loss = 24,000. Percentage loss = 24,000/120,000 × 100 = **20%**
5. **Discount:** *A dress marked 40,000 is sold at a 10% discount.*
   Discount = 4,000 → new price = **36,000 shillings**
6. **Simple interest:** **I = P × R × T ÷ 100**
   *Find the simple interest on 200,000 shillings for 2 years at 10% per year.*
   I = 200,000 × 10 × 2 ÷ 100 = **40,000 shillings**. Amount = 200,000 + 40,000 = **240,000**

**Remember:** profit and loss percentages are always worked out on the **buying (cost)
price**.

# KEY DEFINITIONS

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Vulgar (proper) fraction | A fraction whose top is smaller than its bottom |
| Improper fraction | A fraction whose top is bigger than its bottom |
| Mixed number | A whole number together with a fraction |
| Reciprocal | A fraction turned upside down |
| Rounding off | Writing a number to a given place value |
| Ratio | A way of comparing quantities |
| Proportion | The relationship in size between two quantities |
| Percentage | A fraction out of 100 |
| Profit | Selling price minus buying price |
| Loss | Buying price minus selling price |
| Simple interest | Money paid for the use of money: P × R × T ÷ 100 |

---

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- To divide by a fraction, **multiply by its reciprocal**.
- "Of" means **multiply**.
- Change mixed numbers to **improper fractions** before multiplying or dividing.
- Use **BODMAS** with fractions and decimals too.
- Percentage = part ÷ whole × 100.
- **Profit % and loss % use the buying price.**
- **I = P × R × T ÷ 100**; Amount = Principal + Interest.
- More workers → **fewer** days (inverse proportion).

# COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Adding denominators: 1/2 + 1/3 is **not** 2/5.
- Multiplying mixed numbers without changing them to improper fractions.
- Working profit percentage on the **selling** price.
- Misplacing the decimal point when multiplying.
- Treating an inverse-proportion question as direct.

# P.6 QUICK REVISION

- ÷ a fraction → **flip and multiply** · mixed numbers → improper first
- Rounding: look at the **next digit**; 5 or more rounds up
- Fraction → % : × 100 · % → fraction : ÷ 100

- **I = P × R × T ÷ 100** · Amount = P + I
