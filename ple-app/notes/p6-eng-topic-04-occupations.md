# PRIMARY 6 ENGLISH

# TOPIC 4: OCCUPATIONS

*Curriculum reference: NCDC Primary Six Curriculum, Set One, English Topic 4, 36 periods
(`curriculum/P6-SET-ONE-2010.pdf`, topic outline page 24 and pages 37–46).
Sub-topics: **4A Carpentry (7)**, **4B Tailoring (9)**, **4C Baking (11)** and
**4D Keeping Animals (9)**.*

**What you already know:** You see carpenters, tailors, bakers and farmers in your
community. In P.6 you learn the **English of these four trades** — their tools, their
processes and the structures used to describe them.

**By the end of this topic you should be able to:**

- name the tools and materials used in carpentry, tailoring and baking
- describe a process in the correct order using *first, next, then, finally*
- use *… is used for …*, *… prefer … to …*, *be careful when …*, *not only … but also …*
- use the passive voice, the perfect tense and the "to" infinitive
- talk and write about animal care using *should* and *should not*

## SUB-TOPIC 4A: CARPENTRY

### Vocabulary and tools

| Word | Meaning / use |
|---|---|
| **carpenter** | A person who makes things out of wood |
| **saw** | Used for cutting wood |
| **plane** | Used for smoothing wood |
| **hammer** | Used for driving in nails |
| **drill** | Used for making holes |
| **screwdriver** | Used for driving in screws |
| **sandpaper** | Used for smoothing a surface |
| **bench** | The strong table a carpenter works on |
| **plank / timber** | Sawn pieces of wood |
| **wood glue** | Used for sticking pieces of wood together |
| **varnish / polish** | Put on wood to make it shine and last |
| **saw dust** | The powder left after sawing |

**Products:** chairs, tables, beds, doors, windows, cupboards, shelves, coffins.

### Describing the process

*First* you measure and mark the wood, *next* you cut it with a saw, *then* you plane it
smooth, *after that* you join the pieces with nails or wood glue, and *finally* you sand it
and apply varnish.

### Language structures

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| **… is used for …** | *A saw **is used for** cutting wood.* |
| **First … next … then …** | ***First** you plane the wood, **next** you cut it into pieces, **then** you use wood glue to fix them.* |
| **… besides …** | *The carpenter made a chair **besides** a table.* |
| **Though …** | ***Though** John is not a trained carpenter, he makes good chairs.* |

### Grammar focus: the passive voice

In the **active** voice the doer comes first; in the **passive** the thing done comes first.

| Active | Passive |
|---|---|
| The carpenter **cuts** the wood. | The wood **is cut** by the carpenter. *(present simple)* |
| The carpenter **is making** a table. | A table **is being made** by the carpenter. *(present continuous)* |
| The carpenter **has finished** the chair. | The chair **has been finished** by the carpenter. *(present perfect)* |

**The pattern:** the right form of **be** + the **past participle**.

## SUB-TOPIC 4B: TAILORING

### Vocabulary and tools

| Word | Meaning / use |
|---|---|
| **tailor / seamstress** | A person who makes and mends clothes (a woman who sews is often called a seamstress) |
| **sewing machine** | The machine used for stitching |
| **needle** | Used for hand sewing |
| **thread** | The line used in sewing |
| **bobbin** | The small reel holding the lower thread in a machine |
| **tape measure** | Used for taking measurements |
| **scissors** | Used for cutting cloth |
| **thimble** | Worn on a finger to protect it when hand sewing |
| **seam** | The line where two pieces of cloth are joined |
| **hemming** | Turning and stitching the edge of cloth |
| **button hole** | The hole through which a button passes |
| **patch** | A piece of cloth used to mend a hole |
| **zip** | A fastener with two rows of teeth |
| **texture** | How a material feels |
| **garment** | A piece of clothing |

### Describing the process

*First* the tailor takes the customer's **measurements**, *next* he chooses and cuts the
**material** to a **design**, *then* he **stitches** the pieces on the sewing machine,
*after that* he does the **hemming** and makes the **button holes**, and *finally* he
presses the **garment** with an iron.

### Language structures

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| **Be careful when …** | ***Be careful when** stitching the cloth, because you may hurt your finger.* |
| **… prefer … to …** | *Robert **prefers** sewing **to** knitting.* |

### Grammar focus: the perfect tense and adverbs

- **Present perfect:** *has/have + past participle* — *The tailor **has finished** my dress.*
- **Past perfect:** *had + past participle* — *He **had cut** the cloth before I arrived.*
- **Adverbs:** *carefully, quickly, neatly, well* — *She sews **neatly**.*
- **Conjunctions:** *and, but, because, when, although* join the ideas together.

## SUB-TOPIC 4C: BAKING

### Vocabulary and ingredients

| Word | Meaning / use |
|---|---|
| **baker / bakery** | The person who bakes / the place where baking is done |
| **oven** | Used for baking |
| **baking tin** | The container in which cakes and bread are baked |
| **baking flour** | The main ingredient |
| **yeast** | Makes the dough rise |
| **dough** | The soft mixture of flour and water before baking |
| **knead** | To press and fold the dough with the hands |
| **sieve** | Used for sifting the flour |
| **roll out** | To flatten dough with a rolling pin |
| **cookie cutter** | Used for cutting shapes out of dough |
| **icing sugar** | Fine sugar used for decorating cakes |
| **food colour / flavour** | Used to colour and to give taste |
| **ingredients** | All the items used to make something |
| **recipe** | The instructions for making a dish |
| **a pinch of** | A very small amount held between two fingers |
| **tier** | One layer of a cake standing above another |

**Products:** bread, buns, cakes, biscuits, cookies, a wedding cake, doughnuts.

### A simple recipe (written as instructions)

1. **Sieve** the flour into a bowl and add a **pinch** of salt.
2. Mix the **margarine** and sugar until soft.
3. Add the eggs and **flavour**, then mix in the flour.
4. **Knead** the dough and **roll it out**.
5. Cut shapes with a **cookie cutter** and place them in a greased **baking tin**.
6. Bake in a hot **oven** for about twenty minutes.
7. **Turn off** the oven, remove the tin and let the cakes cool.
8. Decorate with **icing sugar**.

### Language structures

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| **"to" infinitive** | *Don't forget **to shut** the oven.* |
| **… used to …** | *I **used to** bake cakes when I was young.* |
| **… used for …** | *A spoon **is used for** mixing butter and sugar.* |
| **… prefers … to …** | *Musa **prefers** cakes **to** bread.* |

**Careful:** *used to* (past habit) is different from *is used for* (purpose) and from
*is used to* (is accustomed to).

### Grammar focus: forming nouns

| Verb | Noun | Verb | Noun |
|---|---|---|---|
| bake | baker / bakery / baking | sew | sewing / sewer |
| mix | mixture | measure | measurement |
| decorate | decoration | build | builder / building |
| teach | teacher | cook | cook / cooking |

## SUB-TOPIC 4D: KEEPING ANIMALS

### Vocabulary from the syllabus

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **welfare** | The good condition and comfort of an animal |
| **husbandry** | The care and management of animals |
| **veterinary** | To do with the treatment of animals |
| **dip** | To put an animal into a liquid that kills ticks |
| **tether** | To tie an animal with a rope to a peg |
| **dock** | To cut short an animal's tail |
| **stray** | An animal wandering with no owner |
| **cruelty** | Deliberately causing pain to an animal |
| **torture** | Causing severe pain |
| **misery / sorrow / anxiety** | Suffering and distress |
| **comfort / discomfort** | Being at ease / not being at ease |
| **body signs** | The way an animal shows how it feels |
| **slaughter** | To kill an animal for meat |

### Caring for animals

Give **clean water and enough feed** · build a **clean, dry shelter** · **treat and
vaccinate** them · **dip or spray** against ticks · handle them **gently** · give them
**space and company** · protect them from thieves and bad weather · never overwork or beat
them · call a **veterinary officer** when they are sick.

### How animals show how they feel (body signs and sounds)

A dog **wags its tail** when happy and **growls** when angry · a cow **lows (moos)** when
hungry or looking for its calf · a hen **clucks** to call its chicks · a goat **bleats** ·
a cat **purrs** when content and **hisses** when frightened. A sick animal is **quiet, dull,
off its feed** and keeps apart from the others.

### Language structures

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| **You should …** | *You **should** mind about the welfare of animals.* |
| **You should not …** | *You **should not** dock dogs, because it hurts them.* |
| **Not only … but also …** | ***Not only** should you feed animals **but also** treat them when they are sick.* |
| **If 2 conditional** | *If I **had** a cow, I **would** build it a shed.* |

**Note:** after *Not only* at the beginning of a sentence, the verb comes **before** the
subject: *Not only **should you** feed them…*

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- **… is used for + verb-ing:** *A saw is used **for cutting** wood.*
- Order words: **first, next, then, after that, finally**.
- Passive = the right form of **be + past participle**.
- **prefer … to …** — *He prefers sewing **to** knitting.*
- **used to** = a past habit; **is used for** = purpose.
- After **Not only** at the start, the verb comes **before** the subject.
- **Yeast** makes dough rise; **kneading** is pressing and folding the dough.
- **Docking** is cutting an animal's tail short — the syllabus says you **should not** do it.

---

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- A saw is used ____ cutting wood → **for**.
- A person who makes clothes → a **tailor**.
- Tool used for taking measurements → a **tape measure**.
- What makes dough rise → **yeast**.
- Fine sugar used for decorating cakes → **icing sugar**.
- Robert prefers sewing ____ knitting → **to**.
- Change to passive: *The carpenter cuts the wood.* → *The wood **is cut** by the carpenter.*
- Cutting an animal's tail short → **docking**.

---

# COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Writing *"used for cut"* — after **for** use the **-ing** form.
- Writing *"prefers sewing than knitting"* — it is **prefer … to …**.
- Confusing **used to** (past habit) with **is used for** (purpose).
- Forgetting the **be** verb in the passive: *"The wood cut by the carpenter."*
- Writing *"Not only you should feed…"* — the verb comes first: *Not only **should you** feed…*
- Spelling: **scissors, thimble, bakery, veterinary, carpenter**.

4. Change to the passive: *The tailor has finished my shirt.*
5. Name any six items used by a tailor and say what each is used for.
6. Give one word for: the line where two pieces of cloth are joined.
7. Give one word for: the small object worn on a finger to protect it when sewing.
8. Complete: *Be careful ____ stitching the cloth, because you may hurt your finger.*
9. Complete: *Robert prefers sewing ____ knitting.*
10. Name any six things used in baking and say what each is used for.
11. What is the work of yeast in baking? What does *knead* mean?
12. Write out five steps of baking cakes, in order.
13. Complete: *Don't forget ____ ____ the oven.* (two words)

16. Give any six ways of caring for animals.
17. Give the meaning of: welfare, docking, tethering, stray.

# ANSWERS TO REVISION QUESTIONS

1. Any six: **saw** — cutting wood; **plane** — smoothing wood; **hammer** — driving in nails; **drill** — making holes; **screwdriver** — driving in screws; **sandpaper** — smoothing a surface; **tape measure** — measuring.
2. *Learner's answer,* for example: **First** he measures and marks the wood, **next** he cuts it with a saw, **then** he planes it smooth and joins the pieces with nails, and **finally** he sands it and applies varnish.
3. *A table **is being made** by the carpenter.*
4. *My shirt **has been finished** by the tailor.*
5. Any six: **sewing machine** — stitching; **needle** — hand sewing; **thread** — sewing; **tape measure** — taking measurements; **scissors** — cutting cloth; **thimble** — protecting the finger; **bobbin** — holding the lower thread.
6. A **seam**.
7. A **thimble**.
8. **when**
9. **to**
10. Any six: **oven** — baking; **baking tin** — holding the dough; **sieve** — sifting flour; **rolling pin** — rolling out dough; **cookie cutter** — cutting shapes; **yeast** — making dough rise; **icing sugar** — decorating.
11. **Yeast** makes the dough **rise** so that the bread or cake is soft and light. To **knead** is to press and fold the dough with the hands until it is smooth.
12. *Learner's answer,* for example: sieve the flour → mix the margarine and sugar → add eggs and flour and knead → put the dough in a greased tin → bake in a hot oven, then cool and decorate.
13. **to shut**
14. **Used to** shows a habit in the past: *I **used to** bake cakes when I was young.* **Is used for** shows purpose: *A spoon **is used for** mixing butter and sugar.*
15. bake → **baker/bakery/baking**; mix → **mixture**; decorate → **decoration**; measure → **measurement**; teach → **teacher/teaching**.
16. Any six of: give clean water and enough feed; build a clean dry shelter; treat and vaccinate them; dip or spray against ticks; handle them gently; give them space; protect them from thieves and bad weather; never beat or overwork them; call a veterinary officer when they are sick.
17. **Welfare** — the good condition and comfort of an animal; **docking** — cutting short an animal's tail; **tethering** — tying an animal with a rope to a peg; **stray** — an animal wandering with no owner.
18. **should you**
19. Any three, for example: a dog **wags its tail** when happy and **growls** when angry; a cow **lows** when hungry or looking for its calf; a hen **clucks** to call its chicks; a sick animal becomes **dull and quiet and refuses to eat**.
20. *Learner's paragraph.* It should name the occupation, give reasons for the choice, mention some of its tools or activities, and be written in one paragraph with correct punctuation.

- Passive = **be + past participle**
