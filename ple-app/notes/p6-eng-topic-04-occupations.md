## INTRODUCTION

**1. What is an occupation?**

An occupation is a job or activity that a person does regularly to earn a living.

**2. What is a profession?**

A profession is a type of occupation that usually requires specialised education,
training or skills.

**3. What is livelihood?**

Livelihood is the way a person earns money or obtains what is needed to live.

**4. Why do people work?**

People work to earn a living and to provide for their needs and those of their
families.

**5. Why is it important to respect different occupations?**

Every honest occupation contributes to individuals, families and the development of
the community.

**6. What occupations are mainly covered under Primary Six Occupations?**

They are:

1. Carpentry
2. Tailoring
3. Baking
4. Keeping Animals

**7. Which other professions may be discussed under occupations?**

Other professions include teaching, medicine and law.

**8. What should we appreciate about occupations?**

We should appreciate the skills, effort and services provided by people in
different occupations.

## PART A: CARPENTRY — INTRODUCTION

**9. What is carpentry?**

Carpentry is the occupation or skill of making and repairing things mainly from wood.

**10. Who is a carpenter?**

A carpenter is a person who makes or repairs wooden objects.

**11. What materials does a carpenter mainly work with?**

A carpenter mainly works with wood or timber.

**12. What products can a carpenter make?**

A carpenter can make: chairs, tables, beds, doors, windows, cupboards, desks,
benches, shelves and stools.

**13. What is furniture?**

Furniture refers to movable objects such as chairs, tables, beds and cupboards that
are used in homes, offices and other places.

**14. What is timber?**

Timber is wood that has been prepared for use in construction or making wooden
objects.

**15. What is a plank?**

A plank is a long, flat piece of wood.

## TOOLS USED IN CARPENTRY

**16. What is a saw?**

A saw is a tool with a toothed blade used for cutting wood.

**17. What is a plane?**

A plane is a carpentry tool used for making the surface of wood smooth and even.

**18. What is a drill?**

A drill is a tool used for making holes in wood and other materials.

**19. What is a hammer?**

A hammer is a tool used mainly for driving nails into wood and for other tasks.

**20. What are nails?**

Nails are small metal objects with pointed ends used for joining pieces of wood.

**21. What is wood glue?**

Wood glue is an adhesive used to join pieces of wood.

**22. What is sandpaper?**

Sandpaper is a rough material used for smoothing wooden surfaces.

**23. What is a screwdriver?**

A screwdriver is a tool used for driving or removing screws.

**24. What is polish?**

Polish is a substance used to improve the appearance and finish of a surface.

**25. What is varnish?**

Varnish is a liquid coating applied to wood to protect it and give it a smooth or
shiny finish.

**26. What is sawdust?**

Sawdust is the fine waste produced when wood is cut or worked on.

**27. What is a workbench?**

A workbench is a strong table on which a carpenter works.

**28. Why does a carpenter use a saw?**

A carpenter uses a saw for cutting wood.

**29. Why does a carpenter use a plane?**

A carpenter uses a plane for smoothing wood.

**30. Why does a carpenter use a hammer?**

A carpenter uses a hammer for driving nails and carrying out other tasks.

**31. Why does a carpenter use sandpaper?**

A carpenter uses sandpaper for smoothing wooden surfaces.

**32. Why does a carpenter use a screwdriver?**

A carpenter uses a screwdriver for fixing and removing screws.

## PROCESSES IN CARPENTRY

**33. What is the first thing a carpenter should do before making an object?**

The carpenter should plan and measure the materials needed.

**34. Why does a carpenter measure wood?**

A carpenter measures wood to obtain the correct sizes for the object being made.

**35. What happens after measuring the wood?**

The carpenter marks the wood and cuts it into the required pieces.

**36. What is done to rough wooden surfaces?**

They are smoothed using a plane and sandpaper.

**37. How can pieces of wood be joined?**

They can be joined using nails, screws, wood glue or other suitable methods.

**38. Why is wood polished?**

Wood is polished to improve its appearance.

**39. Why is varnish applied to wood?**

Varnish helps protect the wood and gives it a good finish.

**40. Arrange these activities correctly: cutting, planning, measuring.**

Answer: Measuring, planning/marking and then cutting.

**41. What should a carpenter do after assembling a wooden object?**

The carpenter should check it, smooth rough parts and give it a suitable finish.

## ACTIVE AND PASSIVE VOICE IN CARPENTRY

**55. What is active voice?**

Active voice is a sentence in which the subject performs the action.

**56. Give an example of active voice.**

"Angela makes good chairs."

**57. What is passive voice?**

Passive voice is a sentence in which the subject receives the action.

**58. Change this sentence into passive voice: "Angela makes good chairs."**

Answer: "Good chairs are made by Angela."

**59. Change this sentence into passive voice: "The carpenter repairs the table."**

Answer: "The table is repaired by the carpenter."

**60. Change this sentence into active voice: "The chair is made by Peter."**

Answer: "Peter makes the chair."

## PART B: TAILORING — INTRODUCTION

**65. What is tailoring?**

Tailoring is the occupation or skill of making, repairing and altering clothes and
other garments.

**66. Who is a tailor?**

A tailor is a person who makes or repairs clothes.

**67. Who is a seamstress?**

A seamstress is a woman who sews clothes professionally.

**68. What is a garment?**

A garment is an item of clothing.

**69. What is tailoring used for?**

Tailoring is used for making, repairing, decorating and altering clothes.

**70. What materials can be used in tailoring?**

Materials include cloth, thread, buttons, zips and other sewing materials.

## TAILORING TOOLS AND MATERIALS

**71. What is a tape measure?**

A tape measure is a flexible measuring tool used for taking body or cloth
measurements.

**72. Why does a tailor use a tape measure?**

A tailor uses it to take accurate measurements.

**73. What is a needle?**

A needle is a thin pointed tool with an eye through which thread is passed.

**74. What are scissors?**

Scissors are cutting tools used for cutting cloth and other materials.

**75. What is a sewing machine?**

A sewing machine is a machine used for joining pieces of cloth with thread.

**76. What is a bobbin?**

A bobbin is a small spool that holds thread in a sewing machine.

**77. What is a thimble?**

A thimble is a small protective covering worn on a finger when sewing by hand.

**78. What is a thread?**

Thread is a thin strand used for sewing or joining pieces of cloth.

**79. What is a button?**

A button is a small object attached to clothing and used for fastening it.

**80. What is a zip?**

A zip is a fastening device used on clothes, bags and other items.

**81. What is a patch?**

A patch is a piece of material used to cover or repair a hole or damaged part of
clothing.

**82. What is a seam?**

A seam is a line where two pieces of cloth are joined.

**83. What is a buttonhole?**

A buttonhole is a small opening made in cloth through which a button passes.

**84. What is cloth?**

Cloth is woven or knitted material used for making clothes and other items.

**85. What is material?**

Material is the substance used for making something.

## TAILORING PROCESSES

**86. What is stitching?**

Stitching is joining pieces of cloth with thread.

**87. What does "sew" mean?**

To sew means to join pieces of material using a needle and thread or a sewing
machine.

**88. What does "mend" mean?**

To mend means to repair something that is damaged or torn.

**89. What does "weave" mean?**

To weave means to make cloth by interlacing threads.

**90. What is hemming?**

Hemming is folding and sewing the edge of a piece of cloth to make it neat and
prevent it from fraying.

**91. What is knitting?**

Knitting is making fabric by interlocking loops of yarn using needles or a machine.

**92. What is a design?**

A design is a plan or pattern showing how something will look.

**93. What is texture?**

Texture is the way a material feels or looks on its surface.

**94. What is an article in tailoring?**

An article is an item made by a tailor or from cloth.

**95. What is a seamstress's first important task when making clothes for a customer?**

She should take the customer's measurements accurately.

**96. Why are measurements important in tailoring?**

They help the tailor make clothes that fit properly.

**97. What happens after measurements are taken?**

The measurements are used to prepare and cut the cloth according to the required
design.

**98. What happens after the cloth is cut?**

The pieces are stitched together according to the design.

**99. What is done after sewing?**

The garment may be checked, fitted, finished, ironed and decorated.

Baking is the process of cooking food using dry heat, usually in an oven.

**129. Who is a baker?**

A baker is a person who makes bread, cakes, biscuits and other baked products.

**130. What is a bakery?**

A bakery is a place where bread, cakes and other baked products are made or sold.

**131. What products can be made by a baker?**

A baker can make: bread, cakes, biscuits, cookies, buns, wedding cakes and other
baked products.

An oven is an appliance used for baking and cooking food.

**133. What is a baking tin?**

A baking tin is a container used for holding food while it is baked.

**134. What is a cookie cutter?**

A cookie cutter is a tool used to cut cookie dough into different shapes.

**135. What is a sieve?**

**136. What is a rolling pin?**

**137. What is a pinch?**

**139. What is food colour?**

**140. What is yeast?**

**141. What is margarine?**

**142. What is baking flour?**

## BAKING INGREDIENTS

**143. What are ingredients?**

Ingredients are the substances or foods used to prepare a particular food.

**144. What ingredients may be used in baking?**

Depending on the recipe, ingredients may include: flour, sugar, salt, butter or
margarine, eggs, milk, yeast, water and flavouring.

**145. What is a recipe?**

A recipe is a set of instructions showing how to prepare a particular food.

It tells the baker the ingredients, quantities and steps needed to prepare the food.

**147. What is flavour?**

**156. What is a loaf?**

**157. What is a bun?**

## PART D: KEEPING ANIMALS — INTRODUCTION

**190. What is animal keeping?**

Animal keeping is the practice of caring for and managing animals.

**191. Why do people keep animals?**

People keep animals for: food, income, companionship, security, breeding, transport
and agricultural purposes.

**192. What is animal welfare?**

Animal welfare refers to the well-being and proper treatment of animals.

**193. Why is animal welfare important?**

It helps animals remain healthy, safe and free from unnecessary suffering.

**194. What animals can people keep?**

People can keep animals such as: cattle, goats, sheep, pigs, chickens, dogs, cats
and rabbits.

## ANIMAL CARE

**195. What is proper animal care?**

Proper animal care means providing animals with suitable food, water, shelter,
health care, protection and clean surroundings.

**196. Why should animals be given food?**

Animals need food for growth, energy and good health.

**197. Why should animals be given clean water?**

Animals need water to survive and remain healthy.

**198. Why should animals have shelter?**

Shelter protects animals from harsh weather, predators and other dangers.

**199. Why should animal shelters be kept clean?**

Clean shelters reduce disease and help animals remain healthy.

**200. What is veterinary care?**

Veterinary care is medical care provided to animals by animal-health professionals.

**201. Who is a veterinary professional?**

A veterinary professional is a person trained to provide healthcare to animals.

**202. Why should sick animals receive treatment?**

Treatment helps them recover and prevents diseases from becoming worse or spreading.

## ANIMAL WELFARE VOCABULARY

**203. What is a pet?**

A pet is an animal kept mainly for companionship.

**204. What does "misery" mean?**

Misery is a state of great suffering or unhappiness.

**205. What is comfort?**

Comfort is a state of being free from pain, distress or discomfort.

**206. What is discomfort?**

Discomfort is a feeling of uneasiness or lack of comfort.

**207. What does "anxious" mean?**

Anxious means worried, nervous or uneasy.

**208. What is anxiety?**

Anxiety is a feeling of worry, nervousness or uneasiness.

**209. What is cruelty?**

Cruelty is behaviour that causes unnecessary suffering or pain.

**210. What is a stray animal?**

A stray animal is an animal that is lost, abandoned or living without proper
ownership or care.

**211. What is sorrow?**

Sorrow is a feeling of sadness.

**212. What does "cruel" mean?**

Cruel means causing pain or suffering deliberately or without concern.

**213. What are body signs in animals?**

Body signs are physical actions or changes that may show how an animal feels or
whether it is healthy.

**214. What is torture?**

Torture is deliberately causing severe pain or suffering.

**215. What is animal husbandry?**

Animal husbandry is the care, breeding and management of domestic animals.

**216. What is dipping?**

Dipping is treating animals with a suitable chemical solution to control external
parasites such as ticks.

**217. What does "slaughter" mean?**

Slaughter means killing an animal, especially for food.

**218. What does "tether" mean?**

**219. What is mistreatment?**

- providing clean water;
- providing shelter;
- keeping shelters clean;

## IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- The four P.6 occupations are **carpentry, tailoring, baking and keeping animals**.
- A **carpenter** works with **wood/timber**: saw cuts, plane smooths, hammer drives
  nails, drill makes holes, screwdriver handles screws, sandpaper smooths, glue
  joins, varnish protects.
- Carpenter's order: **plan and measure → mark → cut → smooth → join → check → finish**.
- A **tailor/seamstress** makes clothes: first **take measurements**, then cut the
  cloth, stitch the pieces, then check, fit, finish, iron and decorate.
- A **baker** follows a **recipe**: prepare and measure ingredients, mix or knead,
  let dough rise, bake at the right temperature — handle the oven carefully.
- **Yeast** makes dough rise; **kneading** presses and folds dough; a **pinch** is a
  very small amount.
- Proper animal care: **food, clean water, shelter, clean surroundings, veterinary
  care, protection and careful handling**.
- Animal sounds: dog **barks**, cat **meows**, cow **moos**, goat/sheep **bleats**,
  pig **grunts**, hen **clucks**, cock **crows**, horse **neighs**.
- Passive voice: the receiver becomes the focus — "Good chairs **are made by** Angela."
- Adverb types: **manner** (carefully), **time** (soon), **place** (outside),
  **emphasis** (very), **quality**.
- Structures: **used for** (purpose), **first…next…then** (order), **prefer…to**
  (preference), **neither…nor**, **not only…but also**, **need not**, **if…would**.
- **All honest occupations are valuable** — respect the work and skills of others.
