# PRIMARY 5 ENGLISH

# TOPIC 2: PRINT MEDIA

*Curriculum reference: NCDC Primary Five Curriculum, Set One, English Topic 2, 16 periods
(`curriculum/P5-SET-ONE-2010.pdf`, pages 18–19).*

**What you already know:** You have seen newspapers and magazines. In P.5 you learn the
**words used in the print media** and you practise the **articles a, an and the**, the
**relative pronouns** and the **past simple and future tenses**.

**By the end of this topic you should be able to:**

- name the different kinds of print media and their parts
- name the people who work in the print media and say what each does
- use *a*, *an* and *the* correctly
- use the relative pronouns *who, which, that, whose*
- use the past simple and the future tense
- write a short article, an advertisement or an announcement

---

## 1. WHAT PRINT MEDIA MEANS

**Print media** are the things that carry news and information in **printed form** —
newspapers, magazines, newsletters, brochures and pamphlets. They are read, not listened to.

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Newspaper** | Printed sheets carrying the news of the day or week |
| **Magazine** | A booklet printed weekly or monthly with stories and pictures |
| **Newsletter** | A short printed letter of news from a school, church or office |
| **Brochure** | A small printed book that advertises a place or a service |
| **Pullout** | A section that can be pulled out of a newspaper and kept |

---

## 2. VOCABULARY FROM THE SYLLABUS

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **news** | Reports of things that have just happened |
| **story** | An account of an event, written for readers |
| **article** | A piece of writing on one subject in a newspaper or magazine |
| **editorial** | The article that gives the opinion of the newspaper itself |
| **column** | A regular piece written by the same person; also a narrow strip of print down a page |
| **front page** | The first page, carrying the biggest news |
| **back page** | The last page, which often carries sport |
| **advertisement** | A notice that tells people about something for sale |
| **announcement** | A public notice telling people about an event |
| **cartoon** | A funny drawing, often about the news |
| **crossword** | A word puzzle in squares |
| **puzzle** | A game that makes you think |
| **media** | All the ways news reaches people |

### The people who work in the print media

| Person | Work |
|---|---|
| **Reporter** | Goes out, finds the facts and writes the news |
| **Journalist** | Anyone whose work is collecting and writing news |
| **Editor** | Corrects the work and decides what will be printed |
| **Columnist** | Writes a regular column, often giving opinions |
| **Photographer** | Takes the pictures that go with the stories |
| **Vendor** | Sells the newspapers in the street |

> **Reporter or journalist?** Every reporter is a journalist, but a journalist may also be
> an editor, a columnist or a photographer.

---

## 3. GRAMMAR: THE ARTICLES A, AN AND THE

| Article | When it is used | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **a** | Before a word beginning with a **consonant sound** | Mpungu is **a** good columnist. |
| **an** | Before a word beginning with a **vowel sound** (a, e, i, o, u) | The journalist wrote **an** interesting story. |
| **the** | For something already known or only one of its kind | **The** article was on **the** front page. |

**Careful cases**

| Correct | Why |
|---|---|
| **an** hour | *hour* begins with a vowel **sound** — the h is silent |
| **a** university | *university* begins with the sound "yu" |
| **an** editor | begins with a vowel sound |
| **a** newspaper | begins with a consonant sound |

---

## 4. GRAMMAR: RELATIVE PRONOUNS

A **relative pronoun** joins two sentences and tells us more about a person or thing.

| Pronoun | Used for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **who** | People | The reporter **who** wrote the story is my uncle. |
| **which** | Things | The article **which** appeared in the newspaper last week was sad. |
| **that** | People or things | The magazine **that** I bought has good pictures. |
| **whose** | Belonging to | The editor **whose** office is upstairs called us. |
| **whom** | People, as the object | The columnist **whom** we met is famous. |

**Joining two sentences**

```
The story was sad. It appeared in the newspaper.
→ The story which appeared in the newspaper was sad.

I met a journalist. She works for a daily paper.
→ I met a journalist who works for a daily paper.
```

---

## 5. GRAMMAR: TENSES

| Tense | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Present simple** | Something that happens regularly | The vendor **sells** newspapers every morning. |
| **Past simple** | Something finished in the past | The advertisement **appeared** yesterday. |
| **Future (will)** | Something still to happen | The advertisement on games and sports **will be** in the newspapers next week. |

**Past simple of some useful verbs:** write → **wrote** · read → **read** · buy → **bought** ·
say → **said** · print → **printed** · appear → **appeared** · sell → **sold**.

## 6. WRITING FOR THE PRINT MEDIA

### An announcement

```
ANNOUNCEMENT

All pupils of Primary Five are informed that the debate that was to take place
on Friday has been moved to Monday, 3rd March, at 2:00 p.m. in the school hall.

Signed: The Head Prefect
```

An announcement gives **what, when, where, who** — and nothing more.

### An advertisement

```
FOR SALE

A strong bicycle in good condition. New tyres and brakes.
Price: 180,000 shillings. Call at Plot 4, Market Road, Mbale.
```

An advertisement is **short**, uses **attractive words** and gives a way to reach the seller.

### An article

An article has a **heading**, an **introduction**, a **body** with the facts, and a
**conclusion**. It should answer **who, what, where, when, why and how**.

# KEY DEFINITIONS

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Print media | News and information that reaches people in printed form |
| Article | A piece of writing on one subject |
| Editorial | The article that gives the newspaper's own opinion |
| Reporter | A person who finds the facts and writes the news |
| Editor | The person who corrects and decides what is printed |
| Columnist | A person who writes a regular column |
| Advertisement | A notice about something for sale |
| Announcement | A public notice about an event |
| Relative pronoun | A word such as *who* or *which* that joins two sentences |

---

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- **Print** media are read; radio and television are **not** print media.
- **a** before a consonant sound, **an** before a vowel sound.
- It is **an hour** but **a university** — go by the **sound**, not the letter.
- **who** for people, **which** for things, **that** for either.
- The **editorial** carries the newspaper's opinion; a **column** carries one writer's opinion.
- The **front page** carries the biggest news; the **back page** often carries sport.
- *Will* is used for the future.

---

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- The article **which** appeared in the newspaper last week was sad.
- The journalist wrote **an** interesting story about elections.
- Mpungu is **a** good columnist.
- The advertisement on games and sports **will be** in the newspapers next week.
- A person who sells newspapers in the street is a **vendor**.
- The past simple of *write* is **wrote**.

---

# COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Writing *a article* instead of **an article**.
- Writing *a hour* instead of **an hour**.
- Using **which** for people or **who** for things.
- Mixing tenses in one sentence: *Yesterday he writes an article.*
- Calling the radio a print medium.
- Writing an advertisement that leaves out the price or the address.
