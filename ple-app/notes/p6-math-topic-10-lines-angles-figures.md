# TOPIC 10: LINES, ANGLES AND GEOMETRIC FIGURES

*Curriculum reference: NCDC Primary Six Curriculum, Set One, Mathematics Theme 5 Topic 1,

**What you already know:** You can name shapes and measure angles. In P.6 you **construct**
lines, angles and figures, learn **Pythagoras' theorem**, and study **quadrilaterals** and
**prisms**.

**By the end of this topic you should be able to:**

- construct parallel and perpendicular lines and use their symbols
- construct angles of 30°, 45°, 60° and 90°
- construct a regular hexagon
- name the types of angles and the pairs made by a transversal
- apply Pythagoras' theorem in a right-angled triangle
- state the properties of quadrilaterals and prisms

**Instruments needed:** a sharp pencil, a ruler, a pair of compasses, a protractor and a set square.

## 1. LINES

| Term | Meaning | Symbol |
|---|---|---|
| **Line segment** | A straight line with two end points | AB |
| **Ray** | A line with one end point, going on in one direction | — |
| **Parallel lines** | Lines that never meet, always the same distance apart | **∥** |
| **Perpendicular lines** | Lines that meet at 90° | **⊥** |
| **Intersecting lines** | Lines that cross each other | — |
| **Transversal** | A line that cuts across two or more other lines | — |
| **Vertex** | The point where two lines meet | — |
| **Bisect** | To cut into two equal parts | — |

## 2. ANGLES

| Angle | Size |
|---|---|
| Acute | Less than 90° |
| Right | Exactly 90° |
| Obtuse | Between 90° and 180° |
| Straight | Exactly 180° |
| Reflex | Between 180° and 360° |

### Special pairs of angles

| Pair | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Complementary angles** | Add up to **90°** |
| **Supplementary angles** | Add up to **180°** |
| **Vertically opposite angles** | **Equal** |
| **Adjacent angles on a straight line** | Add up to **180°** |
| **Angles at a point** | Add up to **360°** |

### With a transversal across parallel lines

| Pair | Rule | Shape to look for |
|---|---|---|
| **Corresponding angles** | Equal | letter **F** |
| **Alternate angles** | Equal | letter **Z** |
| **Co-interior (allied) angles** | Add up to **180°** | letter **C** or **U** |

*Example:* if one angle is 70°, its vertically opposite angle is **70°**, its
corresponding angle is **70°**, its alternate angle is **70°**, and its co-interior angle
is 180 − 70 = **110°**.

---

## 3. CONSTRUCTIONS

**Never rub out your arcs — they show your working.**

### (a) A perpendicular bisector of a line

Open the compasses to more than half the line · draw arcs above and below from each end
with the same radius · join the two crossing points. The line is cut into two equal parts
at **90°**.

### (b) Bisecting an angle

From the corner, draw an arc cutting both arms · from each cut, draw arcs that cross ·
join the corner to the crossing point.

### (c) Constructing angles

| Angle | How |
|---|---|
| **60°** | Draw an arc from the end of the line; from where it cuts the line, draw another arc of the same radius; join |
| **90°** | Construct a perpendicular, or bisect a straight angle |
| **45°** | **Bisect 90°** |
| **30°** | **Bisect 60°** |
| **120°** | Two 60° arcs, one after the other |

### (d) A regular hexagon

Draw a circle of any radius · keeping the compasses at that **same radius**, step round the
circumference, making six marks · join the six points in order. The result is a regular
hexagon with six equal sides and six equal angles of **120°**.

## 4. PYTHAGORAS' THEOREM

**In a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides:**

**a² + b² = c²**, where **c** is the **hypotenuse** — the side opposite the right angle and always the longest.

### Worked examples

1. *The two shorter sides are 3 cm and 4 cm. Find the hypotenuse.*
   c² = 9 + 16 = 25 → c = √25 = **5 cm**

2. *The hypotenuse is 13 cm and one side is 5 cm. Find the other side.*
   b² = 169 − 25 = 144 → b = **12 cm**

3. *A ladder 10 m long leans against a wall, with its foot 6 m from the wall. How far up the wall does it reach?*
   h² = 100 − 36 = 64 → h = **8 m**

**Triples worth remembering:** 3, 4, 5 · 6, 8, 10 · 5, 12, 13 · 8, 15, 17 · 9, 12, 15.

---

## 5. QUADRILATERALS AND POLYGONS

**Quadrilateral:** A closed figure with **four** straight sides. Its angles add up to **360°**.

| Quadrilateral | Properties |
|---|---|
| **Square** | 4 equal sides; 4 right angles; diagonals equal and cross at 90° |
| **Rectangle** | Opposite sides equal; 4 right angles; diagonals equal |
| **Rhombus** | 4 equal sides; opposite angles equal; diagonals cross at 90° |
| **Parallelogram** | Opposite sides equal and parallel; opposite angles equal |
| **Trapezium** | Only **one** pair of parallel sides |
| **Kite** | Two pairs of adjacent equal sides; one line of symmetry |

### Angle sums of polygons

**Sum of interior angles = (n − 2) × 180°** · **Sum of exterior angles = 360°**

| Polygon | Sides | Interior sum | Each interior angle (regular) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Triangle | 3 | 180° | 60° |
| Quadrilateral | 4 | 360° | 90° |
| Pentagon | 5 | 540° | 108° |
| Hexagon | 6 | 720° | **120°** |
| Octagon | 8 | 1,080° | 135° |

---

## 6. PRISMS AND SOLIDS

**Prism:** A solid with two identical ends (the cross-section) and flat rectangular sides all along its length.

| Solid | Faces | Edges | Vertices |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cube** | 6 | 12 | 8 |
| **Cuboid** | 6 | 12 | 8 |
| **Triangular prism** | 5 | 9 | 6 |
| **Square pyramid** | 5 | 8 | 5 |
| **Cylinder** | 3 (2 flat, 1 curved) | 2 | 0 |

**Properties of a prism:** the two ends are identical and parallel · the cross-section is
the same all along · the side faces are rectangles · its volume = **area of cross-section ×
length**.

**Lines of symmetry:** a square has 4, a rectangle 2, an equilateral triangle 3, a circle
countless, and a regular hexagon 6.

# KEY DEFINITIONS

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Parallel lines | Lines that never meet, always the same distance apart |
| Perpendicular lines | Lines that meet at 90° |
| Transversal | A line cutting across two or more lines |
| Complementary angles | Angles that add up to 90° |
| Supplementary angles | Angles that add up to 180° |
| Bisect | To cut into two equal parts |
| Hypotenuse | The longest side of a right-angled triangle, opposite the right angle |
| Quadrilateral | A four-sided closed figure |
| Prism | A solid with identical parallel ends and rectangular sides |
| Line of symmetry | A line that divides a shape into two matching halves |

---

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- Symbols: **∥** parallel, **⊥** perpendicular.
- Complementary = **90°**; supplementary = **180°**; angles at a point = **360°**.
- **F** corresponding (equal) · **Z** alternate (equal) · **C** co-interior (**180°**).
- **60°** comes from equal arcs; **30°** is half of 60°; **45°** is half of 90°.
- A regular hexagon is drawn by **stepping the radius six times round a circle**; each angle is **120°**.
- **Pythagoras: a² + b² = c²**, with c the **hypotenuse**.
- Angles of a quadrilateral add up to **360°**; interior sum of a polygon = (n − 2) × 180°.
- A cube has **6 faces, 12 edges and 8 vertices**.

---

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- One angle on parallel lines is 70° → alternate 70°, co-interior 110°.
- Sides 3 cm and 4 cm → hypotenuse 5 cm.
- Hypotenuse 13 cm, one side 5 cm → other side 12 cm.
- Each interior angle of a regular hexagon = 120°.
- Angle sum of a quadrilateral = 360°.
- Faces of a triangular prism = 5.
- Angle constructed by bisecting 60° = 30°.

---

# COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Rubbing out construction arcs.
- Using the hypotenuse as one of the shorter sides in a² + b² = c².
- Forgetting to take the **square root** at the end of a Pythagoras question.
- Mixing complementary (90°) with supplementary (180°).
- Saying a trapezium has two pairs of parallel sides.
- Measuring an angle from the wrong scale of the protractor.

# REVISION QUESTIONS

1. Give the difference between parallel and perpendicular lines, and write the symbol for each.
2. Name the five types of angles according to size.
3. What is the complement of 35°? What is the supplement of 35°?
4. Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One angle is 65°. Find the co-interior and the alternate angle.
5. Describe briefly how to construct an angle of 60°.
6. How do you construct an angle of 45°?
7. Describe how to construct a regular hexagon using a pair of compasses.
8. State Pythagoras' theorem.
9. The two shorter sides of a right-angled triangle are 6 cm and 8 cm. Find the hypotenuse.
10. The hypotenuse is 17 cm and one side is 8 cm. Find the other side.
11. A ladder 13 m long has its foot 5 m from a wall. How far up the wall does it reach?
12. State any three properties of a rhombus.
13. Give the difference between a trapezium and a parallelogram.
14. Find the sum of the interior angles of a hexagon, and each angle if it is regular.
15. How many faces, edges and vertices has a triangular prism?

---

# ANSWERS TO REVISION QUESTIONS

1. **Parallel lines** never meet and stay the same distance apart (symbol **∥**); **perpendicular lines** meet at 90° (symbol **⊥**).
2. Acute, right, obtuse, straight and reflex.
3. Complement = 90 − 35 = **55°**; supplement = 180 − 35 = **145°**.
4. Alternate angle = **65°**; co-interior angle = 180 − 65 = **115°**.
5. Draw a straight line. Place the compass point at one end and draw an arc cutting the line. Without changing the radius, place the point where the arc cuts the line and draw another arc crossing the first. Join the end of the line to the crossing point.
6. First construct 90°, then **bisect** it.
7. Draw a circle of any radius. Keeping the compasses at the **same radius**, step round the circumference making six marks, then join the six points in order.
8. In a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides: **a² + b² = c²**.
9. c² = 36 + 64 = 100 → c = **10 cm**
10. b² = 289 − 64 = 225 → b = **15 cm**
11. h² = 169 − 25 = 144 → h = **12 m**
12. Any three of: all four sides are equal; opposite angles are equal; opposite sides are parallel; the diagonals cross at 90°.
13. A **trapezium** has only **one** pair of parallel sides, while a **parallelogram** has **two** pairs of parallel sides with opposite sides equal.
14. (6 − 2) × 180 = **720°**; each angle of a regular hexagon = 720 ÷ 6 = **120°**
15. **5 faces, 9 edges and 6 vertices.**
