# PRIMARY 7 ENGLISH

# TOPIC 1: SCHOOL HOLIDAYS

*Curriculum reference: NCDC Primary Seven Curriculum, Set One, English Topic 1, 30 periods
(`curriculum/PRIMARY-SEVEN-SET-ONE.pdf`, topic outline page 30 and pages 32–35).
Sub-topics: **1A Holiday Plans** and **1B Holiday Activities**.*

**What you already know:** You have talked and written about your holidays before. In P.7
you learn the **vocabulary, language structures and grammar** that let you describe plans
and activities correctly.

**By the end of this topic you should be able to:**

- use holiday vocabulary correctly in speech and writing
- use "going to" for the near future and form question tags
- change direct speech into reported speech
- use participles and the third conditional (If 3)
- form adjectives and use the active voice
- write compositions, stories and poems about holiday plans and activities

### Language structures

| Structure | Example | When it is used |
|---|---|---|
| **… going to …** (near future) | *I am going to visit my grandmother next holiday.* | For a plan already decided |
| **Question tags** | *You will go to the village next week, **won't you**?* | To ask for agreement |
| **Reported speech** | *She said that she was going to travel.* | To report what someone said |

### Grammar focus 1: Question tags

A question tag is a short question added at the end of a statement.

**The rule:** positive statement → **negative** tag; negative statement → **positive** tag.
The tag uses the same auxiliary verb and a pronoun.

| Statement | Tag |
|---|---|
| You are travelling tomorrow, | **aren't you?** |
| She isn't coming, | **is she?** |
| We shall visit Jinja, | **shan't we?** |
| They went home, | **didn't they?** |
| Peter can swim, | **can't he?** |
| Let us go, | **shall we?** |
| Open the window, | **will you?** |
| I am late, | **aren't I?** |

### Grammar focus 2: Reported speech

**Direct speech** gives the exact words in quotation marks. **Reported (indirect) speech**
tells what was said, without quotation marks.

**When reporting, the tense moves one step back:**

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| present simple → | past simple |
| present continuous → | past continuous |
| past simple → | past perfect |
| will → | would · can → could · may → might |

Words also change: *today → that day · tomorrow → the following day · here → there ·
this → that · now → then · I → he/she.*

**Examples**

- Direct: *"I am going to the village," said Ann.*
  Reported: *Ann said that **she was going** to the village.*
- Direct: *"We shall write to our pen pals," the pupils said.*
  Reported: *The pupils said that **they would write** to their pen pals.*
- Direct: *"Did you pick her from the park?" he asked.*
  Reported: *He asked **whether I had picked** her from the park.*

### Grammar focus 3: Forming adjectives

| From a noun | Adjective | From a verb | Adjective |
|---|---|---|---|
| danger | dangerous | enjoy | enjoyable |
| beauty | beautiful | excite | exciting / excited |
| care | careful / careless | interest | interesting / interested |
| friend | friendly | tire | tiring / tired |
| child | childish | bore | boring / bored |

**Careful:** *-ing* describes the thing (*the journey was **tiring***); *-ed* describes the
person (*I was **tired***).

### Vocabulary from the syllabus

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| tour | A journey visiting several places |
| camp | To stay in tents away from home |
| concert | A show of music, dance and drama |
| revise | To go over work already learnt |
| chores | Small jobs done at home |
| routine | The usual order of doing things each day |
| vacation | A holiday period |

### Language structures

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| **Participle tense** | *Having finished my chores, I went to play.* |
| **If 3 (third conditional)** | *If I had seen him in the holiday, he would have given me money.* |

### Grammar focus 4: Participles

A **participle** is a form of a verb used with a helping verb or as an adjective.

| Type | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Present participle | verb + **-ing** | *We were **swimming** in the lake.* |
| Past participle | usually verb + **-ed**, or an irregular form | *We have **eaten**. The **broken** cup…* |
| Perfect participle | **having** + past participle | ***Having washed** the plates, she rested.* |

### Grammar focus 5: The third conditional (If 3)

**If 3** talks about something in the **past that did not happen**.

**If + past perfect , would have + past participle**

- *If I **had known**, I **would have come** earlier.*
- *If we **had saved** money, we **would have travelled** to Mombasa.*
- *If she **had revised**, she **would have passed**.*

Compare the three conditionals:

| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| If 1 (real, future) | If + present simple, will + verb | *If it rains, we **will stay** indoors.* |
| If 2 (unreal, present) | If + past simple, would + verb | *If I **had** money, I would buy a bicycle.* |
| If 3 (unreal, past) | If + past perfect, would have + past participle | *If I **had had** money, I would have bought a bicycle.* |

### Grammar focus 6: Active voice

In the **active voice** the subject does the action; in the **passive** it receives it.

- Active: **Mother cooked** the food. → Passive: The food **was cooked** by mother.
- Active: **The pupils cleaned** the compound. → Passive: The compound **was cleaned** by the pupils.

The syllabus asks you to use the **active voice** when telling stories, because it is
shorter and livelier.

### Writing practice

**A holiday composition should have:** an interesting **title**, an **introduction** saying
when and where, a **body** in proper paragraphs telling what happened in order, and a
**conclusion** saying how you felt. Use the **past tense**, joining words (*first, then,
after that, finally*), and correct punctuation.

# KEY DEFINITIONS

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Question tag | A short question added at the end of a statement |
| Direct speech | The exact words spoken, written in quotation marks |
| Reported speech | Telling what someone said without using their exact words |
| Participle | A verb form ending in -ing or -ed used with a helping verb or as an adjective |
| Conditional sentence | A sentence with "if", showing that one thing depends on another |
| Active voice | A sentence in which the subject does the action |
| Passive voice | A sentence in which the subject receives the action |
| Adjective | A word that describes a noun |
| Pen pal | A friend one writes letters to |

---

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- Positive statement takes a **negative** tag; negative statement takes a **positive** tag.
- *I am late,* **aren't I?** (not "amn't I")
- In reported speech the tense moves **one step back** and time words change.
- **If 3** = *If + had + past participle , would have + past participle.*
- *-ing* adjectives describe the **thing**; *-ed* adjectives describe the **person**.
- "Going to" is used for a plan already made.
- Use the **past tense** in a holiday composition.

---

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- *You will travel tomorrow, ____?* → **won't you**
- *"I am tired," said John.* → *John said that **he was tired**.*
- *If I had gone early, I ____ (meet) her.* → **would have met**
- Form the adjective from *danger* → **dangerous**; from *enjoy* → **enjoyable**.
- Change to the active voice: *The letter was written by Ann.* → *Ann wrote the letter.*
- One word for "a friend one writes to but has never met" → **pen pal**.

---

# COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Writing *"amn't I?"* instead of **aren't I?**
- Keeping the present tense in reported speech: *She said she **is** going* → *She said she **was** going.*
- Mixing conditionals: *If I would have known…* → *If I **had** known…*
- Saying *I was very boring* when you mean *I was very **bored***.
- Forgetting the comma before the question tag.

1. Add a correct question tag: *You are travelling to Gulu tomorrow, ____?*
2. Add a correct question tag: *She didn't attend the concert, ____?*
3. Add a correct question tag: *Let us begin the journey, ____?*

5. Change to reported speech: *"We shall board the bus at six o'clock," the children said.*
6. Change to direct speech: *He said that he had enjoyed the trip.*
7. Complete with the correct form: *If I ____ (know) about the party, I would have come.*
8. Complete: *If she had revised well, she ____ (pass) the examination.*
9. Give the adjective formed from each: care, beauty, friend, excite.
10. Fill in: *The journey was very ____ (tire) and we were all ____ (tire) when we arrived.*

12. Rewrite using "going to": *I intend to visit my grandparents next holiday.*
13. Give one word for: a friend you write letters to but may never have met.
14. Give one word for: extra lessons given to help a learner who is behind.
15. Use the word *commence* in a sentence of your own.
16. Rewrite beginning with the words given: *He finished his chores and then went to play.* — **Having** …
17. Give the meaning of *up-country* and use it in a sentence.
18. Write four sentences describing your plans for the next holiday, using "going to" in each.
19. Write a short paragraph (about six sentences) about the most interesting thing you did last holiday. Use the past tense.
20. Give two differences between direct speech and reported speech.

# ANSWERS TO REVISION QUESTIONS

1. **aren't you?**
2. **did she?**
3. **shall we?**
4. Maria said that **she was going to visit her aunt**.
5. The children said that **they would board the bus at six o'clock**.
6. *"I enjoyed the trip,"* **he said**. (Also acceptable: *"I have enjoyed the trip," he said.*)
7. **had known**
8. **would have passed**
9. care → **careful** (or careless); beauty → **beautiful**; friend → **friendly**; excite → **exciting** (or excited).
10. The journey was very **tiring** and we were all **tired**.
11. **The pupils swept the compound.**
12. **I am going to visit my grandparents next holiday.**
13. A **pen pal**.
14. **Remedial classes.**
15. *Learner's sentence,* for example: *The lessons will **commence** at eight o'clock.*
16. **Having finished his chores, he went to play.**
17. *Up-country* means away from the city, in the villages. Example: *My father travelled **up-country** to see his parents.*
18. *Learner's answer.* Each sentence must use "am/is/are going to" + verb, for example: *I **am going to** help my mother in the garden.*
19. *Learner's paragraph.* It should be in the **past tense**, in one paragraph, with a topic sentence, correct punctuation and capital letters.
20. In **direct speech** the exact words are used and put in quotation marks, while in **reported speech** the words are not exact and there are no quotation marks; in reported speech the **tense moves one step back** and words such as *today, here, this* change to *that day, there, that*.

# P.7 QUICK REVISION

- Question tags: **positive → negative**, negative → positive · *I am…, **aren't I?***
- Reported speech: tense **one step back**, change time and place words
- **If 3**: *If + had + p.p. , would have + p.p.* (about the past that did not happen)
- Participles: **-ing** (present) · **-ed / irregular** (past) · **having + p.p.** (perfect)
- **-ing** describes the thing · **-ed** describes the person
- Holiday composition: title · introduction · body in paragraphs · conclusion · past tense
