**By the end of this topic you should be able to:**

---

## 1. CHANGE IN THE ENVIRONMENT

**Change:** Something becoming different from what it was.

Changes happen all around us — day into night, seedlings into trees, children into adults,
wet soil drying, iron rusting. Changes are grouped into **biological, physical and
chemical**.

### Agents of change

**Heat and cold** · **water** · **air (oxygen and wind)** · **living things** — bacteria,
fungi, plants, animals and **people** · **time** · **chemicals**.

---

## 2. BIOLOGICAL CHANGES

**Biological change:** A change that happens in living things as they grow and develop.

| Change | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Growth in plants** | A seed germinates → a seedling → a mature plant → flowers → fruits and seeds |
| **Growth in animals** | An egg → a tadpole → a frog; a chick → a hen; a calf → a cow |
| **Growth in human beings** | Baby → child → **adolescent** → adult → old person |
| **Decay** | Dead plants and animals rot into humus |
| **Metamorphosis** | Egg → larva → pupa → adult (butterfly, mosquito, housefly) |
| **Healing** | A wound closing; a broken bone joining |

### Physical (bodily) changes in growing children

| In girls | In boys |
|---|---|
| Breasts develop; hips widen | Voice deepens; shoulders broaden |
| **Menstruation begins** | **Wet dreams begin** |
| Pubic and armpit hair grows | Pubic, armpit and facial hair grows |
| Rapid growth in height; oily skin and pimples | Rapid growth in height and muscle; oily skin and pimples |

**These changes are normal.** They come at different ages for different people, and nobody
should be laughed at for changing early or late.

### Managing body changes

**Body care:** bathe daily and wash the private parts with clean water · wear clean cotton
underwear and change it daily · girls should use **clean sanitary materials** and change
them regularly · keep nails short and hair clean · eat well and rest.

**Guidance:** talk to a **parent, teacher, health worker or another trusted adult** about
anything that worries you · choose good friends and resist peer pressure · **abstain from
sex** at your age · say **NO** to anyone who tries to touch your private parts, get away and
**report it at once** — it is never the child's fault.

---

## 3. PHYSICAL CHANGES

**Physical change:** A change in which **no new substance is formed**, and which can usually be **reversed**.

| Example | What happens |
|---|---|
| Ice **melting** into water | Same substance, different state |
| Water **boiling** into steam, steam **condensing** | Reversible |
| **Dissolving** salt in water | The salt can be got back by evaporation |
| **Breaking** a stick or a glass | Still the same material |
| **Bending** a wire or **moulding** clay | Shape changes only |
| **Expansion and contraction** of metal | Size changes only |
| **Drying** clothes | Water evaporates |
| **Magnetising** a steel bar | Can be demagnetised |

---

## 4. CHEMICAL CHANGES

**Chemical change:** A change in which a **new substance is formed**, and which usually **cannot be reversed**.

| Example | New substance formed |
|---|---|
| **Burning** wood, paper or charcoal | Ash, smoke, carbon dioxide |
| **Rusting** of iron | Rust (iron oxide) |
| **Cooking** food | New substances — the food cannot go back to being raw |
| **Souring** of milk | Curds and acid |
| **Rotting** of fruit or dead matter | Humus and gases |
| **Digestion** of food | Simple sugars, amino acids |
| **Ripening** of fruit | Sugars formed |
| **Photosynthesis** | Starch and oxygen |
| **Baking** with yeast | Carbon dioxide and new dough |

### How to tell them apart

| Physical change | Chemical change |
|---|---|
| No new substance | **A new substance is formed** |
| Usually **reversible** | Usually **irreversible** |
| Only shape, size or state changes | The material itself changes |
| Little or no heat or light produced | Often gives off **heat, light, gas or a smell** |

**Rusting** needs both **air (oxygen) and water**. It is prevented by **painting, oiling,
greasing, galvanising, plastic coating** and keeping metals dry.

---

# KEY DEFINITIONS

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Change | Something becoming different from what it was |
| Biological change | A change happening in living things as they grow |
| Physical change | A change in which no new substance is formed |
| Chemical change | A change in which a new substance is formed |
| Reversible | Able to go back to the original state |
| Irreversible | Not able to go back to the original state |
| Metamorphosis | The change of form as an insect or amphibian grows |
| Decay | The rotting of dead plant and animal matter |
| Rusting | The chemical change of iron in air and water |
| Adolescence | The period of change from childhood to adulthood |

---

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- Three types of change: **biological, physical and chemical**.
- **Physical** — no new substance, usually **reversible** (melting, dissolving, breaking).
- **Chemical** — a **new substance** is formed, usually **irreversible** (burning, rusting, cooking, souring).
- Agents of change: **heat, water, air, living things, time, chemicals**.
- **Metamorphosis:** egg → larva → pupa → adult.
- **Rusting needs air and water**; prevent it by painting, oiling or greasing.
- Body changes at adolescence are **normal**; keep clean and talk to a trusted adult.

---

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- Melting of ice → a **physical** change. Burning of paper → a **chemical** change.
- Rusting of iron → a **chemical** change, needing **air and water**.
- Souring of milk → **chemical**. Dissolving sugar → **physical**.
- The change from a tadpole to a frog → **biological (metamorphosis)**.
- One way of preventing rusting → **painting** (or oiling, greasing, galvanising).
- One sign that a chemical change has taken place → a new substance, or heat, light, gas or a smell.
- What a child should do when worried about body changes → talk to a **trusted adult**.

# ANSWERS TO REVISION QUESTIONS

1. Change is something becoming different from what it was. The three types are **biological, physical and chemical**.
2. Any five of: heat and cold, water, air (oxygen and wind), living things such as bacteria, fungi, plants, animals and people, time, chemicals.
3. A biological change is a change that happens in living things as they grow and develop. Examples: germination of a seed; growth of a chick into a hen; a tadpole becoming a frog; decay of dead matter; healing of a wound (any four).
4. **Baby → child → adolescent → adult → old person.**
5. **Girls:** breasts develop; hips widen; menstruation begins; pubic and armpit hair grows. **Boys:** the voice deepens; shoulders broaden; wet dreams begin; pubic, armpit and facial hair grows.
6. Any six of: bathe daily and wash the private parts with clean water; wear clean cotton underwear and change it daily; use and change clean sanitary materials; keep nails short and hair clean; eat well and rest; talk to a trusted adult about worries; choose good friends; abstain from sex.
7. The child should **say NO firmly, move away and tell a trusted adult at once** — a parent, teacher, health worker, religious leader or the police. It must not be kept secret, and it is **never the child's fault**.
8. A physical change is one in which **no new substance is formed** and which can usually be reversed. Examples: melting ice; boiling water; dissolving salt; breaking a stick; bending a wire (any five).
9. A chemical change is one in which **a new substance is formed** and which usually cannot be reversed. Examples: burning wood; rusting of iron; cooking food; souring of milk; rotting of fruit (any five).
10. In a **physical** change no new substance is formed, it is usually **reversible**, and only shape, size or state changes; in a **chemical** change a **new substance** is formed, it is usually **irreversible**, and heat, light, gas or a smell is often produced.
11. Melting ice — **physical**; burning wood — **chemical**; dissolving salt — **physical**; rusting iron — **chemical**; breaking glass — **physical**; souring milk — **chemical**.
12. **Air (oxygen) and water (moisture).**
13. Any four of: painting; oiling or greasing; galvanising (coating with zinc); coating with plastic; keeping the metal dry; using stainless steel or non-rusting metals.
14. Metamorphosis is the **change of form as an animal grows**. For a butterfly: **egg → larva (caterpillar) → pupa → adult butterfly**.
15. Because cooking forms **new substances** — the food changes in colour, smell, taste and texture, and it **cannot be turned back** into the raw food it was.

# P.5 QUICK REVISION

- Changes: **biological · physical · chemical**
- **Physical**: no new substance, **reversible** — melting, dissolving, breaking
- **Chemical**: **new substance**, irreversible — burning, **rusting**, cooking, souring
- Rusting needs **air + water** → paint, oil, grease, galvanise
- Metamorphosis: **egg → larva → pupa → adult**
- Adolescent changes are **normal** — keep clean, talk to a **trusted adult**, report abuse
