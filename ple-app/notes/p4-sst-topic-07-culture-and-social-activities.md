## 1. MEANING OF CULTURE

**What is culture?**

Culture is the way of life of a group of people.

**What does culture include?**

Culture includes the way people:

- Speak.
- Dress.
- Eat.
- Work.
- Marry.
- Worship.
- Greet one another.
- Celebrate.
- Build their homes.
- Bring up children.

**Why do people have different cultures?**

People have different cultures because they come from different communities and have different histories, beliefs and ways of life.

**Mention some examples of culture in Uganda.**

- Different languages.
- Different foods.
- Different forms of dressing.
- Traditional dances.
- Traditional songs.
- Marriage customs.
- Ways of greeting.
- Traditional ceremonies.
- Religious practices.
- Traditional beliefs.

---

## 2. IMPORTANCE OF CULTURE

**Why is culture important?**

- It gives people an identity.
- It teaches people how to live.
- It helps people know their history.
- It preserves traditions.
- It promotes unity.
- It teaches good behaviour.
- It helps people respect their heritage.

**What is cultural identity?**

Cultural identity is the feeling of belonging to a particular culture or community.

**Why should children learn their culture?**

- To know their history.
- To learn good cultural practices.
- To respect their elders.
- To preserve their culture.
- To know their community.
- To pass good traditions to future generations.

**Why should we respect other people's cultures?**

- To promote peace.
- To promote unity.
- To avoid conflicts.
- To live well with other people.
- To appreciate other people's ways of life.

## 4. LANGUAGE

**What is language?**

Language is a way people use to communicate with one another.

**Why is language important in a community?**

- It helps people communicate.
- It helps people share ideas.
- It helps people teach children.
- It helps preserve culture.
- It brings people together.

**Mention some languages spoken in Uganda.**

- Runyankole-Rukiga.
- Lusoga.
- Luo.
- Runyoro-Rutooro.
- Lugisu.
- Ateso.

We should respect other people's languages because language is an important part of their culture and identity.

**How is food part of culture?**

Food is part of culture because different communities have different foods and ways of preparing and eating food.

**Mention some traditional foods eaten in Uganda.**

- Matooke.
- Millet bread.
- Sweet potatoes.
- Cassava.
- Posho.
- Beans.
- Groundnuts.
- Fish.

Dressing is part of culture because different communities have different traditional clothes and ways of dressing.

- Kanzu.
- Bark cloth.
- Animal skins.
- Traditional beads and ornaments.

## 6. MUSIC AND DANCE

**What is traditional music?**

Traditional music is music that belongs to a particular community and is passed from one generation to another.

**What is traditional dance?**

Traditional dance is a dance performed according to the customs of a particular community.

**Why are traditional music and dances important?**

- They entertain people.
- They preserve culture.
- They teach people about their history.
- They bring people together.
- They are used during ceremonies.
- They can promote tourism.

**Mention some traditional dances in Uganda.**

- Bwola.
- Bakisimba.
- Ekitaguriro.
- Larakaraka.
- Imbalu dances.

## 7. CUSTOMS AND TRADITIONS

**What is a custom?**

A custom is a way of behaving that has been followed by a particular community for a long time.

**What is a tradition?**

A tradition is a belief or practice passed from one generation to another.

**Give examples of customs and traditions.**

- Ways of greeting.
- Marriage practices.
- Naming children.
- Traditional dances.
- Traditional ceremonies.
- Respecting elders.
- Preparing traditional foods.

**Why are customs and traditions important?**

- They preserve culture.
- They teach children how to behave.
- They unite communities.
- They help people remember their history.
- They give people a sense of identity.

Cultural values are good beliefs and principles that guide the behaviour of people in a community.

- Hard work.
- Kindness.
- Sharing.
- Cooperation.

**What are social activities?**

Social activities are activities in which people come together and interact with one another.

**Mention examples of social activities.**

- Weddings.
- Introduction ceremonies.
- Naming ceremonies.
- Burial ceremonies.
- Birthday celebrations.
- Religious celebrations.
- Cultural festivals.
- Sports.
- Community work.
- Meetings.

**Why do people take part in social activities?**

- To celebrate.
- To meet other people.
- To strengthen relationships.
- To preserve culture.
- To support one another.
- To promote unity.
- To share ideas.

## 10. MARRIAGE AND WEDDING CEREMONIES

**What is marriage?**

Marriage is the union of a man and a woman as husband and wife.

**What is a wedding?**

A wedding is a ceremony held to celebrate a marriage.

**What is an introduction ceremony?**

An introduction ceremony is a ceremony where a person introduces his or her future spouse to the family.

**Why are marriage ceremonies important?**

- They unite families.
- They bring relatives together.
- They strengthen relationships.
- They celebrate the joining of a couple.
- They help communities follow their customs.

---

## 11. NAMING CEREMONIES

**What is a naming ceremony?**

A naming ceremony is a ceremony held when a child is given a name.

**Why are naming ceremonies important?**

- They give a child a name.
- They bring families together.
- They introduce the child to the community.
- They preserve cultural practices.

**Who may take part in a naming ceremony?**

- Parents.
- Relatives.
- Friends.
- Religious leaders.
- Elders.
- Other members of the community.

---

## 12. BURIAL CEREMONIES

**What is a burial ceremony?**

A burial ceremony is an activity carried out when a dead person is buried.

**Why are burial ceremonies important?**

- They give people an opportunity to mourn.
- They bring relatives together.
- They allow people to support the bereaved family.
- They help communities show respect for the dead.

**How should children behave during burial ceremonies?**

- They should respect the bereaved family.
- They should behave quietly.
- They should follow instructions.
- They should avoid playing around the burial place.
- They should show respect to the dead.

**Mention examples of religious activities.**

- Religious celebrations.

- Giving offerings or charity.

**Why are religious activities important?**

- They teach good behaviour.
- They strengthen faith.
- They encourage people to help others.
- They promote unity among believers.

- They bring communities together.

## 17. IMPORTANCE OF SOCIAL ACTIVITIES

**Why are social activities important?**

- They bring people together.
- They promote unity.
- They strengthen relationships.
- They preserve culture.
- They provide entertainment.
- They help people support one another.
- They promote cooperation.
- They help people share ideas.
- They teach children good behaviour.

**How do social activities promote unity?**

They bring people together to celebrate, work, share ideas and support one another.

**How do social activities preserve culture?**

They help people pass their customs, traditions, songs, dances, language and other cultural practices from one generation to another.

**How do social activities promote cooperation?**

People work together to organise and take part in activities.

## 18. GOOD AND BAD CULTURAL PRACTICES

Not every cultural practice is good. Some practices can harm people.

**What are good cultural practices?**

Good cultural practices are cultural practices that help people live healthy, peaceful and responsible lives.

**Mention examples of good cultural practices.**

- Respecting elders.
- Helping the needy.
- Caring for children.
- Sharing with others.
- Welcoming visitors.
- Working together.
- Respecting other people.

**What are harmful cultural practices?**

Harmful cultural practices are practices that can hurt people or prevent them from living healthy and peaceful lives.

**Mention examples of harmful cultural practices.**

- Child marriage.
- Female genital mutilation.
- Denying children education.
- Discrimination.
- Violence.
- Some harmful traditional beliefs.

**Why should harmful cultural practices be stopped?**

- They can hurt people.
- They can cause diseases.
- They can deny children their rights.
- They can cause suffering.
- They can prevent community development.

**What should we do about good cultural practices?**

We should preserve and pass good cultural practices to future generations.

**What should we do about harmful cultural practices?**

We should discourage and stop harmful cultural practices.

---

## 19. CULTURE AND CHANGE

Culture can change with time.

**Why does culture change?**

Culture changes because people learn new ideas and come into contact with other communities.

**Mention factors that can cause cultural change.**

- Education.
- Technology.
- Religion.
- Migration.
- Trade.
- Intermarriage.
- Communication.
- Contact with other communities.

**How has technology changed people's culture?**

Technology has changed the way people communicate, work, learn, travel and entertain themselves.

**How does education change culture?**

Education gives people new knowledge and ideas that may change some of their ways of living.

**How does migration change culture?**

When people move to new places, they share their language, food, dressing, beliefs and other cultural practices with other people.

## 20. RESPECTING DIFFERENT CULTURES

**Why should we respect people from different cultures?**

- To promote peace.
- To promote unity.
- To avoid discrimination.
- To live well with others.
- To learn from one another.

**How can we show respect for other cultures?**

- Respecting other people's languages.
- Respecting their customs.
- Respecting their beliefs.
- Avoiding insulting other cultures.
- Learning about other cultures.
- Living peacefully with people from different communities.

**What is discrimination?**

Discrimination is treating a person unfairly because of differences such as culture, tribe, religion or other characteristics.

**Why should we avoid discrimination?**

We should avoid discrimination because it causes hatred, conflicts and disunity.

## 21. CULTURE AND NATIONAL UNITY

**How can culture promote national unity?**

- It brings people together.
- It helps people appreciate one another.
- It encourages respect for different cultures.
- It promotes peaceful living.
- It helps people share cultural experiences.

**Why is cultural diversity important in Uganda?**

Cultural diversity gives Uganda different languages, foods, dances, music, customs and traditions.

**What should Ugandans do despite having different cultures?**

Ugandans should respect one another, cooperate, live peacefully and work together for the development of Uganda.
