## 1. What is a resource?

**Resource:** A resource is anything that people use to satisfy their needs.

**Natural resources:** Natural resources are things provided by nature that people use
to satisfy their needs. Examples of natural resources found in East Africa include
land, water, forests, wildlife, minerals, fish and soils.

---

## 2. Why is land an important resource?

Land is important because people use it for farming, settlement, mining, transport,
industries and other activities. Land is used for crop growing, keeping animals,
settlement, mining, building industries, transport, tourism and forestry.

Examples of crops grown on land in East Africa include maize, beans, bananas, coffee,
tea and cotton.

---

## 3. Why is water an important resource?

Water is important because people, animals and plants need it for survival and many
other activities.

Sources of water in East Africa include lakes, rivers, springs, wells, rainfall and
oceans. Water is used for drinking, cooking, washing, irrigation, fishing, transport,
producing electricity and industrial activities.

Major lakes in East Africa include Lake Victoria, Lake Kyoga, Lake Albert, Lake
Edward, Lake Tanganyika and Lake Turkana. Major rivers include the River Nile, River
Tana, River Athi and River Rufiji.

---

## 4. Why are forests important resources?

Forests provide useful products and help to protect the environment. People get
products such as timber, firewood, charcoal, fruits, medicines and poles from forests.

Forests protect soil from erosion, provide homes for wild animals, help to maintain
rainfall, provide fresh air and help to conserve the environment.

**Forestry** is the growing, caring for and using of trees and forests.

---

## 5. Why is wildlife an important resource?

**Wildlife** refers to animals and plants that live in their natural environment.
Wildlife is important because it provides income through tourism and provides
employment to some people.

Examples of wild animals found in East Africa include lions, elephants, giraffes,
zebras, buffaloes, rhinoceroses and leopards. Many wild animals are protected in game
parks and game reserves.

**Tourism** is the activity of travelling to places for leisure, learning or other
purposes. Wild animals attract tourists who visit East Africa to see and enjoy them.

Wildlife benefits East African countries because it brings income through tourism,
provides employment, attracts visitors from other countries, provides foreign exchange
and promotes conservation.

---

## 6. What are minerals?

**Minerals** are useful substances found in or under the ground. Examples of minerals
found in East Africa include gold, copper, iron ore, limestone, salt, tin and diamond.

Minerals are important because they provide raw materials for industries and income
through mining and trade. **Mining** is the activity of removing minerals from the
ground.

Mining provides employment, provides income, provides raw materials for industries and
provides minerals for export. Poor mining practices can destroy land, pollute water,
destroy vegetation and leave open pits.

---

## 7. Why is soil an important resource?

Soil is important because it supports the growth of crops and natural vegetation.
Activities that depend on soil include crop growing, livestock keeping, forestry and
settlement. Fertile soil helps crops to grow well and produce good yields.

Loss of soil fertility can be caused by continuous cultivation, soil erosion,
overgrazing, bush burning and poor farming methods. Soil can be conserved by planting
trees, practising crop rotation, using manure, controlling soil erosion and avoiding
overgrazing.

---

## 8. Why is fish an important resource?

Fish provides food and income to many people. Fishing is mainly carried out in lakes,
rivers and the ocean. Important fishing areas in East Africa include Lake Victoria,
Lake Kyoga, Lake Albert, Lake Tanganyika and the Indian Ocean.

Fishing provides food, provides employment, provides income and provides fish for
trade. Fishing can be affected by overfishing, water pollution, destruction of
breeding areas and the use of illegal fishing methods.

---

## 9. Why is climate important to people?

Climate affects activities such as farming, livestock keeping and tourism. Rainfall
and suitable temperatures provide conditions needed for crops to grow. Favourable
weather can attract tourists to visit different places.

---

## 10. What are human resources?

**Human resources** are people and their skills, knowledge and abilities that are used
to produce goods and services. People use their knowledge, skills and abilities to
carry out different activities and develop their communities and countries.

Examples of people who provide important skills and services include teachers,
doctors, nurses, farmers, engineers, drivers, builders and traders. People can improve
their skills through education, training and practice.

---

## 11. Why are natural resources important to East Africa?

Natural resources are important to East African countries because they provide food,
provide raw materials, provide employment, provide income, promote trade, promote
tourism, support industries and provide government revenue.

People get jobs in activities such as farming, fishing, mining, forestry and tourism.
People and countries buy and sell products obtained from resources such as crops,
fish, minerals and forest products. Resources provide the raw materials needed by
industries.

---

## 12. What is conservation of resources?

**Conservation** is the proper use and protection of resources. Resources should be
conserved so that they can continue benefiting people now and in the future.

Land can be conserved by planting trees, controlling soil erosion and avoiding
overgrazing. Forests can be conserved by planting trees, controlling unnecessary
cutting of trees, preventing forest fires and protecting forests. Wildlife can be
conserved by protecting game parks and reserves, preventing poaching, protecting
animal habitats and enforcing wildlife laws.

Water can be conserved by preventing water pollution, protecting water sources and
avoiding unnecessary wastage of water. Fish can be conserved by controlling
overfishing, using legal fishing methods, protecting fish breeding areas and
preventing water pollution. Minerals can be conserved by using them carefully and
controlling mining activities to reduce environmental damage.

---

## 13. What happens when resources are misused?

Misuse of resources can lead to environmental destruction, soil erosion, loss of
forests, loss of wildlife, water pollution, loss of fertile soil and shortage of
resources.

**Environmental destruction** is the damage caused to the natural environment by human
activities. People should avoid destroying natural resources because they depend on
them for food, income, employment and other needs.

## 14. MAP WORK

A P.6 learner should be able to locate important areas where major resources are found
and used. The major water bodies a learner should practise locating are Lake Victoria,
Lake Kyoga, Lake Albert, Lake Edward, Lake Tanganyika, Lake Turkana and the Indian
Ocean. The Indian Ocean borders East Africa, and the major river a learner should be
able to locate is the River Nile.

## 16. IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

The major resources of East Africa are land, water, forests, wildlife, minerals, soil,
fish, climate and human resources. Resources are important because they provide food,
employment, income, raw materials and support economic development. Resources should be
conserved so that they can continue benefiting people now and in the future.
