# TOPIC 2: PHYSICAL FEATURES IN OUR DISTRICT

*Curriculum reference: NCDC Primary Four Social Studies Syllabus, Term I, Topic 2 (20 periods, pages 20–23).*

**How to use this topic.** The syllabus asks about the physical features of **your own
district**. These notes teach you what each feature is, what it is used for and how to care
for it. You then name the ones found in your district.

**By the end of this topic you should be able to:**

- say what physical features are
- name the physical features found in your district and locate them on a map
- explain the uses of physical features
- explain the dangers of some physical features
- suggest ways of caring for physical features

---

## 1. What are physical features?

**Physical features** are the **natural things on the earth's surface** — things that were
not made by people.

| Physical features (natural) | Made by people (not physical features) |
|---|---|
| Hills, mountains, valleys, plains | Houses, roads, bridges |
| Rivers, lakes, swamps | Dams, canals, fish ponds |
| Forests, rocks, islands | Schools, factories, markets |

> A **dam** is listed in the syllabus with lakes because a dam holds water like a lake — but
> remember a dam is **built by people**, while a lake is natural.

## 2. Name the physical features in the syllabus and what each one is.

| Feature | What it is |
|---|---|
| **River** | Water flowing in a channel from a highland to a lake or sea |
| **Lake** | A large body of water surrounded by land |
| **Dam** | A wall built across a river to hold back water |
| **Hill** | Raised land, lower than a mountain |
| **Mountain** | Very high raised land, often with a rocky top |
| **Valley** | Low land lying between hills or mountains |
| **Rift valley** | A very long, deep, wide valley formed when the land sank between cracks |
| **Plain** | Flat or gently sloping land |

**Others you may see in your district:** swamps, forests, islands, waterfalls, rocks and
springs.

## 3. What is a swamp?

A swamp is **wet land covered with water and water plants**.

---

## 4. What are physical features used for?

Physical features are **natural resources which people use to earn a living**.

| Feature | How people use it |
|---|---|
| **Rivers and lakes** | Fishing · water for drinking, washing and animals · transport by boat · irrigation · tourism |
| **Dams** | Storing water · making electricity · watering animals |
| **Hills and mountains** | Quarrying stones · growing crops on the slopes · tourism and climbing · rain formation |
| **Valleys** | Growing crops such as rice · grazing animals · settlement |
| **Plains** | Farming · grazing cattle · building homes, roads and towns |
| **Swamps** | Papyrus for mats and baskets · clay for bricks and pots · growing yams and rice · home for birds and fish |
| **Forests** | Timber · firewood · herbs for medicine · homes for animals · rainfall |

---

## 5. What dangers come from physical features?

Physical features are useful, but they can also cause harm.

| Feature | Danger |
|---|---|
| **Rivers and lakes** | **Drowning** · floods · dangerous animals such as crocodiles and hippos · water-borne diseases such as bilharzia and cholera |
| **Mountains and steep hills** | **Landslides** · falling rocks · soil erosion |
| **Valleys and low plains** | Flooding after heavy rain · waterlogging |
| **Swamps** | Mosquitoes that spread **malaria** · people can sink and drown |
| **Forests** | Dangerous animals and snakes · people get lost · bush fires |

---

## 6. How can we keep safe near physical features?

- Do not swim in a lake or river without a grown-up who can swim.
- Do not build a house at the bottom of a very steep slope.
- Sleep under a **treated mosquito net** if you live near a swamp.
- Do not enter a forest alone.
- **If you see someone in danger, call an adult at once. Do not jump into the water
  yourself.**

---

## 7. How do we care for physical features?

| Feature | How to care for it |
|---|---|
| **Rivers and lakes** | Do not throw rubbish in the water · do not wash cars or bathe in the source · leave plants growing on the banks · do not fish with poison or small nets |
| **Wells and springs** | Fence them · keep animals away · keep the surroundings clean |
| **Hills and mountains** | Plant trees and grass · dig **terraces** and trenches across the slope · do not cut all the trees |
| **Swamps** | Do not drain the whole swamp · harvest papyrus carefully and let it grow again |
| **Forests** | Plant more trees · cut only mature trees · avoid bush burning |
| **Soil and land** | Practise **crop rotation** and mulching · avoid over-grazing |

---

## 8. Words to know

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Conservation** | Protecting something so that it lasts |
| **Afforestation** | Planting trees where there were none |
| **Reafforestation** | Planting trees again where they were cut |
| **Deforestation** | Cutting down trees and not replacing them |
| **Soil erosion** | The carrying away of soil by water or wind |
| **Terracing** | Cutting steps across a slope to stop the soil washing away |

---

## 9. How do physical features affect people?

- People **settle** where there is water and good flat land, so plains and lake shores are
  crowded.
- People **avoid** very steep slopes, very dry places and big swamps, so these have few
  people.
- Physical features decide the **work** people do — fishing at a lake, farming on a plain,
  quarrying on a hill.
- Mountains **help rain to form**, so their slopes are usually green and good for crops.
- Rivers and lakes can be **boundaries** between districts.

---

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Calling a road, a bridge or a building a physical feature.
- Saying a hill and a mountain are the same — a **mountain is much higher**.
- Giving a **use** when the question asks for a **danger**.
- Writing "cutting trees" as a way of **caring** for a forest.
- Mixing up **afforestation** (planting new) with **reafforestation** (planting again).
- Naming a feature that is not in your own district.
