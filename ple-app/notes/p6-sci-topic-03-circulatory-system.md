## 1. BLOOD CIRCULATION

**Circulatory system:** The system that carries blood round the body. It is made up of the **heart, the blood vessels and the blood**.

**Circulation** means the movement of blood round the body, pumped by the heart.

### The two circuits

| Circuit | Path |
|---|---|
| **Pulmonary circulation** | Heart → **lungs** → heart (to pick up oxygen) |
| **Systemic circulation** | Heart → **the rest of the body** → heart (to deliver oxygen and food) |

**Simple path:** body → **right atrium** → right ventricle → lungs → **left atrium** →
left ventricle → body.

---

## 2. THE HEART

**Heart:** The muscular organ that pumps blood round the body. It is about the size of your closed fist and lies in the chest, slightly to the left, protected by the ribs.

### Structure

| Part | Work |
|---|---|
| **Right atrium (auricle)** | Receives blood **without oxygen** from the body |
| **Right ventricle** | Pumps that blood to the **lungs** |
| **Left atrium (auricle)** | Receives blood **with oxygen** from the lungs |
| **Left ventricle** | Pumps oxygen-rich blood to the whole body; it has the **thickest wall** |
| **Valves** | Stop blood from flowing backwards |
| **Septum** | The wall separating the right side from the left side |
| **Cardiac muscle** | The special heart muscle that never tires |

**Note:** the **left ventricle** has the thickest wall because it pushes blood all round
the body.

---

## 3. BLOOD VESSELS

| Vessel | What it does | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Arteries** | Carry blood **away from** the heart | Thick, elastic walls; carry blood at high pressure; carry oxygenated blood (except the pulmonary artery) |
| **Veins** | Carry blood **back to** the heart | Thinner walls, wider space inside, have **valves**; carry deoxygenated blood (except the pulmonary vein) |
| **Capillaries** | Join the smallest arteries to the smallest veins | Very thin — one cell thick — so food and oxygen pass through into the cells |

**The two exceptions to learn:** the **pulmonary artery** carries blood **without** oxygen
to the lungs; the **pulmonary vein** carries blood **with** oxygen from the lungs.

---

## 4. COMPOSITION AND FUNCTIONS OF BLOOD

| Part of blood | Description | Work |
|---|---|---|
| **Plasma** | The pale yellow liquid part (about 55%) | Carries food, water, salts, hormones and waste |
| **Red blood cells** | Round cells containing **haemoglobin**, made in the bone marrow | Carry **oxygen** from the lungs to the body |
| **White blood cells** | Larger, fewer cells | **Fight germs** and protect the body from disease |
| **Platelets** | Tiny fragments | Help the blood to **clot** and stop bleeding |

### Functions of blood

- **Transport** — oxygen, food, water, hormones and waste.
- **Protection** — white blood cells fight germs; platelets clot the blood.
- **Regulation** — spreading heat and keeping the body temperature even.

**Blood groups:** A, B, AB and O. A person with group **O negative** can donate to nearly
everybody, and a person with **AB** can receive from nearly everybody. Blood for
transfusion must always be **tested and matched** first.

---

## 5. DISEASES AND DISORDERS OF THE SYSTEM

| Condition | Cause / sign |
|---|---|
| **Anaemia** | Too few red blood cells or too little iron; tiredness, pale eyes and palms |
| **Sickle cell disease** | Inherited; red cells become sickle-shaped, causing pain and anaemia |
| **Leukaemia** | Cancer of the white blood cells |
| **High blood pressure (hypertension)** | Blood pushes too hard on the artery walls; linked to too much salt, obesity and stress |
| **Heart attack** | Blood supply to the heart muscle is blocked |
| **Haemophilia** | Inherited; the blood fails to clot properly |
| **Varicose veins** | Veins swell when their valves fail |

### HIV/AIDS and blood

HIV is found in **blood** and other body fluids. It can be passed on through:

- transfusion of **untested blood**;
- **sharing** needles, syringes, razor blades or other skin-cutting instruments;
- from mother to child during pregnancy, birth or breastfeeding;
- unprotected sexual contact.

**Prevention:** only **screened blood** is used for transfusion · use **sterile, single-use**
needles and razor blades · never share sharp instruments · mothers who are HIV positive
follow medical advice to protect the baby · abstain from sex at your age and avoid all
risky situations · anybody who is HIV positive can live long and well on **ARV medicine**,
and should never be discriminated against.

### Increasing the volume of blood in circulation

Eat **iron-rich foods** — dodo, nakati, spinach, liver, beans, groundnuts, meat and eggs ·
eat fruits with **vitamin C** (oranges, mangoes) so the body absorbs the iron · **deworm**
regularly · sleep under a **mosquito net** to avoid malaria · treat bleeding wounds quickly ·
take **exercise** · rest well · **blood transfusion** in serious cases, using screened
blood.

# KEY DEFINITIONS

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Circulatory system | The system that carries blood round the body |
| Heart | The muscular organ that pumps blood |
| Artery | A vessel carrying blood away from the heart |
| Vein | A vessel carrying blood back to the heart |
| Capillary | The tiniest blood vessel, one cell thick |
| Plasma | The liquid part of blood |
| Haemoglobin | The substance in red blood cells that carries oxygen |
| Platelets | Parts of blood that help it to clot |
| Anaemia | A shortage of red blood cells or iron |
| Transfusion | Giving a person blood from another person |

---

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- The circulatory system = **heart + blood vessels + blood**.
- The heart has **four chambers**: two atria and two ventricles.
- The **left ventricle** has the thickest wall.
- **Arteries carry blood away** from the heart; **veins carry it back**; **veins have valves**.
- The **pulmonary artery** carries blood without oxygen; the **pulmonary vein** carries blood with oxygen.
- **Red cells carry oxygen; white cells fight germs; platelets clot the blood; plasma transports.**
- Blood groups: **A, B, AB and O**.
- **Anaemia** comes from too little iron or too few red cells; eat dodo, liver and beans.
- Never share **razor blades, needles or syringes**.

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- Organ that pumps blood → the heart.
- Chamber with the thickest wall → the left ventricle.
- Vessel that carries blood away from the heart → an artery.
- Vessels with valves → veins.
- Cells that fight germs → white blood cells.
- Part of blood that helps clotting → platelets.
- Substance in red cells that carries oxygen → haemoglobin.
- Disease caused by lack of iron → anaemia.
- One way HIV is spread through blood → sharing needles or razor blades / untested transfusion.

# ANSWERS TO REVISION QUESTIONS

1. The **heart**, the **blood vessels** and the **blood**.
2. In the **chest**, slightly to the left, protected by the **ribs** (and the breastbone).
3. **Right atrium** — receives blood without oxygen from the body; **right ventricle** — pumps it to the lungs; **left atrium** — receives blood with oxygen from the lungs; **left ventricle** — pumps it to the whole body.
4. Because it pumps blood to **the whole body**, which needs much greater force than pumping to the lungs.
5. They **stop blood from flowing backwards**.
6. **Arteries, veins and capillaries.** Arteries carry blood **away** from the heart with thick elastic walls at high pressure, while veins carry blood **back** to the heart, have thinner walls and contain **valves**.
7. The **pulmonary artery** carries blood without oxygen; the **pulmonary vein** carries blood with oxygen.
8. **Plasma** — carries food, water, salts and waste; **red blood cells** — carry oxygen; **white blood cells** — fight germs; **platelets** — help the blood to clot.
9. **A, B, AB and O.**
10. Any four of: transporting oxygen; transporting food and water; carrying waste to the excretory organs; fighting germs; clotting to stop bleeding; spreading heat round the body.
11. Any four of: anaemia, sickle cell disease, leukaemia, high blood pressure, heart attack, haemophilia, varicose veins.
12. **Spread:** transfusion of untested blood; sharing needles or syringes; sharing razor blades or other skin-cutting instruments (also mother to child). **Prevention:** use only screened blood; use sterile single-use needles; never share razor blades or sharp instruments (and mothers who are HIV positive should follow medical advice).
13. Any five of: eat iron-rich foods such as dodo, nakati, liver, beans and meat; eat fruits rich in vitamin C; deworm regularly; sleep under a mosquito net; treat wounds and bleeding quickly; exercise; rest well; have a transfusion of screened blood in serious cases.
14. To make sure it is the **right blood group** for the patient and that it is **free from germs and infections such as HIV, hepatitis and syphilis**, so that the transfusion helps instead of harming.
15. Body → **right atrium** → right ventricle → **lungs** (to get oxygen) → **left atrium** → left ventricle → back to the body.
