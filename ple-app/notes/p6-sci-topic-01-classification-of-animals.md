## 1. WHAT CLASSIFICATION IS

**Classification:** The grouping of living things according to their similarities and differences.

**Why we classify:** it makes study easier · it helps us identify animals · it shows how
animals are related · it helps in caring for them.

| Group | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Vertebrates** | Animals **with a backbone** |
| **Invertebrates** | Animals **without a backbone** |

---

## 2. VERTEBRATES

Vertebrates are divided into **five classes**. Two are **warm-blooded** (their body
temperature stays the same) and three are **cold-blooded** (their body temperature changes
with the surroundings).

### Warm-blooded vertebrates

| Class | Characteristics | Reproduction | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mammals** | Body covered with **hair or fur**; have **mammary glands** to feed young with milk; breathe with lungs; have external ears | Give **birth to live young** (except the platypus and echidna, which lay eggs) | Cow, goat, human being, bat, whale, dog |
| **Birds** | Body covered with **feathers**; have a **beak** and **two wings**; two legs with scales; light hollow bones | **Lay eggs** with hard shells and incubate them | Hen, eagle, ostrich, duck, crow |

### Cold-blooded vertebrates

| Class | Characteristics | Reproduction | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reptiles** | Dry skin covered with **scales**; most crawl; breathe with lungs | **Lay eggs** with leathery shells; do not incubate them | Snake, lizard, crocodile, tortoise, chameleon |
| **Fish** | Live in water; body covered with **scales**; breathe using **gills**; have **fins** and a tail for swimming | Lay very many eggs in water | Tilapia, Nile perch, catfish, mudfish |
| **Amphibians** | Live **both in water and on land**; moist smooth skin; young breathe with gills, adults with lungs and skin | Lay eggs in water; go through **metamorphosis** (egg → tadpole → adult) | Frog, toad, newt, salamander |

**Special cases to remember:** a **bat** is a mammal that flies; a **whale and a dolphin**
are mammals that live in water; a **penguin and an ostrich** are birds that cannot fly.

---

## 3. INVERTEBRATES (NON-VERTEBRATES)

| Group | Characteristics | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Arthropods** | Jointed legs; a hard outer covering (**exoskeleton**); the body is in parts | See the four sub-groups below |
| **Molluscs** | Soft body, often with a shell; move on a muscular foot | Snail, slug, oyster |
| **Worms** | Long soft body with no legs | Earthworm, tapeworm, roundworm, hookworm |

### The four arthropod groups

| Group | Legs | Body parts | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Insects** | **6** | 3 (head, thorax, abdomen) | Housefly, bee, mosquito, grasshopper, butterfly |
| **Arachnids** | **8** | 2 (cephalothorax and abdomen) | Spider, scorpion, tick, mite |
| **Myriapods** | Many | Many segments | Millipede (2 pairs per segment), centipede (1 pair per segment) |
| **Crustaceans** | 10 or more | 2 | Crab, crayfish, prawn, lobster |

**Note:** a **spider is not an insect** — it has 8 legs and 2 body parts.

---

## 4. CARE FOR AND PROTECTION OF ANIMALS

- Give them **enough clean food and water**.
- Provide **clean, dry shelter**.
- **Treat and vaccinate** them against disease.
- Handle them **gently** — no beating or overloading.
- Protect them from **thieves, wild animals and bad weather**.
- Do not **poach**; protect wild animals in game parks and sanctuaries.
- Keep their **habitats** — forests, wetlands and lakes — undestroyed.
- Report **cruelty and poaching** to the authorities.

**Importance of animals:** food (meat, milk, eggs, honey) · hides and skins · manure ·
transport and work · money and employment · tourism · pest control (birds and frogs eat
insects) · pollination by bees and butterflies · company from pets.

# KEY DEFINITIONS

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Classification | Grouping living things according to similarities and differences |
| Vertebrate | An animal with a backbone |
| Invertebrate | An animal without a backbone |
| Warm-blooded | Having a body temperature that stays the same |
| Cold-blooded | Having a body temperature that changes with the surroundings |
| Mammary glands | The glands that produce milk in mammals |
| Exoskeleton | A hard outer covering of arthropods |
| Metamorphosis | The change of form as an animal grows, as from tadpole to frog |
| Gills | The organs fish use to breathe in water |

---

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- **Vertebrates have a backbone; invertebrates do not.**
- Five classes of vertebrates: **mammals, birds, reptiles, fish, amphibians**.
- **Mammals and birds are warm-blooded**; reptiles, fish and amphibians are cold-blooded.
- Mammals have **hair or fur and mammary glands**; birds have **feathers and a beak**.
- Fish breathe using **gills**; amphibians live on land and in water.
- **Insects have 6 legs and 3 body parts; arachnids have 8 legs and 2 body parts.**
- A **millipede** has two pairs of legs per segment; a **centipede** has one pair.
- A bat is a **mammal**; a whale is a **mammal**; a spider is **not** an insect.

---

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- Animals with a backbone → vertebrates.
- The only mammal that flies → the bat.
- Class of animals with feathers → birds.
- Class that lives both in water and on land → amphibians.
- Number of legs of an arachnid → 8.
- Group to which a snail belongs → molluscs.
- Organ fish use for breathing → gills.
- Change from tadpole to frog → metamorphosis.

# ANSWERS TO REVISION QUESTIONS

1. Classification is the grouping of living things according to their similarities and differences. Reasons: it makes study easier and it helps us identify animals and know how to care for them.
2. **Vertebrates** have a backbone; **invertebrates** do not.
3. Mammals — cow; birds — hen; reptiles — lizard; fish — tilapia; amphibians — frog.
4. **Mammals and birds.**
5. Any four of: body covered with hair or fur; have mammary glands and feed young on milk; give birth to live young; breathe using lungs; are warm-blooded; have external ears.
6. Any four of: body covered with feathers; have a beak; have two wings and two legs; lay eggs with hard shells; are warm-blooded; have light hollow bones.
7. **Reptiles:** dry scaly skin, live on land (or water) and breathe with lungs all their life, lay leathery eggs on land. **Amphibians:** moist smooth skin, live both in water and on land, young breathe with gills and adults with lungs and skin, lay eggs in water. (Any three pairs.)
8. Fish breathe using **gills**, and they move using **fins and a tail**.
9. Arthropods, molluscs and worms.
10. Insects — housefly; arachnids — spider; myriapods — millipede; crustaceans — crab.
11. Insects have **6 legs**, arachnids have **8**; insects have **3 body parts**, arachnids **2**; most insects have wings and antennae, arachnids have neither.
12. A **millipede** has **two pairs** of legs on each body segment and rolls up when touched; a **centipede** has **one pair** per segment and is a fast-moving hunter with poison claws.
13. Because it has **hair on its body, mammary glands and gives birth to live young which it feeds on milk** — even though it flies, it has no feathers or beak.
14. Any five of: give clean food and water; provide clean dry shelter; treat and vaccinate them; handle them gently; protect them from thieves and wild animals; do not poach; protect their habitats.
15. Any five of: food such as meat, milk, eggs and honey; hides and skins; manure; transport and work; money and employment; tourism; pest control; pollination; company from pets.
