## 1. UNITS AND CONVERSIONS

### Length

| Unit | Equal to |
|---|---|
| 1 centimetre (cm) | 10 millimetres (mm) |
| 1 metre (m) | 100 cm |
| 1 kilometre (km) | 1,000 m |

### Mass

| Unit | Equal to |
|---|---|
| 1 gramme (g) | 1,000 milligrammes (mg) |
| 1 kilogramme (kg) | 1,000 g |
| 1 tonne | 1,000 kg |

### Capacity and volume

| Unit | Equal to |
|---|---|
| 1 litre (l) | 1,000 millilitres (ml) |
| 1 litre | **1,000 cubic centimetres (cm³)** |
| 1 cubic metre (m³) | 1,000 litres |

**Remember:** to change to a smaller unit, **multiply**; to change to a bigger unit, **divide**.

*Examples:* 3.5 km = 3,500 m · 250 cm = 2.5 m · 4,500 g = 4.5 kg · 2.75 litres = 2,750 ml

## 2. PERIMETER AND CIRCUMFERENCE

### Definitions

**Perimeter:** The total distance round the outside of a shape.

**Circumference:** The perimeter of a circle.

| Shape | Perimeter |
|---|---|
| Square | 4 × side |
| Rectangle | 2 × (length + width) |
| Triangle | Add the three sides |
| Circle | **πd** or **2πr** (use π = 22/7) |

### Worked examples

1. *A rectangle is 12 m by 7 m. Find its perimeter.*
   2 × (12 + 7) = 2 × 19 = **38 m**

2. *A circle has a radius of 7 cm. Find its circumference.*
   2 × 22/7 × 7 = **44 cm**

3. *The circumference of a circle is 88 cm. Find its radius.*
   2 × 22/7 × r = 88 → 44r/7 = 88 → r = 88 × 7 ÷ 44 = **14 cm**

## 3. AREA

### Definition

**Area:** The amount of surface a shape covers. It is measured in **square units** (cm², m², km²).

| Shape | Area formula |
|---|---|
| Square | side × side |
| Rectangle | length × width |
| Triangle | ½ × base × height |
| Parallelogram | base × height |
| Trapezium | ½ × (a + b) × height |
| Circle | **πr²** |

### Worked examples

1. *Find the area of a triangle with base 12 cm and height 9 cm.*
   ½ × 12 × 9 = **54 cm²**

2. *Find the area of a circle of radius 7 cm.*
   22/7 × 7 × 7 = **154 cm²**

3. *A trapezium has parallel sides 10 cm and 6 cm and a height of 5 cm.*
   ½ × (10 + 6) × 5 = ½ × 16 × 5 = **40 cm²**

4. *A rectangular plot is 40 m by 25 m. Find its area in square metres.*
   40 × 25 = **1,000 m²**

5. *A path 2 m wide runs round the outside of a garden 20 m by 15 m. Find the area of the path.*
   Outer rectangle = (20 + 4) × (15 + 4) = 24 × 19 = 456 m²
   Garden = 20 × 15 = 300 m²
   Path = 456 − 300 = **156 m²**

---

## 4. VOLUME

### Definition

**Volume:** The amount of space a solid takes up. It is measured in **cubic units** (cm³, m³).

| Solid | Volume formula |
|---|---|
| Cube | side × side × side |
| Cuboid | length × width × height |
| Cylinder | **πr²h** |
| Any prism | area of cross-section × length |

### Worked examples

1. *A tank is 2 m long, 1.5 m wide and 1 m high. Find its volume.*
   2 × 1.5 × 1 = **3 m³**

2. *A cylinder has radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. Find its volume.*
   22/7 × 7 × 7 × 10 = **1,540 cm³**

3. *A cube has a volume of 64 cm³. Find the length of one edge.*
   4 × 4 × 4 = 64, so the edge is **4 cm**

---

## 5. CAPACITY

### Definition

**Capacity:** The amount of liquid a container can hold.

**Volume** is the space a solid occupies; **capacity** is how much a container holds. They are linked by **1 litre = 1,000 cm³**.

### Worked examples

1. *How many litres does the cylinder in the example above hold?*
   1,540 cm³ ÷ 1,000 = **1.54 litres**

2. *A tank measures 100 cm by 50 cm by 40 cm. Find its capacity in litres.*
   Volume = 100 × 50 × 40 = 200,000 cm³ → 200,000 ÷ 1,000 = **200 litres**

3. *A jerrycan holds 20 litres. How many full jerrycans can be filled from a tank of 3 m³?*
   3 m³ = 3,000 litres → 3,000 ÷ 20 = **150 jerrycans**

# KEY DEFINITIONS

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Perimeter | The distance round a shape |
| Circumference | The perimeter of a circle |
| Radius | The distance from the centre of a circle to the edge |
| Diameter | The distance across a circle through the centre (2 × radius) |
| Area | The surface covered by a shape, in square units |
| Volume | The space taken up by a solid, in cubic units |
| Capacity | The amount a container can hold |
| Mass | The amount of matter in an object |
| Pi (π) | The number 22/7 (about 3.14) used with circles |

---

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- 1 m = 100 cm · 1 km = 1,000 m · 1 kg = 1,000 g · 1 tonne = 1,000 kg
- **1 litre = 1,000 cm³** and **1 m³ = 1,000 litres**
- Circumference = πd = 2πr · Area of a circle = πr²
- Area of a triangle = ½ × base × height
- Volume of a cylinder = πr²h
- Use **π = 22/7** when the radius is a multiple of 7.
- Area is in **square** units; volume is in **cubic** units — always write the unit.

---

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- Radius 7 cm → circumference 44 cm and area 154 cm².
- Cylinder r = 7 cm, h = 10 cm → 1,540 cm³ = 1.54 litres.
- Tank 100 × 50 × 40 cm → 200 litres.
- Area of a path = outer area − inner area.
- Diameter = 2 × radius; if the diameter is given, halve it first.

---

# COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Using the diameter in πr² instead of the radius.
- Forgetting the ½ in the area of a triangle.
- Writing cm instead of cm² for area, or cm² instead of cm³ for volume.
- Dividing by 100 instead of 1,000 when changing cm³ to litres.
- Adding only two sides when finding the perimeter of a rectangle.

# REVISION QUESTIONS

1. Change 4.2 km to metres.
2. Change 3,750 g to kilogrammes.
3. Change 5.5 litres to millilitres.
4. Find the perimeter of a rectangle 15 cm by 9 cm.
5. Find the circumference of a circle of radius 14 cm. (π = 22/7)
6. The circumference of a circle is 132 cm. Find its radius.
7. Find the area of a rectangle 18 m by 12 m.
8. Find the area of a triangle with base 16 cm and height 7 cm.
9. Find the area of a circle of diameter 28 cm.
10. Find the area of a trapezium whose parallel sides are 12 cm and 8 cm and whose height is 6 cm.
11. Find the volume of a cuboid 8 cm by 5 cm by 4 cm.
12. Find the volume of a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 20 cm.
13. A tank measures 2 m by 1 m by 0.5 m. Find its capacity in litres.
14. A rectangular garden 30 m by 20 m has a path 1 m wide all round it inside the garden. Find the area of the path.
15. How many 500 ml bottles can be filled from 12 litres of juice?

---

# ANSWERS TO REVISION QUESTIONS

1. 4.2 × 1,000 = **4,200 m**
2. 3,750 ÷ 1,000 = **3.75 kg**
3. 5.5 × 1,000 = **5,500 ml**
4. 2 × (15 + 9) = **48 cm**
5. 2 × 22/7 × 14 = **88 cm**
6. 2 × 22/7 × r = 132 → r = 132 × 7 ÷ 44 = **21 cm**
7. 18 × 12 = **216 m²**
8. ½ × 16 × 7 = **56 cm²**
9. Radius = 14 cm. Area = 22/7 × 14 × 14 = **616 cm²**
10. ½ × (12 + 8) × 6 = ½ × 20 × 6 = **60 cm²**
11. 8 × 5 × 4 = **160 cm³**
12. 22/7 × 7 × 7 × 20 = **3,080 cm³**
13. Volume = 2 × 1 × 0.5 = 1 m³ = **1,000 litres**
14. Inner rectangle = (30 − 2) × (20 − 2) = 28 × 18 = 504 m². Garden = 600 m². Path = 600 − 504 = **96 m²**
15. 12 litres = 12,000 ml → 12,000 ÷ 500 = **24 bottles**
