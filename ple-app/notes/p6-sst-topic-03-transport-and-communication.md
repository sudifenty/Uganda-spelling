*Curriculum reference: NCDC Primary Six Curriculum, Set One, Social Studies Topic 3,*

**What you already know:** You know how people in your village send messages and move from
place to place. In P.6 you study **transport and communication in the whole of East
Africa** — the different types of transport, the means of communication, and the part
both play in trade, farming, tourism and development.

**By the end of this topic you should be able to:**

- define transport and communication
- name the main types of transport and give their advantages and disadvantages
- name important railway lines, ports, airports, lakes and water bodies
- describe traditional and modern means of communication
- describe the problems facing transport and communication and how they can be improved
- explain how transport and communication promote trade and development

## 1. What is transport?

Transport is the movement of people and goods from one place to another.

Transport helps people and goods to move from one place to another.

- People
- Goods

- Road transport
- Railway transport
- Water transport
- Air transport
- Pipeline transport


---

## 2. What is road transport?

Road transport is the movement of people and goods from one place to another using roads.

- Buses
- Cars
- Lorries
- Taxis
- Motorcycles
- Bicycles

- Provides door-to-door services.
- Can reach many places.
- Is suitable for short distances.
- Can carry people and goods.
- Is easily available in many areas.

- Can be affected by traffic jams.
- Can cause road accidents.
- Can be expensive for long distances.
- Can be affected by poor roads.
- Causes air pollution through vehicle smoke.

- Poor roads.
- Traffic congestion.
- Road accidents.
- High cost of road construction and maintenance.
- Vehicle breakdowns.


---

## 3. What is railway transport?

Railway transport is the movement of people and goods using trains on railway lines.

- Carries many people.
- Carries large amounts of goods.
- Is suitable for long distances.
- Can reduce pressure on roads.

- Is expensive to construct.
- Cannot reach places without railway lines.
- May be slow compared with air transport.
- Requires regular maintenance.

The Kenya-Uganda railway is an important railway connection between Kenya and Uganda.

It helps to transport goods between Uganda and the Kenyan coast.


---

## 4. What is water transport?

Water transport is the movement of people and goods by using water bodies.

- Lakes
- Rivers
- Seas
- Oceans

- Boats
- Canoes
- Ferries
- Ships
- Steamers

- Can carry large amounts of goods.
- Is useful for long distances.
- Is suitable for bulky goods.
- Can be cheaper than some other forms of transport.

- Is slower than air transport.
- Can be affected by storms and bad weather.
- Cannot be used in places without navigable water.
- Accidents can lead to loss of lives and property.

- Lake Victoria
- Lake Tanganyika
- Lake Albert
- Lake Kyoga
- Indian Ocean


---

## 5. What is a port?

A port is a place on the coast or a water body where ships and boats load and unload people and goods.

The Port of Mombasa in Kenya is an important seaport serving East African countries.

It provides Uganda, a landlocked country, with an important route for importing and exporting goods through the Indian Ocean.


---

## 6. What is air transport?

Air transport is the movement of people and goods by aircraft.

- Aeroplanes
- Helicopters

- Is the fastest means of transport.
- Is suitable for long distances.
- Can reach distant places quickly.
- Is useful for transporting urgent goods.
- Is useful for international travel.

- Is expensive.
- Carries less bulky goods than ships and some trains.
- Can be affected by bad weather.
- Requires airports and special equipment.

An airport is a place where aircraft take off and land.

Entebbe International Airport is an important international airport in Uganda.


---

## 7. What is pipeline transport?

Pipeline transport is the movement of liquids or gases through pipes from one place to another.

- Petroleum products
- Water
- Natural gas

It can transport liquids continuously over long distances.

It is expensive to construct and maintain.


---

## 8. What is communication?

Communication is the sending and receiving of information from one person to another.

Communication helps people to share information, ideas and messages.

- Telephone
- Radio
- Television
- Newspapers
- Letters
- Internet
- Postal services
- Face-to-face communication


---

## 9. What is telephone communication?

Telephone communication is the sending and receiving of information using telephones or mobile phones.

- Is fast.
- Allows people to communicate over long distances.
- Can be used to send messages.
- Allows people to speak directly to one another.


---

## 10. What is radio communication?

Radio communication is the sending of information through radio waves.

- Reaches many people.
- Provides news and information.
- Provides education.
- Provides entertainment.
- Can be used in many rural areas.


---

## 11. What is television communication?

Television communication is the sharing of information through television programmes.

- Provides news.
- Provides education.
- Provides entertainment.
- Allows people to see and hear information.


---

## 12. What is a newspaper?

A newspaper is a printed publication containing news and other information.

- News
- Sports
- Business information
- Government information
- Advertisements


---

## 13. What is the Internet?

The Internet is a worldwide system that allows people to share and access information using connected computers and other devices.

- Communication
- Education
- Business
- Research
- Entertainment
- Sharing information


---

## 14. What are traditional means of communication?

Traditional means of communication are ways of passing information that were commonly used before modern communication methods became common.

- Drums
- Horns
- Messengers
- Smoke signals
- Whistling
- Storytelling

Drums were used to send messages to people over distances, especially in communities where people understood the different drum sounds.

Messengers carried information from one person or place to another.


---

## 15. What is modern communication?

Modern communication is the use of modern methods and technology to send and receive information.

- Mobile phones
- Radio
- Television
- Internet
- Email
- Social media

- Is fast.
- Allows information to reach many people.
- Makes communication over long distances easier.
- Helps people to share information quickly.
- Supports business and education.


---

## 16. Why are transport and communication important?

Transport and communication are important because they help people, goods and information to move from one place to another.

Transport helps traders to move goods from places where they are produced to places where they are needed.

Communication helps traders to obtain information about goods, prices, customers and markets.

They help people to move, trade, share information and carry out different economic activities.

Transport helps farmers to take farm products to markets and bring farm inputs to their farms.

Communication helps farmers to receive information about weather, markets, prices and farming methods.

Transport enables tourists to travel to tourist attractions.

Communication allows tourists to obtain information about tourist attractions, accommodation and travel services.


---

## 17. What problems affect transport and communication?

- Poor roads.
- Traffic congestion.
- Road accidents.
- High transport costs.
- Poor railway networks.
- Bad weather.
- Lack of enough transport facilities.

- Poor network coverage.
- High cost of communication.
- Lack of electricity in some areas.
- Lack of communication equipment.
- Poor Internet services in some areas.

Poor transport makes it difficult and expensive to move people and goods.

Poor communication makes it difficult for people to share information quickly.


---

## 18. How can transport and communication be improved?

- Constructing and maintaining good roads.
- Improving railway networks.
- Improving water transport.
- Improving airports.
- Improving road safety.

- Expanding telephone networks.
- Improving Internet services.
- Increasing access to electricity.
- Building more communication facilities.
- Making communication services more affordable.

## IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- The main types of transport are **road, railway, water, air and pipeline transport**.
- The main means of communication are **telephone, radio, television, newspapers, letters, postal services and the Internet**.
- The fastest means of transport is **air transport**.
- **Railway and water transport** can carry large and bulky goods.
- The Port of Mombasa provides an important route for **Uganda's imports and exports** through the Indian Ocean.
- Transport and communication help people, goods and information to move from one place to another and **promote trade and development**.
