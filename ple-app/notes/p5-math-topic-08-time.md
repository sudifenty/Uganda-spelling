## 1. UNITS OF TIME

| Change | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 minute | = **60 seconds** |
| 1 hour | = **60 minutes** |
| 1 day | = **24 hours** |
| 1 week | = 7 days |
| 1 year | = 12 months = 365 days (366 in a leap year) |

```
Hours → minutes:   × 60          Minutes → hours:   ÷ 60
Minutes → seconds: × 60          Seconds → minutes: ÷ 60
```

**Examples**

```
3 hours = 3 × 60 = 180 minutes
2 1/2 hours = 2.5 × 60 = 150 minutes
240 minutes = 240 ÷ 60 = 4 hours
5 minutes = 5 × 60 = 300 seconds
```

---

## 2. TELLING TIME ON THE 12-HOUR CLOCK

The **long hand** shows the minutes. The **short hand** shows the hours.

- For the first **half hour** we say **"past"**.
- For the second half hour we say **"to"** the next hour.

| Clock | We say |
|---|---|
| 7:00 | Seven o'clock |
| 7:15 | A quarter past seven |
| 7:30 | Half past seven |
| 7:45 | A quarter to eight |
| 7:20 | Twenty minutes past seven |
| 7:40 | Twenty minutes to eight |
| 8:50 | Ten minutes to nine |

**How to change a "past" time into a "to" time:** subtract the minutes from 60 and move to
the next hour.

```
9:35  →  60 − 35 = 25  →  twenty-five minutes to ten
```

## 3. A.M. AND P.M.

| a.m. | p.m. |
|---|---|
| From **midnight (12:00 a.m.)** to just before midday | From **midday (12:00 noon)** to just before midnight |
| Morning times | Afternoon, evening and night times |

```
6:30 a.m.  →  early morning
12:00 noon →  midday
3:15 p.m.  →  afternoon
9:00 p.m.  →  night
```

## 4. DURATION

**Duration** is the length of time something takes.

```
Duration = ending time − starting time
```

**Example 1.** A lesson starts at 8:30 a.m. and ends at 11:15 a.m.

```
   11 h 15 min
 −  8 h 30 min

15 minutes cannot take away 30 minutes, so borrow 1 hour = 60 minutes:
   10 h 75 min
 −  8 h 30 min
   ------------
    2 h 45 min

Duration = 2 hours 45 minutes
```

**Example 2.** A journey starts at 10:40 a.m. and takes 3 hours 35 minutes. When does it
end?

```
  10 h 40 min
+  3 h 35 min
  ------------
  13 h 75 min  →  75 min = 1 h 15 min  →  14 h 15 min
14:15 is 2:15 p.m.
```

**Example 3 — crossing midday.** From 10:20 a.m. to 1:05 p.m.

```
10:20 a.m. to 12:00 noon = 1 hour 40 minutes
12:00 noon to 1:05 p.m.  = 1 hour 5 minutes
Total = 2 hours 45 minutes
```

## 5. TIME, DISTANCE AND SPEED

**Speed** tells how fast something moves — the distance covered in one hour (or in one
second).

```
Speed    = Distance ÷ Time
Distance = Speed × Time
Time     = Distance ÷ Speed
```

**Example 1.** A bus covers 120 km in 2 hours. Find its speed.

```
Speed = 120 ÷ 2 = 60 km/hr
```

**Example 2.** A car travels at 80 km/hr for 3 hours. How far does it go?

```
Distance = 80 × 3 = 240 km
```

**Example 3.** How long does a lorry take to cover 150 km at 50 km/hr?

```
Time = 150 ÷ 50 = 3 hours
```

**Example 4.** A cyclist rides 24 km in 1 1/2 hours. Find the speed.

```
Speed = 24 ÷ 1.5 = 16 km/hr
Check: 16 × 1.5 = 24 km  ✓
```

**Example 5.** A taxi left Kampala at 7:30 a.m. and reached a town 180 km away at 10:30 a.m.
Find its speed.

```
Time taken = 10:30 − 7:30 = 3 hours
Speed = 180 ÷ 3 = 60 km/hr
```

# KEY DEFINITIONS

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Second | The smallest common unit of time |
| Minute | 60 seconds |
| Hour | 60 minutes |
| a.m. | The time from midnight to just before midday |
| p.m. | The time from midday to just before midnight |
| Duration | The length of time between a starting and an ending time |
| Speed | The distance covered in one unit of time |
| Distance | How far something travels |
| km/hr | Kilometres travelled in one hour |

---

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- 60 seconds = 1 minute · 60 minutes = 1 hour · 24 hours = 1 day.
- **Hours to minutes: multiply by 60. Minutes to hours: divide by 60.**
- Past the hour for the first 30 minutes; **to** the next hour after that.
- **Speed = Distance ÷ Time.**
- **Distance = Speed × Time.** **Time = Distance ÷ Speed.**
- When subtracting time, borrow **60 minutes**, not 100.
- 12:00 noon is written 12:00 p.m. and 12:00 midnight is 12:00 a.m.

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- 3 hours = **180 minutes**.
- 7:45 is read as **a quarter to eight**.
- 9:35 is **twenty-five minutes to ten**.
- 8:30 a.m. to 11:15 a.m. = **2 hours 45 minutes**.
- 120 km in 2 hours = **60 km/hr**.
- 80 km/hr for 3 hours = **240 km**.
- 150 km at 50 km/hr = **3 hours**.

---

# COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Borrowing 100 instead of 60 when subtracting time.
- Writing 2.45 hours when the answer is 2 hours 45 minutes.
- Mixing up a.m. and p.m. in a duration question.
- Multiplying when the question asks for time (you must divide).
- Forgetting the unit — km, hours or km/hr.
