## 1. UNITS AND CONVERSIONS

| Length | Mass | Capacity |
|---|---|---|
| 1 cm = 10 mm | 1 g = 1,000 mg | 1 litre = 1,000 ml |
| 1 m = 100 cm | 1 kg = 1,000 g | **1 litre = 1,000 cm³** |
| 1 km = 1,000 m | 1 tonne = 1,000 kg | 1 m³ = 1,000 litres |

**To a smaller unit → multiply. To a bigger unit → divide.**

*Examples:* 2.5 km = 2,500 m · 450 cm = 4.5 m · 3,250 g = 3.25 kg · 1.75 litres = 1,750 ml

## 2. PERIMETER AND CIRCUMFERENCE

**Perimeter:** The distance all round a shape. **Circumference:** The perimeter of a circle.

| Shape | Formula |
|---|---|
| Square | 4 × side |
| Rectangle | 2 × (length + width) |
| Triangle | Add the three sides |
| **Circle** | **C = πd** or **C = 2πr** (π = 22/7) |

### Parts of a circle

**Radius (r)** — from the centre to the edge · **Diameter (d)** — right across through the
centre, **d = 2r** · **Circumference** — the distance round · **Chord, arc and sector** are
parts of the circle you should be able to name.

### Worked examples

1. *A rectangle is 14 m by 9 m. Find the perimeter.*
   2 × (14 + 9) = **46 m**
2. *A circle has a radius of 21 cm. Find its circumference.*
   2 × 22/7 × 21 = **132 cm**
3. *A circle has a diameter of 28 cm. Find its circumference.*
   22/7 × 28 = **88 cm**
4. *The circumference of a circle is 66 cm. Find its radius.*
   2 × 22/7 × r = 66 → 44r/7 = 66 → r = 66 × 7 ÷ 44 = **10.5 cm**

## 3. AREA

**Area:** The surface covered by a shape, measured in **square units**.

| Shape | Formula |
|---|---|
| Square | side × side |
| Rectangle | length × width |
| Triangle | ½ × base × height |
| Parallelogram | base × height |
| Trapezium | ½ × (a + b) × height |
| **Circle** | **A = πr²** |

### Worked examples

1. *Find the area of a rectangle 15 m by 8 m.* → **120 m²**
2. *Find the area of a triangle with base 14 cm and height 9 cm.* → ½ × 14 × 9 = **63 cm²**
3. *Find the area of a circle of radius 7 cm.* → 22/7 × 7 × 7 = **154 cm²**
4. *Find the area of a circle of diameter 14 cm.* → r = 7 cm → **154 cm²**
5. *A rectangular plot 30 m by 20 m has a path 2 m wide all round it outside.
   Find the area of the path.*
   Outer = (30 + 4) × (20 + 4) = 34 × 24 = 816 m²; plot = 600 m²; **path = 216 m²**

---

## 4. VOLUME AND CAPACITY

**Volume:** The space a solid occupies, in **cubic units**. **Capacity:** How much a container can hold.

| Solid | Formula |
|---|---|
| Cube | side × side × side |
| Cuboid | length × width × height |
| **Cylinder** | **πr²h** |
| Any prism | area of cross-section × length |

**The link:** **1 litre = 1,000 cm³** and **1 m³ = 1,000 litres**.

### Worked examples

1. *Find the volume of a cuboid 12 cm by 8 cm by 5 cm.* → **480 cm³**
2. *Find the volume of a cube of side 6 cm.* → 6 × 6 × 6 = **216 cm³**
3. *Find the volume of a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 15 cm.*
   22/7 × 7 × 7 × 15 = **2,310 cm³**
4. *Find the capacity in litres of a tank 80 cm by 50 cm by 40 cm.*
   Volume = 160,000 cm³ → 160,000 ÷ 1,000 = **160 litres**
5. *A tank of 2 m³ is emptied using 20-litre jerrycans. How many full jerrycans?*
   2 m³ = 2,000 litres → 2,000 ÷ 20 = **100 jerrycans**

# KEY DEFINITIONS

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Perimeter | The distance all round a shape |
| Circumference | The perimeter of a circle |
| Radius | The distance from the centre of a circle to the edge |
| Diameter | The distance right across a circle through the centre |
| Pi (π) | The number 22/7, used with circles |
| Area | The surface covered by a shape, in square units |
| Volume | The space a solid occupies, in cubic units |
| Capacity | The amount a container can hold |
| Mass | The amount of matter in an object |

# IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- 1 m = 100 cm · 1 km = 1,000 m · 1 kg = 1,000 g · 1 tonne = 1,000 kg
- **1 litre = 1,000 cm³** and **1 m³ = 1,000 litres**
- **C = 2πr = πd** and **A = πr²**, with π = **22/7**
- **d = 2r**, so halve the diameter before using πr².
- Area of a triangle = **½ × base × height**.
- Volume of a cylinder = **πr²h**.
- Area is in **square** units; volume is in **cubic** units — always write the unit.

---

# EXAMINATION POINTS

- Radius 21 cm → circumference 132 cm.
- Diameter 28 cm → circumference 88 cm.
- Radius 7 cm → area 154 cm².
- Cylinder r = 7 cm, h = 15 cm → 2,310 cm³.
- Tank 80 × 50 × 40 cm → 160 litres.
- Area of a path = outer area − inner area.

---

# COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Using the **diameter** in πr² instead of the radius.
- Forgetting the ½ in the area of a triangle.
- Writing cm instead of cm² for area, or cm² instead of cm³ for volume.
- Dividing by 100 instead of **1,000** when changing cm³ to litres.
- Using π = 22/7 with a radius that is not a multiple of 7 and then rounding wrongly — leave the answer as a fraction if it does not divide exactly.
