**What you already know:** Travellers need a place to stay, eat and rest. In P.6
you learn the **vocabulary of the hotel** — what a hotel is, who works there, what
services it offers, the types of hotels and the rules guests follow.

**By the end of this topic you should be able to:**

- say what a hotel, a guest and accommodation are
- name the hotel workers and the work of each
- state the services hotels offer, the types of hotels and their importance
- give the rules hotel guests should follow

## MEANING OF A HOTEL

A **hotel** is a building or establishment where people pay to stay, eat and
receive other services while away from home.

A **hotel guest** is a person who stays in a hotel.

## WHY PEOPLE STAY IN HOTELS

People stay in hotels to get accommodation, food, drinks and other services when
they are away from home.

**Accommodation** is a place where people stay or live temporarily.

## SERVICES OFFERED BY HOTELS

- Accommodation
- Food and drinks
- Laundry services
- Room cleaning
- Internet services

## PEOPLE WHO WORK IN A HOTEL

- **Hotel manager** – supervises the activities of the hotel.
- **Receptionist** – welcomes guests, records their details and helps them with accommodation.
- **Chef** – prepares food for hotel guests.
- **Waiter or waitress** – serves food and drinks to guests.
- **Housekeeper** – keeps hotel rooms and other areas clean and tidy.
- **Security guard** – protects the hotel, workers and guests from danger.
- **Gardener** – maintains flowers, lawns, trees and other plants around the hotel.

## TYPES OF HOTELS

- Budget hotels
- Luxury hotels
- Resort hotels
- Business hotels

## IMPORTANCE OF HOTELS

Hotels are important because they:

- Provide accommodation, food and other services to travellers.
- Provide employment — they employ managers, receptionists, chefs, waiters, housekeepers and security guards.
- Promote tourism — they provide accommodation and other services to tourists, making it easier for them to visit and stay in different places.
- Contribute to the development of a country — they create employment, generate income, pay taxes and support other businesses.

## RULES FOR HOTEL GUESTS

Hotel guests should:

- Keep hotel rooms clean.
- Respect hotel workers and other guests.
- Avoid damaging hotel property.
- Pay for services received.
- Follow hotel rules.

Guests who damage hotel property may be required to pay for the damaged property
and may face other action according to hotel rules.

## IMPORTANT FACTS TO REMEMBER

- A hotel is where people **pay** to stay, eat and receive services **while away from home**.
- Hotels offer **accommodation, food and drinks, laundry, room cleaning and internet services**.
- The **manager** supervises; the **receptionist** welcomes guests; the **chef** prepares food; the **waiter/waitress** serves it; the **housekeeper** cleans; the **guard** protects; the **gardener** cares for the plants.
- The four types of hotels are **budget, luxury, resort and business** hotels.
- Hotels **provide employment, promote tourism, generate income and pay taxes**.
- Guests must **keep rooms clean, respect people and property, pay for services and follow the hotel's rules**; damaging property means paying for it and other action.
